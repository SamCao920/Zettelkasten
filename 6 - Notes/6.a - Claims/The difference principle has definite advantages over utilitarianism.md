## Summary
There are a few reasons why the difference principle is preferred by Rawls over utilitarian principles:
- Utilitarian principles require that we make comparisons of cardinal values (utilities) across individuals, which is difficult to do.
- Utilitarian principles compare subjective “utilities,” while the difference principle compares more objective and observable primary goods.
## Significance
This explains why Rawls uses the difference principle over something like utilitarianism.
