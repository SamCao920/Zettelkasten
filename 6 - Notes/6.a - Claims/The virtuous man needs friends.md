## Summary
The virtuous man possesses all that is good, and since it is better to have friends than not to have friends, the virtuous man needs friends.
## Significance
Makes an argument for why the virtuous person needs friends, since, as some say, the virtuous person is already doing well by themselves.
## Explanation
- Virtuousness is an activity, and it is easier to be virtuous when you keep up the continuous activity with friends.
- Because virtuous friends is like our second self, when we see others do good things and live well, we, in a meaningful way, see ourselves being the same in our friends.
