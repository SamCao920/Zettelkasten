## Summary
Since the states on the borders were able to actually be turned into client states during the Julio-Claudian era, and the territories that the clients protected where the “un-Romanized” parts of the empire,  it didn’t matter much when the clients failed, allowing for this system to function.
## Significance
This explained success of the empire at defending its border during the Julio-Claudian era, and how even while defending vast borders, they were able to amount concentrated offensives against enemies.
## Explanation
Since the tribes that occupied the front were largely unorganized and able to be brought in as client states, this system was viable. Additionally, this represented a positive feedback loop whereby more client states meant stronger border defense/greater offensive ability, which meant the ability to accumulate even more client states.
