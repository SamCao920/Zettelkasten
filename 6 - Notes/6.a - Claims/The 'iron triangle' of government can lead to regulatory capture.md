## Summary
The “iron triangle” of government, consisting of congress, the bureaucracy, and the lobbyists, may often act to benefit each other (as the three exist in a symbiotic relationship) rather than for the good of the public.
## Significance
The “iron triangle” is, as apparent in its name, difficult to break. This makes it a tough topic to address, to enact reforms to ensure that the government actually acts in the interest of the public.
