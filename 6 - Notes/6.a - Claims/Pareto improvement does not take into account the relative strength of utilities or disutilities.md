## Summary
The idea of a pareto improvement is that it leads to an utility gain while leading to no disutility as a result, at all. An action is not considered a pareto improvement even if it raises utility by $100 \ utils$, if the action lowers utility by even $1 \ utils$.
## Significance
I think it is a little strange for the concept of pareto improvement to be defined this way, considering many other concepts in economics have a focus on maximizing overall welfare.
