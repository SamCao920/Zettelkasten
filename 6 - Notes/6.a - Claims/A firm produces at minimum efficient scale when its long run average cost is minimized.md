## Summary
A firm produces at minimum efficient scale (MES) when its long run average cost is at its minimum, meaning that if it produced less, it would not be utilizing its economy of scale effectively, and any more might lead to diseconomies of scale. This is also where the average cost is equal to the marginal cost.
## Significance
Firms always want to be producing at their minimum efficient scale, since it is the most economically efficient for them.
## Explanation
![[Pasted image 20250930222819.png|Figure 1. Graphical depiction of the location of the minimum efficient scale]]
The minimum efficient scale is found where the marginal cost ($MC$) is equal to the average cost ($AC$). When the average cost is above the marginal cost, the firm has not reached the full potential of its economy of scale.
