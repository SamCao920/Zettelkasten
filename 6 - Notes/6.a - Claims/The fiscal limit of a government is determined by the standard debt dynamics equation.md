## Summary
The fiscal limit can be found using the standard debt dynamics equation ($\Delta d \approx (r - g)d - s$), which can be rearranged to produce $Fiscal \ Limit = \frac{s_{max}}{r-g}$.
## Significance
This can be used to calculate the amount of acceptable debt that a government can take on.
## Derivation
The most accurate version of this equation is actually$$
\Delta d_{t} = (\frac{1+r}{1+g})d_{t-1}-s
$$
This comes from the the debt and and GDP equations, i.e., the following:
$$
d_{t}=(1+r)d_{t-1}-s
$$
$$
y_{t}=(1+g)_{t-1}
$$
To get the change in the debt-to-GDP ratio in the following year then, we have to find $\frac{d_{t}}{y_{t}}$ , which produces $$
\Delta d_{t} = (\frac{1+r}{1+g})d_{t-1}-s
$$
