## Summary
Concord is when people have the same opinion about what is to their interest, choose the same actions, and carry out what they decided in common.
## Significance
This is important for Aristotle’s discussions on politics.
## Explanation
- Concord specifically regards an alignment in belief of practical matters that shape life.
	- It regards shared purpose, not just shared opinion.
- It is inherently political, concerning common advantage in the state.
- It is only able to be reached by virtuous people, not vicious ones.
