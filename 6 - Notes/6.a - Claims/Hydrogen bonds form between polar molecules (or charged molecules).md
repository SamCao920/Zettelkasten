## Summary
Hydrogen bonds are only able to form between polar molecules (or between a polar and a charged molecule), as their (partial) charges attract each other.

## Significance
Because they are formed between polar molecules (or between a polar and a charged molecule), they are relatively weak, easily disrupted by many factors.
