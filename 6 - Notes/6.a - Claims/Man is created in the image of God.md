## Summary
The theistic idea that humans were created in the image of God.
## Significance
This has many ethical and theological implications, including for morality.
## Bible
- Adam goes about and names the animals, something that God ought to do. This symbolizes how humans have dominion over the Earth.
- Humans have a special moral status, since they are in the image of God, and thus murder is prohibited.
