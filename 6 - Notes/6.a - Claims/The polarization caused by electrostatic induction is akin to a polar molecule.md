## Summary
The polarization that is caused by electrostatic induction is very much similar to a polar molecule described in chemistry.

## Significance
Since both objects polarized by electrostatic induction and polar objects in chemistry are *polar,* they share similar traits. For example, they are both overall neutral, but the polarized regions may experience a electromagnetic force pulling it towards the opposite charge.
