## Summary
Political attitude is one’s opinions on the degree of political change, how much of it should occur, and how fast it should occur. It can determine the direction towards which a society advances.
## Significance
This is significant for understanding under political system, since understanding political attitude allows for the understanding of why a political shift is occurring when it is occurring.
