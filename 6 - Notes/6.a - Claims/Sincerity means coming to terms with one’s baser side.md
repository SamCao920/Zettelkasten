## Summary
The idea that being sincere to oneself requires coming to terms with one’s baser side, accepting that it exists but not letting it overtake you.
## Significance
This is an important way to ensure that one is able to accept oneself and sees oneself clearly and fairly.
## Explanation
This is what father Zossima argues that one ought to do in *The Brothers Karamazov.* This is the thing that Dimitri attempts to do all throughout the book.
One when holds secrets about the dark thoughts that go through their head, it isolates the person from the rest of society.
