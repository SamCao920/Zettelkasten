## Summary
People can motivate their sinful actions by the fact that they want to be loved.
## Significance
This is apparent through the actions of many people throughout different novels.
## East of Eden
Charles Trask offers this example when he beats up Adam because Cyrus loves Adam more than Charles.
Caleb Track offers an example of this in the next generation, since he engages in the bean business, making a profit at the cost of the nation (price gouging), in order to please Adam.
- After this, he takes Aron to see Kate, which shatters Aron worldview.
