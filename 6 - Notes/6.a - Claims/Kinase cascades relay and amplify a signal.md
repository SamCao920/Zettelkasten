## Summary
Kinase cascades, whereby an original kinase phosphorylates the next kinase, which then phosphorylates the next kinase, is able to relay and amplify a signal across a large distance.
## Significance
This is how signals are transferred within a cell, as they are amplified through this process, which eventually leads to some cell response.
