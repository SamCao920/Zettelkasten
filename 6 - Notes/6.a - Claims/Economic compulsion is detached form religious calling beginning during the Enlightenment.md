## Summary
As history advances, humans have become more rationalistic, and thus, the religious motive is abandoned in the spirit of capitalism, and religious compulsion is no longer needed to justify economic acquisition.
## Significance
This is Weber’s final step in his argue for the development of the spirit of capitalism, as once the religious element fades away, all that is left is the modern conception of capitalism.
