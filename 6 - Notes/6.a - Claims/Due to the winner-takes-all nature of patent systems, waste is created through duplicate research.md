## Summary
Due to the winner-takes-all nature of patent systems, it leads to competitors attempting to create something that is similar, leading to much duplicate research being conducted.
## Significance
This is important as this flaw of the patent system is not often recognized.

## Caveat
At the same time, if there was no patent system, it seems that people would lack incentive to conduct research anyways.
