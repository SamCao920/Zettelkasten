## Summary
When two genes are farther apart on a chromosome, it is more likely that they are going to recombination with the paired sister chromatids.
## Significance
This explains why the recombination frequency increases as the distances between the genes increases.
## Explanation
Think of a chromosome like a long piece of rope. If you close your eyes and cut the rope at a random spot, you are much more likely to cut between two knots if they are at opposite ends of the rope than if they are right next to each other.
