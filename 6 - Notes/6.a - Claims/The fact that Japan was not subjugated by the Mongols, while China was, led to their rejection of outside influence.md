## Summary
The fact that the Japanese were not conquered by the Mongols following two invasion attempts ended the prevalence of outside influence in Japan, as they began to believe that they were superior.
## Significance
This explains in part the origins of Japanese isolationism, and their ideology of superiority that would play a part in WWII.
