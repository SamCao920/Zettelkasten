## Summary
A proton and an electron carry the same magnitude of charge, except in opposite directions. This magnitude of charge is $e$, or an elementary charge, which is roughly $1.602 \times 10^{-19} C$.
## Significance
This is important because despite their mass differences, a proton and an electron carry the same amount of charge.
