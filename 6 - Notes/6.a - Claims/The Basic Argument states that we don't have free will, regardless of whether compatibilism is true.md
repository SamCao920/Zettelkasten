## Summary
The Basic Argument by Galen Strawson attempts to prove impossibility of the existence of free will.
## Significance
If this argument succeeds, the debate over compatibilism is rendered null, and free will is determined to be impossible.
## Argument
The Basic Argument 
- You do what you do because of the way you are.
- To be truly responsible for what you do, you must be responsible for the way you are.
- You are the way you are because of circumstances outside your control.
- Therefore, you are not responsible for your nature, and thus not responsible for your actions.
