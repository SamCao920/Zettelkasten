## Summary
Culture is not determinant of the political institutions that can exist within a society, though it is a factor that can influence political development.
## Significance
This is a critique levied at Samuel Huntington’s idea of Cultural Determinism, that culture is that will underly all political differences between states after the end of the Cold War.
## Examples
- The same culture that lead to a progressive parliamentarian democracy in the Netherlands led to Apartheid in South Africa.
- Korea, due to its Asian culture, was thought to be despotic in nature.
	- This idea that Asian states are naturally despotic have even been used by Asian dictators to legitimize their rule.
- Turkey, due to its Islamic culture, ought to resemble the other Middle Eastern countries, rather than adopting Western legal and political institutions.
	- I think that this is debatable through, since Turkey has long had a much greater exposure to the West and it’s institutions.
