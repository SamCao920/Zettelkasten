## Summary
A natural monopoly arises when the fixed costs are high in an industry, but the variable/marginal costs are low. This means that once production, which may be initially expensive, starts, the firm can quickly achieve an economy of scale.
## Significance
Natural monopolies are often a key justification for the existence of some monopolies.
