## Summary
While just implementing a higher wealth tax may lead to substantial tax flight, by implementing out-migration taxes, you are able to regain much of the lost tax revenue, since people are much less incentivized to leave the country when there is a out-migration tax.
## Significance
This is a strategy that states can use to ensure that they are able to maximize tax revenue even when they raise taxes on the rich.
