## Summary
The reason that many socialist regimes survived into the 1980s and 90s was because of their interactions with the neoliberal international order, which meant that a socialist state essentially acted as a corporation that was trading with other states.
## Significance
This explains the survival of socialist states for decades beyond which the Austrian Economists predicted they would survive.
