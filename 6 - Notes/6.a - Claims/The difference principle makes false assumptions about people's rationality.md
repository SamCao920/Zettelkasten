## Summary
The difference principle lies at the heart of Rawls’ justice as fairness. However, it seems that there are some incorrect assumptions made by Rawls in outlining this principle.
## Significance
This refutes a core component of Rawls’ justice as fairness.
## Explanation
It seems that, behind the veil of ignorance, individuals would not choose the difference principle. Rather, with probabilistic reasoning, they would choose a basic structure which maximizes the average benefit.
