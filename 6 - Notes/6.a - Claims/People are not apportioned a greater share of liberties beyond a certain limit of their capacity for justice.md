## Summary
This is the Range Property Argument. People are not apportioned a greater share of justice based on their capacity for justice beyond a certain limit.
## Significance
Explains the way that the principles of justice are to be applied.
## Explanation
Once someone meets a certain limit in their capacity for justice, they are no longer awarded further considerations of justice corresponding to above that limit.
Of course, it is difficult to pin down the exactly threshold above which one is no longer entitled to greater justice. 
## Note
An obvious objection is that it seems arbitrary that people who pass beyond a specific threshold should not receive more justice.
