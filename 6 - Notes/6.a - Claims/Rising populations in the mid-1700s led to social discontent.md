## Summary
Due to the increasing prevalent of the potato as a food, and the ease with which it can be grown, population boomed beginning during the mid-1700s, leading to social discontent.
## Significance
This social discontent coincided with Enlightenment ideas, and thus produced solutions and reform movements.
## Explanation
- This had many effects during the century:
	- Aristocrats could not find posts for all of their sons (leading to their discontent)
	- Peasants couldn’t leave their land to all their children (creating a class of landless peasants)
	- This surfeit of population would fuel the armies during the French Revolutionary Wars.
