## Summary
The membrane potential is the difference in voltage between the inside ($-60mV\to -90mV$)and the outside of a cell ($0mV$).
## Significance
This is crucial for understanding the movement of materials into and out of a cell, as well as being crucial to the function of many different cells, including neurons and muscles, which use this difference in voltage to send a signal or induce a contraction.
