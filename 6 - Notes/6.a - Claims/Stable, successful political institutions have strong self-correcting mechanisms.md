## Summary
All stable and successful institutions in history have had robust self-correcting institutions such that it is able to correct itself without the need for external stimuli.

## Homeostasis
A contrast can be drawn between good self-corrections and the idea of homeostasis in biology. An instruction can be analogized to be a single person, where our internal system is able to self-correct only with the need for external stimuli. On the other hand, good institutions are ones that are able to self-correct without the need for external stimuli, mostly because such external stimuli is almost always negative and harmful for the overall stability of the institution.
