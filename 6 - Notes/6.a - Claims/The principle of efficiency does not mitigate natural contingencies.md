## Summary
The principle of efficiency is not able to mitigate natural contingencies, which means that interpretations of the second principle of justice must appeal to some other conception of “everyone’s advantage.”
## Significance
The principle of efficiency is not able to account for things such as natural-born intelligence or creativity, which Rawls deems unacceptable. He thus prefers the difference principle.
