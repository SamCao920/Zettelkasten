## Summary
When a ribosome is creating a protein from an mRNA sequence, it first create a “signal sequence,” which tells the ribosome to move to a translocator on the rough ER, and then continue creating the protein. Once the protein is created, it is transported to the Golgi, where it is then repacked based on its signal sequence and sent to its final destination.
## Significance
This is significant because many of our cells’ most important proteins need to be placed in a specific location, and without signal sequences, they would not be able to be transported to the right location.
