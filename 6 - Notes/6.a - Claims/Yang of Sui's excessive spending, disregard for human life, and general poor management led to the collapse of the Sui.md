## Summary
Emperor' Yang’s poor management, including his reckless invasions of Goguryeo and Champa, as well as his efforts to complete the Grand Canal and Great Wall led to his downfall and the end of the Sui.
## Significance
This shows how overspending and poor management can lead to the downfall of an empire, even when before, the country is stable and prosperous.
