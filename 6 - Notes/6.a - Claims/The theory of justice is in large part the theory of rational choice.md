## Summary
Justice is largely determined through the rational choice of individuals (from the perspective of contractarian frameworks), and thus, the study of justice is a study of rational choice fundamentally. 
## Significance
This is helpful for understanding the foundation for many schools of thought in the contractarian camp.
## Explanation
This is derived from the fact that a theory of justice has to be:
- Logical and rationally justified.
- Able to be agreed upon by agents.
And a theory arrived upon via rational choice fits both these criteria.
