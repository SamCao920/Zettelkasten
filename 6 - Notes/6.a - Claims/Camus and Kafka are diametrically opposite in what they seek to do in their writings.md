## Summary
While Camus writes about the tragedy of human existence in the absence of transcendence, Kafka reveals that the universe is full of things which we cannot understand, things which represents impossible transcendence.
## Significance
This explains a major contrast between two major writers of the 20th century.
## Note
It seems to me that while Kafka’s writings explicate the strangeness and of that things that we don’t typically realize are strange, and reveals to the reader worlds which are fantastical, Camus does the opposite. He shows the reader the strangeness and absurdity of the mundane things.
In this way, I believe that Camus is the greater writer, since he does not rely on constructed scenarios in order to present the absurdity of human existence.
