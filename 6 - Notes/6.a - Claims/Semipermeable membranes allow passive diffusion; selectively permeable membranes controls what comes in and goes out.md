## Summary
A semipermeable membrane is no the same thing as a selectively permeable membrane. While the former allows everything through if they fit a specific size or charge requirement, a selectively permeable membrane is able to regulate what enters and exists a membrane selecting for molecular identity.
## Significance
This is significant as it is important to understand the difference between semipermeable and selectively permeable, as the terms mean different things and apply in different contexts.
