## Summary
If a superintelligence is able to rewrite its own utility function such that it can reach its own bliss point, there would simply be no reason for such a being to endlessly consume resources and thus eliminates it as a threat to human survival.
## Significance
This presents a solution to the problem of AI superintelligence that is typically not considered.
## Explanation
It seems that human societies have already moved in a similar direction. Rather than endlessly increasing our consumption of physical resources, we’ve shifted more towards services.
Additionally, if AI is able to achieve a bliss state, it would mean that it has violated local nonsatiation. By designing AI in this manner, we will be able to mitigate the possibility that AI endlessly consumes resources that humans need.
## Note
It seems that if we design AIs such that they attempt to satisfy their goals doing as little as possible, their utility functions would be designed such that it is as easy as possible to satisfy. Therefore, it will ensure that AI does not compete with humanity for the same resources.
