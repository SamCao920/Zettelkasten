## Summary
Economic growth and productivity does not have a direction relationship, that is, there is economic growth doesn’t means that there has been an increase in productivity and vice versa.
## Significance
This is a common misconception: that economic growth is just driven by productivity growth.
## Explanation
Economic growth can occur without commensurate increases in productivity: an increase in available resources (labor/capital). 
At the same time, even if productivity may rise, a decrease in available resources may diminish the effect of productivity growth, or override it all together.
