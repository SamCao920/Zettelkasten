## Summary
Weber makes the claims that the entire history of monasticism, that is, the ascetic practice, can be frame as strings of attempts to secularize the influence of wealth and detach it from religion.
## Significance
Weber uses this to contrast the Protestant attitude: Protestants essentially began viewing wealth through the lens of religion, in a sense incorporating it into their belief system. This can be seen through their belief that making money is a sign of God’s blessing (when the money is not wasted).

## Quote
> [!quote] John Wesley
> I fear, wherever riches have increased, the essence of religion has decreased in the same proportion… For religion must necessarily produce both industry and frugality… But as riches increase, so will pride, anger, and love of the world…
