## Summary
A consumption tax can be made progressive if it is rebated in a progressive manner. Usually, consumption taxes are regressive, since low-income people have a higher MPC, meaning that they would proportionally be taxed more. This can be ameliorated with a progressive rebate system.
## Significance
This adds complexity to the idea that consumption taxes are totally regressive, since they could be made progressive with the right rebate system.
## Example
A revenue neutral carbon tax could be tax at, for example, 10%. With this in place, a low-income individual pays $100 in taxes, while a wealthier people might pay $1000. After taxes are levied, each individual receives $500. This makes the tax progressive.
