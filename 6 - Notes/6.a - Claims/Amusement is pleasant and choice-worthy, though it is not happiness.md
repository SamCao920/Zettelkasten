## Summary
Play, games, joking, or other forms of recreation or rest is considered to be amusement, which is choice-worthy and pleasant, but it is not the happiness that one seeks.
## Significance
This distinguishes amusements from happiness, which is an important distinction to make.
## Explanation
- Amusements are not an end; rather they are a sort of means to provide relieve from exertion for ourselves so that we may work.
