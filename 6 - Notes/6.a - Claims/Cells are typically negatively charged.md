## Summary
Due to the fact that many of the molecules in a cell are negatively charged, most prominently Potassium ions, cells are typically negatively on the whole. They are usually around $-60$ to $-70 mV$, known as its *resting potential*.
## Significance
This important for explaining the movement of molecules across the cell membrane, and the membrane potential.
