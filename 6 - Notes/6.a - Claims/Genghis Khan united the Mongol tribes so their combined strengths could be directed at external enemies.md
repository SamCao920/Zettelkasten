## Summary
Genghis Khan was able to take advantage of the fighting ability of the Mongols and pit them towards external enemies rather than focusing on internal squabbling (see Leaders often use external conflict to overcome internal division).

## Significance
This, combined with the raw firepower of the Mongol warriors, was what allowed them to take over so much of Eurasia.
