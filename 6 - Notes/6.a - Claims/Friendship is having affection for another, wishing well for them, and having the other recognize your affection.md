## Summary
Aristotle defines friendship as when two people have affection for each other, wish well for one another, and both mutually recognize their affection for one another. If these conditions are not met, it is not a friendship.
## Significance
Understanding the definition of friendship is crucial for further argumentation on the matter.
## Note
When applied to the three types of friendships:
- For utilitarian friendships, all three conditions are contingent on the fact that the other person is useful to you. 
- For pleasurable friendships, all three conditions are contingent on the fact that the other person is pleasant to you.
- In the case of a virtuous friendship, all three conditions are contingent on the fact that the other person has good character.
Additionally, friends of virtue don’t need justice between them (not to say that it does not exist, but simply that justice is not needed as an external check). Justice is only applicable between friends of utility.
