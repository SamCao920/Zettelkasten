## Summary
Someone who is temperate is someone who actively seeks pleasure, but they do not pain when there is no pleasure. 
They take pleasure in the right things (they desire the right things), in the right amount, at the right time, and in the right way. 
## Significance
This is a part of Aristotle’s analysis of what is means to be various virtues.
