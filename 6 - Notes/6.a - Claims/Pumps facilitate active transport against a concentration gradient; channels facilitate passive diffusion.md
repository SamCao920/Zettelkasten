## Summary
Pumps are able to, through manipulating their movements, actively move molecules in and out of a cell, typically against a concentration gradient. Channels, on the other hand, simply facilitate passive diffusion, which can occur due to thermal energy in the environment, a concentration gradient, or a charge difference.
## Significance
This is significant because the two different methods of moving stuff into and out of a cell are drastically different in terms of their purposes, method, and end goals.
