## Summary
Economics espionage has hugely adverse effect for firms, including leading to a large loss in revenue and decreased R&D spending.
## Significance
Since economic espionage is targeted specifically towards American firms, it means that there would be large, adverse effects for such firms.
## Explanation
After experiencing economic espionage, firms
- Experience a 40% loss in revenue within 5 years.
- Experience a 40% loss in R&D expenditures within 5 years.
This damage can entirely be left up to the result of the loss of secrets, since in cases where the espionage is thwarted, there is a 0% revenue drop.
Revenue drops are most apparent in below-median R&D industries, which means the loss of monopoly rent for the industry, and immediate technological obsolescence.
There are effect for the macro economy too.
- There was a 60% decline in US exported in targeted industries over ten years.
However, even when firms/industries experience economic espionage, firms do not change their strategy. They do not close up to outside innovation.
