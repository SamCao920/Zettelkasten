## Summary
The idea that moral virtue is not about having the right end goal, it is about how one chooses to attain that goal, the actions that they undertake.
## Significance
This ties into Aristotle’s larger philosophy regarding what is considered virtuous and how we attain virtue.
## Explanation
In this way, deliberation and choice are required in order for someone to be virtuous, since it is the job of deliberation to decide on the means, and it is choice which allows us to enact the means that we decide upon.
