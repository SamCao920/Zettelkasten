## Summary
A world that is dominated by China would be completely different from the current world which is dominated by the US. This is due to cultural and economic differences between the two powers.
## Significance
This note predicts how a world dominated by China would look, compared to a world dominated by the US.
## Explanation
- First, it is unlikely that a lack of demand from the US is going to cause a huge problem for China, since contrary to popular belief, China is not a export-based economy. ![[Pasted image 20260322084829.png]] [Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NE.EXP.GNFS.ZS?locations=CN-FR-DE-KR-US)
- China surpasses the US in state capacity, but the state is not always the bets at being able to use the resources that it has in the most efficient manner.
	- This means that while nationally, China might dominate, they won't necessarily live better than the citizens of the US have.
- China is more selfish than the US, and would not provide the same public goods that the US as provided, though they are perfectly capable of doing so if needed.
	- China's had a generally inward looking foreign policy in its history.
- Chinese culture won't be as influential as the US's was to the isolation of China was the rest of the the world digitally.
- Due to the weakness of IP protection and "other institutional factors," innovation in China will likely be a lot of incremental improvements rather than big technological change.
