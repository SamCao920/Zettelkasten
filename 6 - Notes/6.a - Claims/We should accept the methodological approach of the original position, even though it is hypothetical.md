## Summary
Even though the original position is a hypothetical decision scenario, it still makes sense for us to believe in its methodological power.
## Significance
Here, Rawls justifies the use of the original position.
## Explanation
Although it is hypothetical, it is made up of a collection of axioms which we would independently accept (this is by definition, since our own description of the original position utilizes our own axioms; in this way, the heavily epistemic work is done when Rawls attempts to convince us of the truth of his particular set of axioms).
- These principles outline the limits of what we would accept as fair terms of social cooperation.
