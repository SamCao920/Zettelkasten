## Summary
The US should reevaluate the foreign policy engagement that it made during the Cold War era to see if they are still valuable today, now that the borders of conflict have shifted to civilizational ones, and not ideological ones.
## Significance
This note is calling for a re-evaluation of US foreign policy.
## Explanation
Many of the foreign policy commitments made by the US during the Cold War were shaped by the ideological conflict during the Cold War, and thus do not reflect the new world order that is civilization-based.
Examples of such involvements include:
- American alliance with Japan.
- American alliance with Pakistan.
- The West's alignment with Turkey.
