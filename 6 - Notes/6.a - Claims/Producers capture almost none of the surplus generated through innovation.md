## Summary
Producers capture very little of the surplus that is generated through innovation.
## Significance
This shows the difficulty of innovation in a market capitalist system.
## Explanation
- Only about 2.2% of total social surplus is captured by innovators.
- The rest of the 98% actually goes to the consumer in the form of lower prices, better products, and higher standards of living.
- Markets typically overestimate Schumpeterian profits and underestimate the decay of such profit.
	- Technology has increased the decay speed, since information is difficult to create, but easy to re-create.
