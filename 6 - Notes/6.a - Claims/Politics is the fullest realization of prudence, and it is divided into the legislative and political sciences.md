## Summary
Politics is the virtue of prudence when applied to ruling a state.
## Significance
Politics is what Aristotle considers to be the most important of the activities that an individual ought to engage in.
## Legislative vs. Political
- **Legislative Science:** deals with the general outlines of running a state well, i.e., writing laws, etc.
- **Political Science:** deals with the particulars that arise when running a state, and thus engaging in the action (last step in the deliberative process).
