## Summary
When considering the case of innovation, being an incumbent in a market may actually be a disadvantage.
## Significance
This turns common antitrust logic on its head.
## Explanation
When there is a new technology being introduced that has the capability to replace an existing technology, having large sunk costs can actually be a barrier to change rather than a barrier to entry.
For instance, the new model of LLM search has the capability to replace web search. This means that if Google were to invest in LLM search, they would essentially be cannibalizing a crucial part of their current services in order to engage with this new innovation. On the other hand, established players in a field, by innovating, reduce the market share of the incumbent, so it benefits them to innovate.
