## Summary
In a fixed exchanged rate system, a country cannot hold a stock of money that exceeds its relative productivity, since if a country were to hold more than their relative productivity, the money would simply leave and move to other countries through increased imports.
## Significance
This explains why although nations may seek to use the expansion of credit as a means of increasing consumption, this does not work.
