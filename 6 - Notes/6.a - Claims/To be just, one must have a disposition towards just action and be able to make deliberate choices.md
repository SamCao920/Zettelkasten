## Summary
In order for one to be just, one has to continually practice adopting a just disposition, and one must be in control of their actions. The opposite is also true for injustice.
## Significance
This is Aristotle explaining the conditions that must be met in order for someone to be considered just.
## Explanation
It is important to note that just doing an unjust thing once does not make someone unjust. The unjust actions have to be both sustained and deliberate by the person in order for the person to be unjust, and vice versa.
