## Summary
The individualistic institutions that underlie much of the Baptist sects and the Quackers (exemplified by their rejection of large scale church institutions, which even the Calvinists adopted), allowed for a development of a sense of individualism, leading to the spirit of capitalism.
## Significance
This shows that part of the development of the spirit of capitalism could be traced to the Baptist sects and the Quackers. Also, this could serve as the explanation for why the spirit of capitalism is so intense in the US.
