## Summary
Christian morality inherently adopts an attitude which makes it obedient to authority.
## Significance
This represents a new perspective on the issue of Christian morality.
## Explanation
This is used by J.S. Mill in order to reply to the claim that Christian morality is entirely right and cannot be otherwise. This aspect of Christian morality which he identifies as an counterargument to that claim.
