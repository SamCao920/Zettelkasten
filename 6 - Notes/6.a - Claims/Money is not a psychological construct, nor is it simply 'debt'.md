## Summary
Yuval Noah Harari claims that money is a psychological construct that purely the result of our imagination, and David Graeber claims that money is simply “debt.” However, both these points of view are incorrect, since money is an actually economic good
## Significance
This is a rebuttal to the ideas that money is a psychological construct, or that it is simply “debt.”
## Explanation
Money is a commodity that naturally evolved from barter. It has intrinsic value as a unit of value, a store of value, and as a unit of exchange. Things become money because they serve these purposes well. 
- At some point in the past, money had to have value, or was tied to value. It is rather ridiculous for people, at some point, to simple assign value to something that is inherently worthless, since that is impractical, especially on a large scale. 
	- You cannot simply gather a group of people and say, “Let's pretend this piece of blue paper is worth one cow.” No one would trust it.
- It is simply impossible for there to even be debt before the existence of money, since debt must be denominated in money. 
	- Additionally, the state did not money in order to pay its soldiers, since if that was the case, there is no reason for the state to choose gold as its means of paying its soldiers. They must have done so since gold already had inherent value as money.
