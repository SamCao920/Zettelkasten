## Summary
Pleasure is not the sole thing that determines whether something is good or not. Rather, it can enhance/contribute to goodness.
## Significance
This means that we should still strive for pleasure, although it is not our sole aim in life.
## Explanation
- The Good should be something that is determinant and unchanging, while pleasure could be had as a result of different activities and indifferent degrees.
- Additionally, there are something that don’t necessarily bring pleasure that it would be good to have anyways, even if we have pleasure. This means that pleasure cannot be the sole thing that is good.
- Also, not every form of pleasure is desirable.
	- Bodily pleasure in the excess, for instance, is not good.
	- Also, Alien pleasure is not good, and are in fact analogous to pain, since they distract us from being able to experience the proper pleasure in a situation.
