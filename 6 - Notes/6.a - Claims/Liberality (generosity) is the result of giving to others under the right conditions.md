## Summary
Liberality, or generosity, mean giving the right amount, at the right time, at the right place, and most importantly, to the right person. This person also does not feel pain when they engage in the act of spending/giving money.
## Significance
This provides a definition for a virtue that Aristotle talks about.
## Note
Liberality is the virtue of wealth management, not just wealth disposal. It means governing well both the inflow and the outflow of wealth.
Like all other virtues, liberality depends of the disposition of the liberal person, not on the amount or quality of things given.
Additionally, of the two vices that surround liberality, illiberality and prodigality, it is better to be prodigal than illiberal, though much more people are illiberal than prodigal.
