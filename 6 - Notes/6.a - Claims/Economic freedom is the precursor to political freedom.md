## Summary
This is one of the main theses in Friedman’s *Capitalism and Freedom*, with him arguing that it is economic freedom and underlies political freedom. Without economic freedom, political freedom cannot stand.
## Significance
This is a strong argument for promoting economic freedom, since political freedom is is cornerstone of so many democracies worldwide.
