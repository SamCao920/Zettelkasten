## Summary
Self-respect means that one is confident that one’s conception of the good is worth carrying out, and that one has the capability to carry out that rational plan.
## Significance
Since this is the case, then we should have our political systems promote self-respect.
## Explanation
The absolute achievement of something that is spelled out in our rational plans in unimportant, with the more important thing being our sense of worth as the actor of the achievement.
