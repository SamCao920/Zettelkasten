## Summary
In a Rawlsian society, people would not be brainwashed into having Rawlsian ideas, but rather, would simply be educated in autonomy, such that they are able to derive the principles of justice themselves from the original position.
## Significance
This can be used to side-step accusations of brainwashing in the Rawlsian society.
## Note
It seems unlikely to be that even if educated in autonomy, people will be able to all come to the same conclusions that Rawls did, because Rawls’ logic relies on people having specific utility functions which would naturally differ between people.
