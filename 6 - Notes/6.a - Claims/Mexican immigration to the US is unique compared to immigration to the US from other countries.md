## Summary
Mexican immigration to the US is different from immigration from other parts of the globe to the US for a few reasons:
- There only a small geographic distance between the two countries, making it easy for immigration to (continue to) occur, legally and illegally.
- Mexican immigrants show a particular aversion to assimilation to local culture.
- Because many part of the US used to be Mexican, and thus have strong cultural roots to Mexico, Mexican immigration to the US could potentially arouse revanchist sentiments.
## Significance
This explains a few of the complications regarding Mexican immigration into the US.
