## Summary
The idea of predestination, which is a major aspect of Calvinism, provides a structure for the rationalization of moral life.
## Significance
Unlike the idea of “good works” present in other branches of Christianity, the idea of predestination entails the creation of a regimented life of asceticism (instead of just "good works"), as acting consistently with discipline represented the characteristics of someone who would go to heaven.
