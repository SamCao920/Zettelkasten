## Summary
While both were branches of Confucianism, Mencius argued that all humans were naturally good-natured, and Xunzi argued that all humans were naturally ill-natured.

## Significance
This shows the ideological diversity that existed in Confucianism following its founding, and The domination of Xunzi’s ideas would shape Confucianism until the Neo-Confucian revival.
## Explaination
- Was developed further my Mencius.
	- He developed the idea of the four cardinal virtues:
		- Benevolence
		- Righteousness 
		- Wisdom
		- Propriety 
	- Argued that all All humans are innately good, though his ideas were overshadowed by those of Xunzi until the revival of Confucianism through Neo-Confucianism.
- Also developed further by Xunzi.
	- Argued that All humans are innately evil.
	- Argued for the importance of learning to develop oneself and make oneself better.

## Why?
- Part of reason that Xunzi and Mencius have conflicting ideas is that they have different definitions of human nature: Mencius defines it as the *potential* for what one could become, while Xunzi defines it as one's *natural dispositions*.
