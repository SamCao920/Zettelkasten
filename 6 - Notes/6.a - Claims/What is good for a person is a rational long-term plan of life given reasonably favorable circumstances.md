## Summary
Rawls deliberately give a thin and non-controversial definition of the good (since people in the original position do not know what they would consider good). Importantly, his definition of good incorporates rationality, and also are necessarily based on/need the primary goods, since the execution of a rational plan needs primary goods.
## Significance
This theory of the good is what underlies all of Rawls’ other claims about what justice would be.
