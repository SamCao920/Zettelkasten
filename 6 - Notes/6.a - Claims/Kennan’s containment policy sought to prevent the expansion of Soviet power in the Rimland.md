## Summary
George F. Kennan’s “containment” policy which shaped American foreign policy during the Cold War was inspired by Spykman’s Rimland Thesis, in that Kennan wanted to prevent the Soviets from controlling as much of the Rimland as possible.
## Significance
This places the US policy of containment during the Cold War as it existed in the context of previous theories that would have justified a policy such as containment.
