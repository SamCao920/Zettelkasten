## Summary
Labor productivity grows faster in manufacturing than service sectors, since technological innovations lead to larger productivity gains when the sector is more capital-heavy (i.e., manufacturing).
## Significance
This is cause of Baumol’s cost disease, since it pushes up wages in relatively less productive sectors, putting a drag on economic growth.
