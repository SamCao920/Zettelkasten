## Summary
Arguably, the main reason for the collapse of the Han was the weak central state and the increasing power of warlords across the nation. Due to the central government’s inability to quell the Yellow Turbans, they had to call for help from regional warlords, greatly strengthening their power, eventually allowing then to challenge imperial power.
## Significance
This explains a main reason for the collapse of the Han dynasty, which threw China into the Three Kingdoms Period.
