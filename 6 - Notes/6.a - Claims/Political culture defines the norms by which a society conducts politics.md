## Summary
Political culture represents the norms and values that society holds when they conduct their politics.
## Significance
Political culture may explain the success of some societies compared to others, or, as according to Huntington’s thesis, it may shape the nature of international relations.

## Explaination
There are four commonly recognized types of political culture:
1. **Parochial:** People barely recognize the government, and there is minimal political participation. Common in tribal societies.
2. **Subject:** People see the government as powerful, but as something that they cannot influence; they simply obey the authority of the government.
3. **Participant:** People are active, engaged, and believe that they can influence politics.
4. **Civic Culture:** A mix of the previous; enough participation to ensure accountability, but enough passivity to ensure stability.
