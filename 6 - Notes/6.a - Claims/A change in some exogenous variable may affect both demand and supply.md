## Summary
A change in some exogenous variable may affect both the demand and supply in some market, and thus, affect the quantity demanded and quantity supplied, and thereby the price.
## Significance
This is someone the core concepts in comparative statics and understanding the way that exogenous factors may shape endogenous variables in a market.

## Explaination
Factors that are outside of the control of some model/market may affect the supply and demand. For example:
- A change in consumer tastes & preferences
- A change in input costs
- A change in consumer income
- A change in exchange rates
- A change in government policy/new subsidies/taxes
- Etc.
all may shift the demand and supply curves, thus resulting in changes in price and quantity demanded/supplied at the equilibrium.
