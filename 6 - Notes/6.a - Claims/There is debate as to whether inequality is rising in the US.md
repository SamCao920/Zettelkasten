## Summary
There is debate as to whether inequality is actually rising in the US, since different economists have used data differently to estimate inequality.
## Significance
This undermines the common statement that inequality is rapidly rising in the US.
## Different Measures
![[Pasted image 20260126220616.png]]
Auden and Splinter have, for example, done research into inequality that contradicts the research that Piketty, Saez, and Zucman (PSZ) have done.
One of the reasons for this difference is that the PSZ team assigns reported income proportionally, where the rich are assumed to have proportionately greater unreported income than the less-rich. Auden-Splinter, on the other hand, uses data from the IRS’s unreported income, which tracks mostly middle and lower income people’s unreported income (e.g., being paid in cash for a service), and uses that.
Both methods have bias.
