## Summary
Under most circumstances, the act of falsifying never reduces to clean deduction.
## Significance
This disproves a common misconception about the nature of science.
## Explanation
There are two main reasons for this:
1. There is not a bijection between hypotheses and models which explains the hypotheses. In fact, many hypotheses could be related to a model, and many models and be related to a single hypothesis.
2. Inaccuracies in the data could lead to incorrect falsifications. 
![[Pasted image 20260606095613.png]]
In the both image, if $P_{0A}$ is taken as the null hypothesis, rejecting it doesn’t say a lot, since there are other neutral models that produces a different distribution of alleles. If we fail to reject it, it also doesn’t say a lot, since there is a case where given that selection matters, the same result can be found. To note is that only a few of an unbounded amount of possible hypotheses and process models are shown here, which makes it impossible to draw a clear logical link between any hypothesis and any process model and any statistical model.
