## Summary
The idea that self-interest can produce overall benefits in the economy is one that is inherently utilitarian, as any deontological system would prohibit acting in an self-interested way. Only a utilitarian conception of the world would allow for self-interest to take place in an economic system.
## Significance
This crucial because it seems to reveal an ideological inconsistency in the right. Typically, right wing thinkers are the most supportive of capitalist theory, and yet often believe in structured moral theory. This seems to be a contradiction.
