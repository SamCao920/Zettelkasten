## Summary
Hoppes describes the “process of civilization” as one that is defined by diminishing time preferences, whereby people are more and more willing to engage in capital formation and to create durable consumer goods.
## Significance
This both explains the importance of time preferences as a means of ensuring the success of civilizations, it also explores the importance of time preferences in ensuring the success/establishment of a working civilization.
