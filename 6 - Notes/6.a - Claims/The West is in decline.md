## Summary
The West is current in a state of decline, which, although it could take a long time and experience fluctuations, will ultimately progress.
## Significance
This is a crucial aspect of Huntington’s thesis in *Clash of Civilizations*.
## Explanation
The West is lacking in several crucial power resource:
- Territory and population.
- Economic production.
- Military capacity.
Huntington characterizes this as a relative decline, whereby the rise of the rest is outpacing the growth in power of the West, not directly as a weakening of Western power.
## Example
An example of the decline of the West is there inability to enforce Western morality upon other countries. For instance, in Asia, the West has not been able to successfully enforce a universal application of human rights. This is due to
- The rising influence of Asian economic power, which Western businesses desperately want to work with.
- The fact that Asian nations work together in order oppose what they see as Western encroachment on their sovereignty.
