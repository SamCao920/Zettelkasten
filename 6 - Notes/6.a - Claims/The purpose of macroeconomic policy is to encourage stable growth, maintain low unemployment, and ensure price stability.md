## Summary
The purpose of macroeconomic policy is to ensure the stable growth of the economy, maintain low unemployment, and minimize inflation. If possible, it should also attempt to procure a positive trade balance.
## Significance
Macroeconomics is the study of macroeconomic policy, a branch of economics that has the stated objective. It is important to understand these objects in designing policy.
