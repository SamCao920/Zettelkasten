## Summary
Fermentation is anaerobic, meaning it does not need oxygen to occur. However, it is much less efficient than aerobic respiration (anaerobic produces 2 net ATP per glucose, while aerobic produces ~36 net ATP per glucose). 
## Significance
Not only is fermentation used to make alcohols, it it also used by our bodies when there is lack of sufficient to carry out aerobic respiration.

## Explaination
Fermentation is only the process of glycolysis, which requires 2 ATP and generates 4 ATP. The NADH that is generated through this process donates its electron back to the pyruvate, producing Lactate acid or ethanol.
