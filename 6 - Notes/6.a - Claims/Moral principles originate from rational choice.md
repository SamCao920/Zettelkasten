## Explanation
This essentially reduces morality to rational decision making, which is an idea that some find appealing, while others reject.
Theories that are in this vein include Rawls’ Contractualism, Contractarianism, and it is even related to Kant’s theories, since Kant argues that morality is something that people *rationally* agree upon.
