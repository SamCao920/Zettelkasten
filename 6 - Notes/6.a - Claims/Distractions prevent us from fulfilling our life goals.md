## Summary
Distractions prevent us from fulfilling our life goals since they constantly side track us. A potential effect of this is our eventual dismay at the fact that we have wasted our lives pursuing distractions rather than what we actually wanted to do.
## Significance
This is both a topic covered in various works of literature and also a fundamental aspect of modern society, where distractions abound.
## Explanation
With so many distractions in the modern world, it becomes increasingly more difficult to not only find one’s purpose, but also stick to one’s purpose without being distracted.
In *The Snows of Kilimanjaro*, Harry is constantly sidetracked by his worldly pursuits, which eventually leads to his inability to pursue his true art, writing.
