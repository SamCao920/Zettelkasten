## Summary
A rational action not identified with desire or temper that is clearly voluntary. It involves the process of deliberation.
## Significance
Choice is important in Aristotle’s philosophy since virtue and vice are things that an individual chooses to do, not merely feel.
## Explanation
Choice is making a decision after a decision, directing our aims in accordance with the decision.
