## Summary
A big reasons why IBM’s market share has fallen in many different industries is because of their failure to invest in emerging technologies, which meant that other companies with began with much smaller market shares were able to out compete them.
## Significance
This demonstrates the importance of keeping up with technological innovation, as falling behind could mean losing a large part of your market share, especially in industries where it is easy to switch to a competitor.
## Notes
- The industries that they lost in include: database software, operating systems, the PC market, semiconductors, etc.
	- Much of its revenue comes from middle software and services (revenue growth has slowed here, however).
	- Its intense stock buyback program hurt growth in the 2010s.
	- Their investment in Linux was a good decision, since Linux has come to dominate the server industry, and their Red Hat serves as a main service that provides open source software to companies
		- They adopt a flexible approach that doesn’t lock companies in when they choose to use their software, which they claim that customers wouldn’t want. 
			- This is interesting though, since they did something similar when they put DOS and OS2 on their computer, and it failed. This seems to have similar intentions.
