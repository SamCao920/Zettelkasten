## Summary
During the dynastic period of China, highly influenced by Confucianism, many government officials in China were also scholars.

## Significance
Their existence was a testament to the Meritocracy that was present in Chinese society at the time, though the Imperial Examination System. It also strengthened the influence of Confucianism in Chinese governmental institutions.
