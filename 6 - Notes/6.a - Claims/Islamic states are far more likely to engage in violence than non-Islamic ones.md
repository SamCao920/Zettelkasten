## Summary
It has been observed than Muslim states are more likely to engage in violence than non-Muslims ones, both towards other Muslim countries and non-Muslims countries.
## Significance
This could explain the relatively higher amount of conflicts between Muslims, other Muslims, and non-Muslims.
## Explanation
There are many possible reasons for this being the case that have been proposed:
- Historical culture which glorified conquest for Muslims.
- Due to their historical conquests, Muslims often live in close proximity to people of different cultures.
- Muslim culture is less tolerant of non-Muslims, drawing a clear distinction between *Dar Al-Islam* and *Dar Al-harb*.
- Muslims victimization by the West (although this would not explain Muslim conflicts against non-Muslim minorities).
- The absence of a core state in the Muslim world.
- Youth bulge, meaning there is an excess in the amount of population of fighting age.
