## Summary
The idea that marginalism is a method of economic that is declining.
## Significance
This has profound for the future direction of economics, and identifies a trendline, a direction of movement for the field of economics.
## Explanation
Tyler Cowen identifies five reasons that marginalism is declining:
1. Empirical science is now displacing theoretical science, meaning that the more theoretical nature of marginalist thinking has been replaced by the data-driven approach of more empirical methods.
2. Price theory is declining in importance.
3. Finance, which was seen as the ultimate applicant of marginalist thinking, has switched to more empirical science.
4. Machine learning is making marginalism obsolete, since it can directly find the structure in data, rather than needing to have a framework like marginalism guide the process of finding the structure in the data.
5. Economics is increasingly turning to the left, and people on the left are generally more uncomfortable with the conclusions made by marginalism.
