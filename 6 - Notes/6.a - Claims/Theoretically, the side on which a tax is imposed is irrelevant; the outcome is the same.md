## Summary
It the statutory incidence of a tax does not matter, since the ultimate market outcome will be the same regardless of if it is tax on the consumers vs. the producers.
## Significance
This means that the demand of whether tax should be on consumers or producers is irrelevant in many circumstances.
## Note
There are circumstances in the real world, including with the feasibility of implementation, ease of tax loop holes, etc., such that this law does not always hold.
