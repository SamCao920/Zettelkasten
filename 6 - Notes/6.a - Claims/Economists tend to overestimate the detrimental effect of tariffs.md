## Summary
Economists tend the overestimate the negative effects of tariffs, since in theory, they are detrimental to the economy. However, other factors may make it so that the effects of tariffs are not as extremely as originally predicted.
## Significance
This is significant since the Trump administration has implemented many tariffs, which economists claim would wreck the US economy, despite that not having happened.

## Explaination
There are several factors that may explain why the effect of tariffs may not be as bad as predicted:
- Specifically regarding the Trump administration’s tariffs, many of the highest tariffs have been reduced, which means that their economic effect is diminished.
- Exports only make up around 11% of the American economy.
- Booms in specific sectors of the economy may make up for the dent caused by tariffs.
- Businesses may temporarily absorb the increased costs brought on by tariffs.
However, in the longer-term, tariffs may increase uncertainty in the economy, and thus be detrimental.
