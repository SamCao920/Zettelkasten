## Summary
It is common to observe how once government institutions are created, it is difficult for them to be ended, since there are so many stakeholders, and there is no incentive for efficiency.
## Significance
This means that over time, governments can become more inefficient and bloated.
## Examples
- The USDA got involved with the Farmers Home Administration in the 1940s in order to support farmers and improve farming. However, even as farms became big businesses over the next few decades, the USDA continued to work in housing, shifting from farmers explicitly to “rural communities.”
