## Summary
One of Thomas Hardy’s preferred techniques is to encapsulate some larger, often symbolic movement in the plot in some minor event.
## Significance
This is a masterful way to hint at the way that the story is heading, and allows the reader to gain a deeper, and more direct understanding of the themes and conflicts in the plot.

## Examples
- When the two wagoners (one hired by Henchard and the other, Farfrae) collide, it is symbolic of a larger struggle between the old and the new, the modern and the archaic, and the past and future.
