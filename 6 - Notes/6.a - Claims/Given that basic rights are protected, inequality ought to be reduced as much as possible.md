## Summary
Rawl’s famous idea that is related to his Veil of Ignorance. Essentially places basic rights and freedoms as the first-order requirement that must be satisfied before attempting to reduce inequality.
## Significance
This is the fundamental axiom that underlies Rawl’s political and moral philsophy.
