## Summary
While there was a nuclear monopoly/oligopoly in the world, the leading powers were able to use the influence of said nukes to ensure stability in the global system. However, with nuclear proliferation, the global system is likely to fragment.
## Significance
This means that nuclear proliferation would be something that the current holders of nukes desperately do not want to occur.
## Examples
- China helped Iran and Pakistan develop nukes. Part of the motivation for this would be to challenge the dominance of the US
- Today, there is not a race to build more/better nukes. Rather, it is race to prevent nations that do not yet have nukes from building any.
