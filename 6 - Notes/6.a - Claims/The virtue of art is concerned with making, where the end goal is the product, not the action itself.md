## Summary
The virtue of art (*techne*) is a productive state that is “truly reasoned.” it focuses on the production, i.e., the end goal, rather than the action, i.e., the process by which one reaches the end goal. It deals with the variable, not the necessary/eternal. It involves reason, not just instinct.
## Significance
The virtue that allows people to create things that don’t necessarily have to exist.
