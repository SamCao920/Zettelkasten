## Summary
The Calvin cycle produces G3P, which is the base for the creation of many other products in a plant.
## Significance
This explains the usefulness of the Calvin cycle, specifically, the G3P that it generates.
## Explaination
The G3P generated could be used to create:
- Carbohydrates
	- Sugars: glucose and fructose
	- Starch: amylose (used for storage)
	- Fiber: cellulose (used for structure)
- Lipids
	- Storage of energy (long-term)
	- Phospholipids (for cell membranes)
- Proteins and nucleic acids: More difficult, as it requires N and P.
- A small amount of the G3P is turned into glucose and used for cellular respiration.
