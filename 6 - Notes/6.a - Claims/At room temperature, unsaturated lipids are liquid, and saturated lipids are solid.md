## Summary
At room temperature, unsaturated lipids (that is, lipids that have double carbon hydrocarbon chains) are liquid, and saturated lipids are solid.
## Significance
This may be relevant for AP Bio.
## Note
The lipids referred to here are **triglycerides**, meaning that they have three hydrocarbon chains.
