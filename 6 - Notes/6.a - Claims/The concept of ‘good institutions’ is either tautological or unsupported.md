## Summary
The idea that the thesis that “good institutions” lead to economic growth is false because the concept of “good institutions” is fundamentally mistaken.
## Significance
This rejects the thesis that there is such a thing as “good institutions,” or that it does not prove a causation that “good institutions lead to economic growth.”
## Explanation
Since it is the case that rich countries have many of the things that we associate with “good institutions,” it doesn’t mean that those things are necessarily the cause of the countries being rich. There is the risk of missing confounding factors and also the possibility of reverse causation.
Overall, the concept of “institutions” is too vague. A better method of study is to look at specific institutions instead.
