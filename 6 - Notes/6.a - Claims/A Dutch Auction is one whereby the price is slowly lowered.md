## Summary
In an English auction, the price of an item is slowly bid up until only one bidder remains. In an Dutch auction, the opposite is true. The price of the item is slowly reduced until someone makes a bid.
## Significance
This is a difference type of auction that differs from our typical perceptions of a auction.
