## Summary
Deliberation is defined by Aristotle as what occurs when the right course of action is not clearly defined, and reason/logic is needed to find the correct means.
## Significance
Understanding deliberation is important for understanding Aristotle’s definition of choice, which is important for his definitions of virtue and vice.
## Note
We only deliberate about the means, and not the end, since ends are set by character and desire. So, for example, we do not deliberate “should I become more healthy?” rather, we deliberate “what should I eat today to become more healthy?”
The final result of a deliberation is the “first action,” i.e., the first step in the means that eventually brings one to the end.
Deliberation is also not concerned with particular facts, as such facts are the matter and concern for sense perception. For instance, we do not deliberate “Am I feeling something soft?”
