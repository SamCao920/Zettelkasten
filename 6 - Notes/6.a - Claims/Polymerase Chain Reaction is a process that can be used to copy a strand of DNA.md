## Summary
Polymerase Chain Reaction is a process by which a strand of DNA can be copied millions/billions of times.
## Significance
This process can make it possible for someone to sequence DNA.
## Explanation
First, procure a strand of DNA and place it within a test tube that also consists of raw material for DNA synthesis, DNA primers that match the bits of the DNA strands that you want to copy, a heat-resistant polymerase, and a buffer solution.
Second, heat up and cool down the solution rapidly to denature the DNA, then allow the primers to attach to the DNA, then utilize the polymerase to create copies. Repeat as many times as needed.
