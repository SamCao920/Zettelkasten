## Summary
The Polish economic miracle occurred largely as a result of EU cohesion funds, which it effectively invested into infrastructure and agriculture; the EU single market, which allowed for the free flow of goods and capital across Europe; and a diversified economy, which helped it weather tough economic storms.
## Significance
This explains why Poland’s economy is as strong as it is, given its background as a former Soviet state.
