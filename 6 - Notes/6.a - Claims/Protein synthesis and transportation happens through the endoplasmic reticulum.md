## Summary
When proteins are constructed by ribosomes, some that need to be transported to a specific location, are attached to the ER first, where they are made. Then, through the use of a vesicle, which is formed from the membrane of the ER, the proteins are first transported to the Golgi apparatus, repackaged, then sent to their end location.
## Significance
This explains the function of the ER, which is relevant for AP Bio.
