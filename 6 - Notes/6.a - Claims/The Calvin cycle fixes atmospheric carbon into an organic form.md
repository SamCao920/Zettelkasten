## Summary
The Calvin cycle takes carbon dioxide from the air, which, when combined with existing G3P molecules, can create one extra G3P (using the carbon from carbon dioxide), which is used to create other molecules in the plant.
## Significance
This is the main process by which plants turn the CO2 in the air into usable material for itself. It is also a key part of the Calvin cycle.
