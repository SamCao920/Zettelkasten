## Summary
The institution of the family is crucial for the prospering of liberal ideals in a society.
## Significance
While many argue that the institution of the family is incompatible with the principles of liberalism, this note says otherwise.
## Explanations
- Family is where humans first learn about rules and rule-making. They also learn about decreasing their time preference, which is a crucial component in being able to participate in the free market.
- Family members have genuine information about the state/nature of people in their family, and are thus knowledge-process institutions which give far more granularity then if the state were to attempt such a thing.
- Families can train their children to have the liberal ethos, which produce people who act in the same way in society.
- The principle of subsidiarity finds its most natural representation in the family. 
- Cultural liberalism likewise originates from the family, and attempting to enforce a single model of the family, whether left or right wing, threatens the diversity which undergirds liberal societies.
- Family teaches us epistemic humility, since we are forced to be with different people that we did not choose.
- Importantly, defending the concept of family is not the same as defending a particular conception of the family.
