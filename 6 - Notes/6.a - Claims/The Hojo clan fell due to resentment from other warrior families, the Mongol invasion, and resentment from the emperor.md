## Summary
The Hojo fell from power due to resentment from the other warrior families, as the Hojo held de facto power in Japan as the regents for the Kamakura, the cost of the defense against the Mongols, and the allying of the emperor with enemies of the Hojo.
## Significance
This explains the fall of the Hojo clan, and the rise of the Ashikaga shogunate.
