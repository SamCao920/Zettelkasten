## Summary
The dollar is ineffective as a tool to exert international pressure, since the people that use dollars the most are the most open and democratic economies, which would not be the target of dollar diplomacy anyways. In fact, this encourages the targets of dollar diplomacy to find alternatives, which further weakens its effect in the future.
## Significance
This note makes the claim that dollar diplomacy is not useful, which is contra the stance of many current US policy stances.
## Quote
> [!QUOTE] “Iran war has exposed the weakness of the dollar”
> Part of the problem is that being cut off from the dominant global payments system is only a threat because the dollar economy is so convenient and profitable to deal in. That means that the weapon is most effective against open economies that are integrated into global supply chains. But these are rarely the ones worth threatening.
> Having been a source of global stability for so long, the dollar system has evolved to be a source of instability as it has become more weaponised. As we stated: “As the US ratchets up pressure, other countries will look to escape dollar power, likely provoking the US to double down in response”
