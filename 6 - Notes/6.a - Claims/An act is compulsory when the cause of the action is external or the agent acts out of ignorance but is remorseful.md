## Summary
An act is compulsory (otherwise known as "involuntary") when the cause of the action is external, i.e., what is compelling the agent to conduct the action does not originate in the agent. Another possibility is that the agent acts out of ignorance, but they are remorseful after the fact.
## Significance
This is Aristotle’s definition for what is a compulsory action. He will use this definition in order to justify many aspects of his philosophy.
## Explanation
An act is involuntary if
1. They were coerced into committing that action by an external force.
2. They acted out of ignorance (of the particular, not of the principle), and they repent for their actions after the fact.
The agent is never morally responsible for their action if they act involuntarily.
