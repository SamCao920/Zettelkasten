## Summary
Compensating differentials is the idea that some jobs pay more due to the non-pecuniary benefits (or disadvantages) associated with employment. This is evident for example for lighthouse workers, who receive very high pay as they are isolated and the job is also dangerous.
## Significance
This can explain some of the variation in the job market, and why some jobs pay a lot more, despite being less technical or physical demanding than some other jobs.
