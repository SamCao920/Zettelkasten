## Summary
The world does not care about what is fair. One’s luck is entirely independent from their moral status/virtuousness.
## Significance
This is a common theme in many philosophies and or works of literature.
## Examples
- The figure of Oedipus, all that he has to suffer when he finds out about his true nature, was not his fault. In fact, he saved the city of Thebes from the Sphinx. Yet, he is not recompensed with his service, rather arbitrarily punished despite his good deeds.
