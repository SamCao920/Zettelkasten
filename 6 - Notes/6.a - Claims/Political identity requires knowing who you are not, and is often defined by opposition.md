## Summary
People are defined more by what they are not than by what they are; what they are not is more important to politics than what they are.
## Significance
This is a view of looking at politics and the way that politics happens different from the typical view.
## Explanation
- Ideology is no longer the most important part about politics. 
- Rather, it is culture: ancestry, religion, language, history, values, customs, and institutions.
- And with regarding these specific factors, people identify against the people who different from them in these specific factors, rather than specific identifying with people who share the same factors.
