## Summary
A kinase, in addition to being controlled by being phosphorylated, can also be controlled via the use of a secondary messenger, cyclin, or calcium ion.
## Significance
This is crucial for the function of kinases, since by having multiple means of activation, it ensures that the kinases are only active in the right environments.
