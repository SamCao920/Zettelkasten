## Summary
When two conductors touch, their combined charge remains the same. However, the charges are individually evenly distributed across the two conductors.
## Significance
This represents the law of charge conservation in action, and is relevant for AP Physics C E&M.
## Example
Given two conductors, one with $Q$ and the other with $2Q$ charge, when the two conductors touch, they both end up with $1.5Q$.
