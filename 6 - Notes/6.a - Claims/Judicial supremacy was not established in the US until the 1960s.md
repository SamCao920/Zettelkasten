## Summary
The idea that the court is the supreme authority that is able to determine the constitutionality of laws was not established until the 1960s with *Aaron v. Cooper*. However, the concept of judicial review had been established early on, by *Marbury v. Madison*. 
## Significance
This refutes a common misconception that judicial review and judicial supremacy are the same thing, and that they originated at the same time (i.e., in the aftermath of *Marbury vs. Madison*).
