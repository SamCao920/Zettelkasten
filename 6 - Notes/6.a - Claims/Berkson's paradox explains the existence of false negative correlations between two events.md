## Summary
Berkson’s paradox explains why there often seems to be a negative correlation between two independent variables due to selection bias.
## Significance
This may help us avoid falling into believing false dichotomies.
## Example
There seems to be a negative correlation the attractiveness of a celebrity and their acting prowess. However, this is likely due to selection bias, as those who are neither attractive nor good at acting become actors.
