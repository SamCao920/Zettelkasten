## Summary
A nonconductor refers to both *poor* conductors and also *(absolutely) non*conductors. An insulator is absolutely not conductive.

## Significance
The two vocabulary is often mixed up, but it is better to have clarity regarding to topic to avoid making mistakes on questions related to this topic.
