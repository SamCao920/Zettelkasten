## Summary
Uncertainty in the economy leads to slowed economic growth, in terms of diminished consumption, hiring, and investment. It also weakens the effect of monetary and fiscal policy. However, its effects in the long run are indeterminate, as uncertainty may in fact encourage R&D and innovation.
## Significance
Today, uncertainty, especially under the Trump administration is especially prominent. It is important to understand the significance of such uncertainty, especially for the economy in the short and long term.
## Explaination
Uncertainty in countercyclical, meaning that it rises during period of economic downturn. This also means that it can worsen recessions, as it can lead to poorer quality of information, heightened policy experimentation, and generally amplify volatility. However, it has ambiguous long term consequences since it seems to have beneficial effects on innovation.
