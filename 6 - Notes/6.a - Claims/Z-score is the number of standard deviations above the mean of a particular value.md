## Summary
A z-score is the number of standard deviations above the mean of a particular value. This can be calculated with the formula: $z = \frac{x_{1}- \bar x}{s}$.
## Significance
Z-score is used to standardize the distance of a value from the mean, such that calculations can be performed easily.
