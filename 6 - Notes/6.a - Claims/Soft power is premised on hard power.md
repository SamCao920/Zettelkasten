## Summary
Soft power is something that is largely built on top of hard power, i.e., a foundation of hard power is needed in order for soft power to succeed.
## Significance
Huntington argues that the soft power exhibited by the East Asian countries are built upon their hard power.
## Explanation
Soft power is based on the attractiveness of culture/ideology, and soft power is only seen as affective because it is rooted in the material success and influence of a region that exhibited the power.
The success of the East Asian states make their soft power particular effective.
