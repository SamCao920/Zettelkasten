## Summary
Where a population is divided into non-overlapping groups, a few groups are randomly selected, and the entire population of that region is sampled.

## Significance
This is one of the major way of sampling. This is easier than simple random sampling and stratified random sampling, especially when subjects are geographically spread out or hard to list individually. However, the clusters must be representative.
