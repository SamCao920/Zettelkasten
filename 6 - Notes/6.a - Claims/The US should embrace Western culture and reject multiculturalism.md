## Summary
The US is made weaker by embracing multiculturalism, which undermines the core Western civilization that underlies the US. Ultimately, this makes the US directionless and weak.
## Significance
This presents an argument against multiculturalism.
## Explanation
The US should instead embrace its civilizational roots and reconnect with Europe through things like free trade agreements in order to revitalize Western civilization.
Specifically, Huntington states that the US must:
- Integrate militarily, politically, and economically with other states of the West.
- Incorporate into NATO & EU more Western (culturally speaking) states in Europe.
- Encourage alignment of Latin American states with the West.
- Restrict the military development of Islamic and Sinic states.
- Slow the drift of Japan away from the West.
- Accept Russia as the core state of Orthodoxy.
- Maintain Western technological and military superiority.
Most importantly, the US must **recognize that Western intervention in the affairs of other civilizations is counterproductive and almost always detrimental to US interests in the long run.**
