## Summary
There are main three problems with price controls: supply shortages, misallocation of resources, and rent-seeking.
## Significance
These represents the large issues that manifest when price controls are implemented.
## Explanation
Price controls cause:
1. Supply shortages.
2. Misallocation of resources, since people have to spend time looking for alternatives, waiting in line to get something that they need, and the people that ultimately get something are often not the ones that value the thing the most.
3. Rent-seeking, since people will engage in activities such as attempting to bribe the people who are selling the good.
