## Summary
The invention of paper money, which replaced much heavier coin money, allowed for the easier transport of value across vast distances, making trade easier. Additionally, it no longer meant that the production of units of value was tied to the rarity of the intrinsic material.
## Significance
The invention of paper money is one of the most important developments in economics, and allowed for the development of advanced economics in the future.
