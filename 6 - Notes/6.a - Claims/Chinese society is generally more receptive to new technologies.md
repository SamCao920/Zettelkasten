## Summary
Chinese society, due to their historically quick adaption of new technologies and the positive benefits of such adaptations, are generally more positive regarding new technologies. 
- This explains their largely positive outlook towards AI and its potential. 83% of people are optimistic about AI in China, whereas only 39% of people are optimistic about it in the US.
## Significance
This means that the Chinese may receive the benefits of new technologies before other societies do.
