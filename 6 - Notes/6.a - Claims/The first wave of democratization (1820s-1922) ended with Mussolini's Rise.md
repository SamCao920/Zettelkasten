## Summary
The first wave of of democracy began around the 1820, and ended around the 1920s.

## Significance
This is the longest and arguably, most impactful wave of democratization. It is also the one that had the most conspicuous reversal, with the rise of Fascism and Communism at its end.

## First Wave
The first wave of democracy began around the 1820s, when a majority of white males in the US were able to vote. Over the next decades, countries around the world began to democratize. This wave ended around 1922, when Benito Mussolini overturned the democratic government in Italy.
