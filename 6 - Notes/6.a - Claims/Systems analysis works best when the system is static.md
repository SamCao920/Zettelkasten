## Summary
The idea that systems analysis is best applicable and most accurate when the system is not dynamic and unpredictable.
## Significance
This outlines when systems analysis ought not to be used.
## Explanation
Systems analysis does not work very well when the system is dynamic is changes drastically (and often unpredictably) with a slight change in input.
## Examples
- When scientists (lead by Song Jian) attempted to use systems theory to model birth rates during the formation of the one-child policy, th ey failed, and China now suffers extremely low birth rates
- When Jay Forrester attempted to model the entire world through the flow of capital and resources, he was not able to predict the future accurately either.
