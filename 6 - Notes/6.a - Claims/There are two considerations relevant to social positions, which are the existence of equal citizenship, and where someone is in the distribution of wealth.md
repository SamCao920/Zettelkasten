## Summary
There are only two considerations which are relevant when we consider how to measure people’s social positions: 
1. whether there is equal citizenship, and 
2. where someone is in the distribution of wealth & income.
## Significance
Here, Rawls specifies what exactly he is measuring when he measures the “expectations” that a representative might have.
## Note
Here, (1) is satisfied by [equal basic liberties]([[Each person has equal basic rights most extensively compatible with similar liberties for others]]) and (2) is satisfied by [the second principle of justice]([[Social and economic inequalities are to satisfy the Difference Principle and Fair Equality of Opportunity]]).
