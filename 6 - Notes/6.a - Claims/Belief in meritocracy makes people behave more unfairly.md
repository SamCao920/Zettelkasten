## Summary
A belief in meritocracy makes people act more unfairly towards one another.
## Significance
This can be used to support arguments that oppose meritocracy.
## Explanation
Scientists have designed experiments where participants play fake games of skill. 
- In such cases, they are more likely to distribute winnings to themselves when they believed that they had won as a result for their superior skill compared to when they thought that it was just luck.
`But doesn't the very fact that this instinct evolved mean that it is in fact the case that, more likely than not, the more meritorious person deserves more rewards? It seems like we are merely exploiting edge cases in our intuition in order to come up with these results.`
