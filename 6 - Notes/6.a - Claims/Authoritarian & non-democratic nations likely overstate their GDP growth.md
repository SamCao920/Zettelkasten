## Summary
Satellite night light data shows that authoritarian or non-democratic nations overstate their GDP growth.
## Significance
Explain briefly how this idea fits into a bigger system, problem, or use-case. Make it meaningful to your future self.
## Explanation
- **Autocracy Gradient**: As a country becomes more authoritarian, the same increase in nightlight level translates into higher levels of GDP growth.
	- The gradient is larger before an election, or when growth is low.
- GDP growth in authoritarian nations are typically overstated by a factor of 1.15 to 1.3.
	- Over the long term, this represents a 40% inflation of GDP.
- China is a major example of this case.
	- Even beyond analysis with nightlights, analysis of GDP growth through the construction of an Engels curve showed the curve shifting over time, indicating overstating and understating inflation by the government over time.
		- The Engel Curve analysis showed the growth was actually underestimated in the 90s, but over estimated from the 2000s onwards. 
		- In total, the Engel-Adjusted real growth of the Chinese economy has fallen below the official growth reported by the Chinese government, in largely part to the the fall in growth during the 2008-2009 crisis.
