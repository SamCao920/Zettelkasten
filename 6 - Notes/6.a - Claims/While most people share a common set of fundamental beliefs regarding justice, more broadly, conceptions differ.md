## Summary
Most people share the belief that
- There should be no unfounded/irrational distinctions between people in assigning rights and duties.
- There should be a proper/fair distribution of social advantages.
However, they would disagree as to how those principles are arrived upon.
## Significance
Here, Rawls is making clear the distinction between the concept of justice and conceptions of justice. In the rest of the book, it is explaining his conception of Justice and why is the best one.
## Note
This is meant to to be extremely deflationary, since Rawls will fill in the structure step up here with his own conception of justice.
