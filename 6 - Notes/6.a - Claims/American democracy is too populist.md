### Summary
American democracy is too populist, focusing too much on “democracy,” and not enough on constitutional liberalism and the overall wellbeing of the nation.

### Significance
This idea presents an interesting critique of American democracy that presents its “democracticness” in a more nuanced view.

## Rising Populism
American politicians are catering to the masses too much, and ignoring the actual needs of the nation and constitutional liberalism.
