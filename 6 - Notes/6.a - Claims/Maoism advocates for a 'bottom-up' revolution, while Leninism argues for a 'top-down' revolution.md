## Summary
Since there was a distinct lack of industrial workers in China that concentrated in working areas, China needed the revolution to spring from the people in the countryside, thus creating a “bottom-up” revolution that is counter to Marx’s original intentions. Importantly, administration remains “top-down.”
## Significance
This explains a key difference between the Maoists and Marxist-Leninists.
