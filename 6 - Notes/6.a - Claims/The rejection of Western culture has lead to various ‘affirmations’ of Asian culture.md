## Summary
The rejection of Western culture in East Asia has led to various beliefs that have been integrated into the way that Asians think about Asian culture.
## Significance
This explains the rise in tensions across cultural boundaries.
## Explanation
The US's power has declined in Asia due to a combination of the following factors:
- The US does not have the will to pressure Asian societies.
- The US thinks concessions will be reciprocal, but Asians just see them as victories.
- Asian countries have banded together in order to oppose the dominance of the US in the region.
There are four such “affirmations”:
1. Asia will have sustained economic growth that will overtake the West.
2. Asian culture leads to economic growth, and is thus superior to the West (specifically, the influence of Confucianism is prominent).
3. Relative homogeneity of Asian culture, which is again a result of influence from Confucianism.
4. Asian culture should be adopted by the West.
