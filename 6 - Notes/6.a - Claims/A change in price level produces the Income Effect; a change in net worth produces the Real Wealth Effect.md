## Summary
The Income Effect and the Real Wealth Effect are two different economic concepts that, although somewhat related, describe different phenomena. The former is produced by a change in the price level, and the latter is produced via an increase in an individual’s net worth.
## Significance
These two concepts are very similar, but there are some crucial differences between the two that should be clarified.
