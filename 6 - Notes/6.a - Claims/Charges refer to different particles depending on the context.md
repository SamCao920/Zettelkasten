## Summary
Depending on the context, “charge” in physics could refer to electrons, protons, or ions.

## Significance
Often, physics problems just mention how “charges move,” but it is not explicitly stated what that entails. Clarifying this detail would aid with solving these problems.

## Carriers of Charge
- Electrons are negatively charged particles that usually move in conductors.
- Protons are positively charged particles that do not move (though in some models, they are shown moving).
- Ions are atoms that have lost or gained electrons, so they carry a net charge.
