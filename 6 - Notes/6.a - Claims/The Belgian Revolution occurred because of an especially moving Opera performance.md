## Summary
The Belgium Revolution of 1830, which saw the establishment of a United Kingdom of Belgium following its occurrence, was instigated by a particular moving performance of the Opera *La muette de Portic*.
## Significance
This shows the power of music to instill strong emotions in people, such that it can serve as the match that lights a pile of dry sticks on fire, eventually leading to a revolution.
