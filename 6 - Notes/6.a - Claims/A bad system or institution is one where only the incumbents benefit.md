## Summary
A bad system is one where only the incumbents are able to benefit. In such a system, innovation is reduced, and the total surplus is usually reduced. Systems with entrenched power hierarchies are usually ones that benefit only the incumbents. 
## Significance
This is relevant for both political and economic systems, as in such system, the consequences of only incumbents benefiting is large.
