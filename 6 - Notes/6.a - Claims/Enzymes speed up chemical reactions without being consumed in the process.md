## Summary
Enzymes are proteins (sometimes RNA) that speed up chemical processes by lowering the required activation energy.

## Significance
Enzymes are a core part of the AP Bio curriculum.

## Activation Energy
- Enzymes lower activation energy by creating an environment that is conducive for the chemical reaction to occur. This may involve:
	- Bringing reactants together in the correct orientation.
	- Forming temporary bonds with substrates.

## Speed
- Enzymes can make reactions happen millions or billions of times faster.
