## Summary
While it can be easier for lower animals to satisfy themselves, animals with greater intelligence can make the fact that they will never be able to satisfy all of their desires bearable.
## Significance
This explains something fundamental about human existence, namely that though we may not be able to achieve complete satisfaction since we are such advanced and creatures, that very fact means that we can allow ourselves to make this incompleteness bearable.
