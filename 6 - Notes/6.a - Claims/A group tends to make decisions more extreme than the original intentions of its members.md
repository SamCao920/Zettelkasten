## Summary
A group tends to make decisions that are more extreme than the original intentions of its members in both directions (both originally cautious and risky actions could be amplified).
## Significance
This demonstrates a core principle when working in groups, and helps one realize when a group decision may have strayed away from the original intentions of the members.
