## Summary
Buddhism was overtaken by the *Bhakti* movement, a movement with roots in Hinduism, around the turn of the first milenium.

## Significance
This explains why Buddhism barely exists in India, despite originating there.

## Explaination
- Spread through Indian between 600 - 1300 A.D., and overtook Buddhism.
- Made Hindu practice more accessible to the common people, even people of low caste, since it focused on intense worship of deities, rather than philosophical speculation or sacrifices, often conducted by the Brahmins.
