## Summary
The idea that, though it may be unintuitive at first, comedy is a medium that can be far more pessimistic and negative than tragedy.
## Significance
This is an important interpretation of the forms of comedy and tragedy, reinterpreting them in a way that shifts and entirely changes their stated purposes.
## Explanation
In tragedy, we take the noble aspiration of man seriously, in order for the tragic events to even have an impact. In comedy, however, we merely “laugh our heads off” when faced with the despairing reality of the world, and view the events of the world as simply petty and inconsequential. We withdraw from sympathy (as in tragedy) and engage in a more desperate and cynical act of laughing at the misfortune of others.
