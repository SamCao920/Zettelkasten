## Summary
The actions of many characters in Hamlet (play), for example, Hamlet or Ophelia, could be interpreted through the lens of Freudian psychoanalysis. Hamlet’s desire to aid his mother and hatred towards his uncle is a manifestation of the Oedipus complex, as is Ophelia’s relationship with her father.
## Significance
This mode of analysis clarified some aspects of the play, especially regarding the motivations of the characters and the actions that they take. Hamlet’s repression of his own thoughts can be construed as resulting in an inaction throughout the play, driving the plot forwards.
