## Summary
Japanese companies care fundamentally structured differently than Western ones, making them better at incremental progress than drastic breakthroughs.
## Significance
This explains why Japanese companies have dominated some fields while Western companies dominate over ones.
## Explanation
Overall, the Japanese system is more decentralized and shareholder focused. For instance, 
- unions are company based, and not industry based.
- workers are more generalized than in Western firms, so they can solve problems all along a production process.
	- The *andon* cord is something unique to Japanese manufacturing, whereby anyone alone a production line can pull the string if they spot any flaws in the product.
- there is very little equity from investors, and its mostly from other companies. This means that companies are not in pursuit of short-term profit, but can rather focus on long-term goals.
- people who typically work their whole lives at a single company, and there is never any mass layoffs.
- promotions based on seniority, not productivity.
All of these facets of the system are interconnected and present as a unified system whereby they cannot be implemented individually. And all of this means that the Japanese firm is able to expand into a variety of industries that range widely.
