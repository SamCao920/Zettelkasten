## Summary
Ionic bonds are bonds that transfer electrons between two ions, one positive and one negative.

## Significance
This is relevant for AP Bio.

## Bonding Mechanism
When one atom transfers electrons to another.
Ionic bonds form between metals and non-metals since metals usually have more electrons than they need, and non-metals have less than they need. When a metal and a non-metal interact, the metal gives the non-metal its extra electron, and thus, the metal becomes positively charged (its proton, which is positively charged, overpowers its remaining electrons) and the non-metal becomes negatively charged (its proton is over powered by its electrons). Now, since positives and negatives attract one another, a bond is formed.
