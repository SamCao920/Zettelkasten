## Summary
Hamlet explores the idea of metacognition, that is, thinking about thinking, in Hamlet.
## Significance
This is something that Shakespeare starts doing a lot more in his plays after this, making his plays a lot more philosophical.
## Evidence
*For here lies the point: if I drown myself wittingly, it argues an act, and an act hath three branches—it is to act, to do, to perform. Argal, she drowned herself wittingly (5.1.10-13).*

This quotes demonstrates clearly the thinking process of one of the grave diggers, being very clear having precise logic. It show’s Shakespeare’s exploration of logical and reasoning, and thinking about people thinking about such matters.
