## Summary
Any sort of regulation enforced by law is, by its nature, context-insensitive. For example, a law may prevent *x*, even though there might be (rare, but) generally acceptable conditions under which it would be moral permissible to *x*.
## Significance
This shows the often unintended side effect of regulation, which can lead to a slew of unintended consequences, misallocated resources, and general harm.
