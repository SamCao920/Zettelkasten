## Summary
AI represents something that is entirely new compared to past innovations (in terms of epistemology) of humans. This manifests both in its potential upside and downside.
## Significance
This represents AI as something that is entirely new in terms of the epistemology that it represents.
## Explanation
The "magics" referenced here are to broad, informational improvements that humanity has experienced, not specific technological advancements.
Humanity's first "magic" is history, a means of storing information which meant that information could be passed down to future generations and thus preserved with detail.
Humanity's second "magic" is science, a means of making observations of nature rigorous and testable, creating generalizable laws of nature in the process.
Humanity's third "magic" is AI, which is able to take in massive amounts of data and come to some sort of conclusion, despite there being no discernable generalizable laws that are formulated in the process.
- While this gives us a huge amount of power in that it allows us to find regularities in complex systems that we would not otherwise be able to find, it also means that internal mechanisms of AI are not able to be comprehended by humans.
