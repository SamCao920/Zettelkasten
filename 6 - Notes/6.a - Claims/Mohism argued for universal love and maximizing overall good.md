## Summary
Argued for an early form of Consequentialism, favoring performing actions that you maximize the overall good.

## Significance
One of the major schools of ancient Chinese philosophy.

## Ideas
- Argued for universal love, as opposed to the partiality regarding love that was espoused by Confucianism.
- Developed an early form of the State of Nature argument.
