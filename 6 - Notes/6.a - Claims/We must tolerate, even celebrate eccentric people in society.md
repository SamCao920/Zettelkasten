## Summary
Since it is the case that we tolerate people having different aesthetic choices and the such, it seems that, we must also tolerate people with different lifestyle choices, i.e., the eccentric.
## Significance
This must be the case since otherwise, we miss out on the geniuses in society and the people who are truly of strong character.
