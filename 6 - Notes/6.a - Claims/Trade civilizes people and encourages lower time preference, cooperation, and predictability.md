## Summary
The idea that trade makes people more civilized, self-controlled (lower time preference), more willing to cooperate, and more predictable. People rely more on trade and bargaining and contracts than violence.
## Significance
This represents the Doux Commerce theory, which has been highly influential.
## Explanation
Studies have shown that trade flows increase the cost of conflict, which has the effect of reducing the level of conflict. Additionally, through looking at the initial days of wide-body aircraft which allowed for more goods to be transported, researches found that more trade resulted in a significant decrease in conflict.
All of this evidence points to the fact that trade does seem to mitigate conflict.
