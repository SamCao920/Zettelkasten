## Summary
People often obsess over what are ultimately just abstractions or mere ideals.
## Significance
These abstractions/ideals often serve as significant motivating forces for people’s real life actions.
## Example
- People often follow specific dogmatic beliefs/ideologies dominated by abstractions of ideas in the world.
	- This comes in the form of specific political ideologies, i.e., Marxism.
- This is also the case in some literary works, such as the Sound and the Fury, where the three Compson brothers are all obsessed with their sister Caddy, even though she has been “exiled” by the family.
