## Summary
Japan is able to sustain its economy through the central bank’s leveraging of the huge amount of savings in the country, whereby domestic savings are reinvested into higher-return, international assets.

Explain briefly how this idea fits into a bigger system, problem, or use-case. Make it meaningful to your future self.
## Explanation
The BOJ is able to enact their strategy due to the low interest rate in Japan, as well as the depreciated nature of the yen. 
However, if interest rates were to rise, or if the yen were to appreciate, this would likely lead to extremely, if any at all, margins. 
Ultimately, this system, though effective so far, puts the savings of ordinary Japanese in huge danger.
