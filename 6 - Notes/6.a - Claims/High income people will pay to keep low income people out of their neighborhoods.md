## Summary
In a neighborhood, higher-income people are willing to pay in order to keep lower-income people out. This around to roughly 550 euros, which is around 20% of the monthly rent in a region in France, where an empirical trial occurred.
## Significance
This illustrates how it may be difficult to actually create policy that ensures diverse neighborhoods, since people are very willing to spend more money in order to segregate themselves based on income.
