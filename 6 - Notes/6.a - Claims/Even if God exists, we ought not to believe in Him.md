## Summary
This is the argument that is made by Ivan Karamazov, essentially presenting the idea that because there is so much suffering in the world, the moral order that God is allowing is unacceptable.
## Significance
This is a main idea that Dostoevsky attempts to take down in his book.
## Explanation
In the novel, these ideas of Ivan are overridden by the philosophy of active love, and also by the fact that they are contradictory.
Since Ivan states that there are no laws in the universe, then by what standard is he judging the universe of God?
