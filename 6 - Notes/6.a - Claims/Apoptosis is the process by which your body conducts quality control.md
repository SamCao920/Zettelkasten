## Summary
Apoptosis is a process of controlled cell death, whereby cells that are mutated, or for some reasons was ordered to kill it self, dies. This ensures that all cells in the body are of high quality.
## Significance
This is an important biological process, too little of which leads to cancer, and too much of which leads to Alzheimer's.
