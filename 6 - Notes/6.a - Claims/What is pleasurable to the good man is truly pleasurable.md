## Summary
Aristotle allows that pleasures vary with people’s character, but he holds that the virtuous person is the measure of what is pleasant without qualification, so the pleasures that accompany virtuous activities are genuinely pleasant and therefore choice worthy.
## Significance
This explains that not all pleasures are good/desirable.
## Explanation
- There are certain pleasures, such as bodily ones, which may appeal to certain individuals.
- However, what is truly worthy striving for are the things that bring about pleasure based on the character of the virtuous person.
