## Summary
Due to the accessibility that AI provides, it will allow consumers to gain more information about the products that they purchase. This can help reduce the information asymmetry that currently exist in markets, allowing consumers to make more optimal choices in their purchase and avoid being taken advantage of.
## Significance
The article estimates that around a quarter of all consumer purchases are of products that suffer from extreme informational asymmetry, meaning that the ability to reduce or eliminate those asymmetries may be incredibly useful.
## Explanation
How AI could make markets more efficient depends on two factors:
1. People have to know how to correctly use AI, since that is the only way for its benefits to manifest.
2. How AI is used by retailers or suppliers, which could affect how much of an advantage the consumer gets.
