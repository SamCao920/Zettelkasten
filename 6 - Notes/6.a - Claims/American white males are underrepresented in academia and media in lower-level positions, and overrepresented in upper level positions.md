## Summary
Lower-level positions are increasingly being dominated by minorities and women, while upper-level positions remain dominated by white males, a trend that emerged around 2014. This was the result of established leaders protecting their own status while sacrificing the prospects of younger men to meet diversity quotas.
## Significance
As a result, many qualified professionals left their fields or remain stuck in lower-level positions.

> At [Berkeley](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LVwbrfB8qcm0SCwaLrBzlZhCcsnbT0VM/view?usp=sharing&ref=compactmag.com), as recently as 2015, white male hires were 52.7 percent of new tenure-track faculty; in 2023, they were 21.5 percent. UC [Irvine](https://ap.uci.edu/new-faculty-appointments/?ref=compactmag.com) has hired 64 tenure-track assistant professors in the humanities and social sciences since 2020. Just three (4.7 percent) are white men. Of the 59 Assistant Professors in Arts, Humanities and Social Science appointed at UC Santa Cruz between 2020-2024, only two were white men (3 percent).
