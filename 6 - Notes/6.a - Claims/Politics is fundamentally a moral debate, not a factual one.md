## Summary
Ultimately, politics is a debate over axiology and morals, not a debate over which object policy is the “best,” since any such judgements are based fundamentally on moral assumptions.
## Significance
This reframes the nature of politics from one of factual, impassionate debate over what is “objectively” the best policy into a debate about what we ought to value.
