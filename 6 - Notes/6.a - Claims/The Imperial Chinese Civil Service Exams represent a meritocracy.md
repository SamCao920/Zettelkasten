## Summary
The Imperial Chinese Civil Service Exam represents a meritocracy, as it allows people to achieve high ranking government positions through hard work, rather than simply through nepotism. This sets China apart from many other civilizations of the world in a similar time period.
## Significance
This explains the highly efficient Imperial Chinese state institutions, as the Civil Service Exams ensured that only the most qualified applicants are able to make it into government.
