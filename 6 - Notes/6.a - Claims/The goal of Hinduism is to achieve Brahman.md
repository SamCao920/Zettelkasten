## Summary
The overall goal of Hinduism is for its adherent to achieve Brahman, or the ultimate state of being.

## Significance
This is the overall goal of Hinduism, one of the world’s major religions.

## Explaination
- Moksha could be under taken in many different ways, depending on the person and their caste.
- The chief goal of humankind is to achieve union with Brahman, the fundamental reality that transcends the duality of existence and non-existence, through a process known as *moksha*.
- The process to achieving union with the Brahman is undertaken through multiple lifetimes, through a process known as *samara*. This is an example of how Reincarnation is an important aspect of many belief systems.
