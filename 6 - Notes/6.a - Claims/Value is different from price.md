## Summary
The idea that value is something that is different from price. Interestingly, this is both an idea in Austrian Economics, and in Marxist Economics, through the definition of value is different in both cases.
## Significance
This is a core concept in both Austrian and Marxist economics.
## Explanation
In Austrian Economics, value is subject and ordinal. Price is merely a reflection of the subjective value in terms of a currency.
In Marxist Economics, value is objective and defined as the Socially Necessary Labor Time, which is the “average labor required under normal conditions to produce something.” This value is only observable through price, the chaotic signal on the market that fluctuates around the true value (SNLT).
