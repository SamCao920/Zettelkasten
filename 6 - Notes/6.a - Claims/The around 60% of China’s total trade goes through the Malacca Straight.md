## Summary
Around 60% of China’s total trade and 80% of its crude oil is imported through the Malacca Straight. This means that for China to have strategic access to the straight is extremely important to ensure that they would not be easily isolated from trade if a rival controls the straight.
## Significance
This is crucial for understanding China’s actions in the South China Sea and how they are diversifying the source of their imports.
