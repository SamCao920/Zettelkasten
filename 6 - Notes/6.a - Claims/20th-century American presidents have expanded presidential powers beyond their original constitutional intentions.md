## Summary
The presidents of the 20th century have expanded the powers of the presidency beyond their previously utilized limits.
## Significance
Explain briefly how this idea fits into a bigger system, problem, or use-case. Make it meaningful to your future self.
## Explanation
The presidents have expanded the power of the presidency in the following manners:
- **Expanding war powers:** Truman bypassed Congress to fight the Korean War; LBJ and Nixon waged the Vietnam War without a formal congressional declaration of war.
- **Growth of the administrative state:** Known as “congressional delegation,” which essentially transfers legislative power to the executive. Roosevelt’s New Deal essentially gave agencies the powers to enact regulations, bypassing the legislative process entirely.
- **Executive orders:** Theodore Roosevelt pioneered the “Stewardship Theory,” whereby the president could do anything that was not explicitly forbidden by the Constitution. FDR and Trump are examples of presidents who expanded executive powers greatly.
