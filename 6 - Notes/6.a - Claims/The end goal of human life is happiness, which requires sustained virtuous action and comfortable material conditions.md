## Summary
Happiness is the final goal of a human life, so one’s efforts should be directed towards achieving happiness in life. And to achieve happiness as Aristotle defines it, we must live a complete life of virtuous action (goodness), and live with comfortable material means.
## Significance
This is the final goal of Aristotle’s ethical philosophy is to promote human happiness. This is what it attempts to achieve in his *Ethics*.
## Explanation
There are three crucial components to living a good life:
1. Sufficient material conditions.
2. Virtuous action of the soul (being a good human), enabled by (1).
3. A complete life (maintaining virtuous action through the entirety of one’s life).
Only with these three components, is one able to be happy.
