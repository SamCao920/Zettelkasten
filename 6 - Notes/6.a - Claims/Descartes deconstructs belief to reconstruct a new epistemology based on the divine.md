## Summary
Descartes’ famous saying of *cognito ergo sum* ("I think, therefore I am") seem to be at the end of an intense and highly skeptical process of deconstructing the accepted epistemology.  However, at the end of this process, Descartes constructs a new theory upon the old one, this time, based on the divine.
## Significance
This process of first deconstructing our epistemology, and then reconstructing it upon the divine is the core project of Descartes’ *Meditations on First Philosophy*.
