## Summary
Developing nations, when they emerge out of low-income status, may get trapped in the so-called “middle income trap,” unable to join the ranks of the high-income nations.
## Significance
This is a common issue for developing nations, as they can remain stuck in the middle income trap if they do not successfully escape the trap.
## Explanation
Nations can be stuck in the middle income trap for many reasons:
1. Lack of required technologies needed to advance to high-income status.
2. Lack of strong rule of law, means means that long-term growth is difficult.
3. Lack of human capital.
4. Reliance on commodities, which means the country relies on selling raw materials rather than developing a diversified economic base.
## Successes
Countries like the Four Asian Tigers were able to escape the trap due to their investments in education and high tech industries.
