## Summary
Both Columb’s Law and Newton’s Law of Gravitation describe similar phenomena, and are formulations of the Inverse Square Law (see The Inverse Square Law appears in many areas in science)

## Significance
Because of their similarity, methods for solving problems involving Newton’s Law can be analogized to solving problems involving Columb’s Law.

## A Crucial Difference
There is one crucial different between the application of Newton’s Law and Columb’s Law. There is a repulsive force in Columb’s Law, but there isn’t one in Newton’s Law.
