## Summary
The idea that determinism and free will are incompatible (i.e., they cannot both be true at the same time). Does not say whether determinism or free will is correct.
## Significance
This is the first step to proving either hard determinism, or libertarianism.
## Arguments
**The Consequence Argument**
- If determinism is true, then our actions are the necessary consequence of the laws of nature and events in the distant past.
- You cannot change the past, nor can you change the laws of nature.
- Therefore, you cannot change the consequences of them (your actions).
- Therefore, compatibilism is false.
