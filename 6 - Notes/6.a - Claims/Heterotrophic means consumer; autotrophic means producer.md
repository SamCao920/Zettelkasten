## Summary
Heterotrophic means an organism the consumes, and autotrophic means an organism that can produce is own food.
## Significance
Both types of cells act and work differently, with different purposes. For example, the mitochondria in a human cell is able to generate ATP at a higher rate for the cell, and thus, act as the “powerhouse of the cell.” In a plant cell, the chloroplasts are autotrophic, helping the plant conduct photosynthesis.
