## Summary
Justice as fairness only applies to institutions, and does not apply to individual dealings.
## Significance
This is a crucial caveat to the application of justice as fairness, namely, that it not cover the aspects of life that are not voluntary.
## Explanation
Justice as fairness is just a theory of the basic structure of society, and has a uniquely pervasive and non-voluntary influence on individuals’ life prospects. 
Additionally, applying this principle to individuals on every case would be too demanding.
