## Summary
The idea that intellectualism and rationalism can only be used to rationalize selfishness. The only way for love to occur is through impulsiveness and emotion.
## Significance
This is one of the most important ideas from *The Brothers Karamazov*.
## Explanation
In *TBK*, Ivan continues to rationalize, and he does so to such a degree that he is genuinely unable to love. 
- For instance, when he comes upon the freezing peasant the second time, in an impulsive decision, he carries the man to the police station and hopes for his recovery.
- He also originally at the desire to confess about his role in the murder of his father, and Dimitri’s innocence, but he rationalized this action away, saying that he will do it tomorrow (after the trial starts).
- However, when he does go in tomorrow, his account doesn’t make anymore sense, since if he were truly responsible, he would have confessed to it earlier.
Dimitri’s impulsiveness and baseness is eventually what allows him to love others.
