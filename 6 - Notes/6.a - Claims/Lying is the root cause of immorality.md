## Summary
The idea that lying, both to oneself and to others, is the source of immorality. 
## Significance
This is an idea that is present in various works of literature and philosophy.
## *The Brothers Karamzov*
Elder Zosima says that lying is the root cause of evil since it means that the liar is no longer able to tell the truth everywhere, and thus loses respect for all others, including himself.
- Perhaps the claim about not being able to tell the truth lies in the fact that the liar has the prescience to the fact that everyone is (could be) lying, so he no longer trusts anyone, including those who actually speak the truth.
	- Fyodor Pavlovich is the demonstration of this in practice. He has no respect for Elder Zossima.
## *East of Eden*
- Lying is bad because it undermines the ability to see/believe in the truth.
	- The lying to Aron about the nature of the mother, the lying to Adam and Charles by Cyrus, the lie of Cathy’s love to Adam (lying to himself).
- Ultimately, it leads to sin and bad outcomes.
