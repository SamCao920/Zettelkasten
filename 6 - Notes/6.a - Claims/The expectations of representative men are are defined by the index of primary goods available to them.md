## Summary
The expectations of some representative person is determined by the primary goods available to them. This is derived from the definition of the good as one’s achievement of one’s long-term rational plan, which requires primary goods.
## Significance
This allows us to pinpoint down what someone’s “expectations” means.
## Note
A few things to consider when constructing the index which makes the construction easier:
1. [Equal basic liberties]([[Each person has equal basic rights most extensively compatible with similar liberties for others]]) account for equality of rights and liberties.
2. Income and power are typically correlated.
3. Only the least advantaged group needs to be indexed at any time, as per the difference principle.
