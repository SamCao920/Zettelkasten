## Summary
While the convenience yield (the amount of interest rate that investors are willing to give up for less risk) for the dollar has remained relatively consistent even after the Great Recession, the convenience yield for treasuries has declined, especially with increased US debt.
## Significance
While in the past, the two have been tightly coupled as being both having high convenience yields, with the decoupling, and the drop of the convenience of treasuries, the world might need another, new form of government bond to replace US treasuries.
## Explanation
The convenience yield for treasures have declined due to increased borrowing by the US, which is drastically increasing the supply of treasuries in the global market. This means that the price of treasuries drops, and thus, their convenience yield, which is measured as the following, where $r_t​$ = government yield, $r^{ps}_t$​ = private sector safe rate:
$$
cy_{t}=r^{ps}_{t}-r_{t}
$$
