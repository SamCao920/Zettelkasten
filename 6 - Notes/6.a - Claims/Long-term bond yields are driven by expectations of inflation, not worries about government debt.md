## Summary
Long-term bond yields are driven primarily by expectations of long-term inflation, not worries about the government defaulting on its debt.
## Significance
This argues against the thesis that long-term bond yields are the result of people’s fears of government default.
## Explanation
Debt levels do matter somewhat for countries with credible central banks, though the main driver of long-term bond yields is still inflation.
