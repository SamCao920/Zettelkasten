## Summary
The idea that introducing bureaucracy, especially bureaucracy that places the ability to regulate housing construction on the local level promotes the construction of single family homes (for example, section 701 of the Urban Planning Assistance Program), since that is in the interests of the existing homeowners.
## Significance
This shows the ineffectiveness of past regulations that initially were established to ensure that housing would be more plentiful.
## Explanation
Section 701 of the Urban Planning Assistance Program introduced bureaucracy into the process of new housing construction. This meant a shift towards the new construction of less dense, single family homes, rather than higher density apartments. In this way, the government introduced the planning capacity to the local cities that allowed them to take charge of development, meaning that the existing homeowners were able to capitalize on their preferences and ensure that future construction would also be single family homes.
