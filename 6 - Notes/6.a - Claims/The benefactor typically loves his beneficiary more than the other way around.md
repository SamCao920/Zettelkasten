## Summary
Aristotle argues that benefactors love their beneficiary more than the other way around.
## Significance
This is used to explain why parents love their children more than the other way around, and lay down a rule about the relationship between benefactor and beneficiary.
## Explanation
This is because the maker typically loves his work more than his work loves him, since bringing into existence is a choice. 
- Additionally, a maker of something is defined by the thing that he makes. I.e., we exist through our activity.
- Craftsman and poets love their creation. In the same way, the benefactor loves the beneficiary.
- Giving is noble, whereas receiving is merely advantageous.
