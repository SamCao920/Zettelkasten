## Summary
The idea that the existence of evil in the world makes it unlikely that a perfectly loving God actually exists.
## Significance
One of the powerful arguments against the existence of God.
## Explanation
## In Literature
This argument is espoused by Ivan Karamazov from *The Brothers Karamazov*.
