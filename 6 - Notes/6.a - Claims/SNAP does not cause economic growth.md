## Summary
SNAP does not boost GDP as although it gives people more money to spend, this can, at best, lead to a temporary spike in GDP, but it cannot lead to sustained growth of the economy.
## Significance
This is a fallacious argument that many discuss regarding the efficacy of food stamps.
## Explanation
SNAP redistributes wealth from the relative more wealth to the less wealthy, and since poorer people have a higher MPC, this would naturally lead to a temporary boost in GDP. However, a dollar spent is a dollar not saved, and, without the savings that would otherwise have existed, long-term economic growth is stunted.
