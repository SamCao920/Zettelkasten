## Summary
Reductivism regarding person identity means that the self is merely a bundle of experience, accumulated over time. 
## Significance
This view of personal identity improves upon the egoist theory of the self in Parfit‘s view.
## Reductivism
This is Derek Parfit‘s view of personal identity.
- In this view, the self is a bundle of experiences, where the self is determined by psychological connectedness.
	- Psychological connection is continuity of experience. This psychological connection is the causal connection is the glue that binds everything together? 
		- Ex. The sight of a chair causes a belief in the existence of the chair.
	- Our life is a series of bundles of mental states at any moment (your beliefs, our desires, your thoughts, etc.)
Rather than claiming the existence of an egoist self, Parfit argues that the self is defined by the accumulation of experience in an individual over time.
- The ego view states that there is a special "me-ness" to someone.
- Time is not the best proxy for this, since there could be really radical change in a very short period of time.
Parfit argues that personal identity is not what we should care about from a self-interested perspective. All that matters is continuity of existence.
