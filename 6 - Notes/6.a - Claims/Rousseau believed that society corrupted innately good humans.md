## Summary
Rousseau had the idea that humans are innately good, but society, specifically bad societal institutions, corrupted them. 
## Significance
This connects with Rousseau’s other thoughts regarding moral and political philosophy, as well as his views on children and civilization.

## Explaination
Rousseau’s views here play into his views regarding moral and political philosophy, as he believed that change needs to occur at the societal level, not the individual level, which connects to his idea of the social contract. He also viewed children in a much more benevolent light, arguing that education must be imparted on young people to prevent the corrupting influence of society.
