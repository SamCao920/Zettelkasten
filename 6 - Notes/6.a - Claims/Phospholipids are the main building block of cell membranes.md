## Summary
Phospholipids are the main building block of cell membranes because they are amphipathic, with their hydrophilic heads facing outside a cell, and the hydrophobic side remaining on the inside.

## Significance
Phospholipids are an important part of AP Bio, and their purpose is important for cells to function.

## Note
Unlike triglycerides, phospholipids only have **two** fatty acid chains.
