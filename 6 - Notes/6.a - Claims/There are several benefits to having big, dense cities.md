## Significance
This represents an argument for why we should have big and dense cities.
## Explanation
1. People are more productive in big cities. 
	1. This can be seen through the fact that there is a noticeable wage premium when people come to work in big cities.
	2. This effect is especially apparent when the city has an intangible economy, where there is a high concentration of intellectual value.
2. Innovation increases when people congregate in big cities.
	1. Especially, there is an increase in innovation which required the combination of atypical combinations of knowledge.
	2. Inventors who move from smaller clusters to bigger clusters tend to see large increases in their patenting productivity.
3. By restricting housing, we are promoting inequality.
	1. Rising wages means that the poorer residents are priced out of their homes, and this leads to gentrification.
	2. By allowing, for instance, local residents to vote on rezoning to increase density, we can solve this problem and ensure that the local residents capture the benefit of development instead of having that development displace them.
	3. There is also regional inequality.
		1. Since it may be the case that for less well-paying jobs, it wouldn't be worth it for people to move to big cities where the rents are much higher compared to their wage increases, this means that people are just trapped in their original cities/states, increasing the disparity between states.
	4. High housing prices also reduce fertility.
		1. Women have less children than they say that they actually want. They often don't have children because it requires getting a bigger house, which they cannot afford.
