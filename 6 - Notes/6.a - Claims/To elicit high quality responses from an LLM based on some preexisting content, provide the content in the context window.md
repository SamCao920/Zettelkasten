## Summary
When you are attempting to elicit a response from a LLM that is based off some source material (e.g., you are asking it to summarize Shakespeare’s *Hamlet*), the model can produce a much higher quality response is the text is given in the context window.
## Significance
This helps one understand how to use an LLM in the most effective way.
