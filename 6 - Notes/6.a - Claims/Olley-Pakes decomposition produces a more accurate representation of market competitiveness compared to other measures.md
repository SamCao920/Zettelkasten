## Summary
Olley-Pakes decomposition, which measures whether consumers are switching to more productive firms, offers a clearer view into the efficiency of a market compared to measure such as HHI (which just looks at size).
## Significance
This offers a methodological improvement to the problem of ensuring efficient and productive markets. This is a methodological improvement because more market concentration doesn’t necessarily imply reduced innovation and stagnating productivity growth for firms. By looking at Olley-Pakes, we can gain a better understanding into if the concentration is due to the higher productivity of one firm compared to another, or something else.
## Note
The difficulty with this method is just that it requires a lot more data. And in order for this method to be effective, data needs to be collected constantly to ensure that things like monopolies temporarily cutting prices in order to remove competitors from the market is not happening.
