## Summary
Covalent bonds are the strongest bonds that exist. Therefore, they can only be broken through a catalysis process by enzymes.

## Significance
A major difference between covalent bonds and other bonds is that it is much stronger, about 3-8x as strong as the next strongest bond, the ionic bond. And since Covalent bonds are hard to form and hard to break, they only method of breaking is different from other bonds.

## Enzymes
Enzymes can break every type of bond
