## Summary
The idea that the more you know, the more powerful you are. I think that this is true in most circumstances.
## Significance
Knowledge is power is a phrase often cited today.

## Example
There are some circumstances where knowledge is indeed power. For example, in a conversation, the person that has more knowledge of the topic being discussed will naturally hold more power in the conversation, able to direct it as they wish.
