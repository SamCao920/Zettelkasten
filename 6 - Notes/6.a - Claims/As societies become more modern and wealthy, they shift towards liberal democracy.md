## Summary
Modernization theory states that as nations become more advanced economically, they shift towards adopting more elements of liberal democracy.
## Significance
This was a major theory of political development which fell out of favor in the 1970s due to its Eurocentrism and its deterministic nature. However, many of its assumptions and conclusions has influenced many different theories up until the modern day.
