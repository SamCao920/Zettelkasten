## Summary
An unrealized gains tax could force people to sell of assets just in order to pay their tax, and in cases where the asset is illiquid, this could lead to a distortion of the actual value of the asset, meaning that in the long run, investment may be weakened. Additionally, if an asset is overvalued, it may be overtaxed, and if undervalued, undertaxed. Having to valuate the asset also introduces transactions costs.
## Significance
This is one of the most significant arguments against an unrealized gains tax, as it would distort the financial markets and cause a decrease in investment.
