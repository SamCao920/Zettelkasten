## Summary
The use of paintings (specifically, different colors) can be used to gleam information about past economic growth.
## Significance
This provides a new way to understand (and provide greater granularity) into historical GDP fluctuations.
## Explanation
- Brighter indoors scenes and the use of more exotic and expensive colors track historical measurement of GDP currently available.
- But this new data increases the resolution of past data, giving us more up to date and precise information about changes in GDP.
- Dark/muted means stagnation or war, and blue/green/bright means growth and more light.
