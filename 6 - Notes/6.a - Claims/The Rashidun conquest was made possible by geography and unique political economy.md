## Summary
The conquests of the Rashidun were made possible by the fact that the geography of the area that they conquered was similar to the geography of the area in which they originated. Additionally, implemented a unique tax system that incentivized people to become Islamic, which both served to raise tax revenues and gain converts.
## Significance
This explains the rapid rise of the Rashidun state.
## Note
Some other factors that allowed for their rapid conquest include the weakness of the surrounding states, the Byzantines and Sassanids having recently fought the “Last Great War of Antiquity.”
