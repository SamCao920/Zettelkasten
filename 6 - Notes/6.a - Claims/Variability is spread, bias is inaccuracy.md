## Summary
There is a distinction between the concepts of variability and bias (analogous to accuracy and precision).
## Significance
This is one of the most important distinctions to make when it comes to the sciences, as while similar, the two terms refer to distinct concepts entirely.
## The Distinction
Variability (or precision) refers to the *spread* of some dataset, i.e., how tightly grouped the datum are.
![[Pasted image 20250819205554.png]]
Accuracy (or bias) refers to how far away the average datum is from the true value, i.e., how close to the true value the average of all of the datum is.
![[Pasted image 20250819205726.png]]
## Connection with Statistics
Understanding this concepts helps one to understand the phrase:

> [!quote] Ms. Le
>  Blocking reduces variability, and randomization reduces bias.
