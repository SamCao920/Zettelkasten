## Summary
Quantitative easing is a far more radical form of open market operations, as it involves the large scale purchase of long-term government treasuries. This is as opposed to open market operations, which sees the purchase of short-erm government treasures.
## Significance
The two are different, since while the first is used commonly to make large adjustments to macroeconomy, usually in the aftermath of a major recessions, the latter is used commonly to make small changes to the money supply.
