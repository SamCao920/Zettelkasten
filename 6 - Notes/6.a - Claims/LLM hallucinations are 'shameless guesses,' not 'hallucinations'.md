## Summary
The idea that LLM "hallucinations" are not actually hallucinations in the way that we think about a hallucination. Rather, it is just a guess the LLM made.
## Significance
This means that we have to rethink how we conceptualize mistakes that AI make. This has implications for how we think about the nature of AI agents as being bullshit machines.
## Explanation
- The common perception of “hallucinations” is that is a form of *sui generis* mistake that AIs make which is entirely foreign to humans.
- Another way to view this, however, is to see hallucinations as probably guesses that the AI makes because its utility function does not take into account “shame” that someone might feel if they made a wild guess.
- This lack of shame is due to the nature of the training process, where the AI is rewarded for correct guesses, but it is not punished for wrong ones.
