## Summary
The difference principle is fundamentally an expression of the idea that the natural talents that we are born with (natural contingencies) ought to be shared in society, such that they are used to achieve the greatest common good, which means aiding the worst-off.
## Significance
This highlights a difference between the difference principle and the principle of redress. While the latter focuses on desert, the former focuses more on achieving the optimizing the expectations of the least advantaged.
