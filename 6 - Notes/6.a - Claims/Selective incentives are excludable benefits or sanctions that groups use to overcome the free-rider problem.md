## Summary
Selective incentives are benefits given out on an individual/household level as to incentivize people to contribute to some cause, or as to discourage people from not participating.
## Significance
This explains the existence and strength of many different political interest groups. Additionally, it solves the free-rider problem in large groups.
## Examples
- Labor unions: While all workers benefit from the wage negotiations carried out by labor unions, those who join the union get additional benefits, such as union-only legal support or strike pay.
- Professional associations: While such organizations create public good as they enforce stronger profession-wide regulations, members are the only ones that are able to benefit from their networking events and get access to the services that the association offers at a discounted rate.
