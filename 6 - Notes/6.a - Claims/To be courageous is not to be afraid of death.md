## Summary
Aristotle claims that someone who is truly courageous is someone who is not afraid of death. Such a person will only fear “what is natural for men to fear” but that they will face such a fear in the “right way” and with principle.
## Significance
This is important, since courage is a main moral characteristic that Aristotle analyzes.
## Note
Courage implies the presence of pain, and thus should be praised since it is more difficult to endure pain than abstain from pleasure.
