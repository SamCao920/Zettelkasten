## Summary
Aristotle distinguishes between “goodwill” and “friendship,” where the former occurs when we feel some merit and good towards someone, but the other person is not aware of these feelings and these feelings are largely superficial.
## Significance
It is important to distinguish between friendship and good will since many of the things that apply to friendship do not apply to goodwill.
