## Summary
The second wave of democracy began around 1945 and ended around the early 1960s.

## Significance
This period saw many major democracies established as a result of American efforts to rebuild the West, and British decolonization.

## Second Wave
The second wave of democracy began after the end of WWII, when much of Western Europe was re-democratized and decolonization began, leading to the formation of new democracies. This came to halt by the 1960s, as democratic norms began to dissolve, partly due to the rivalry created by the Cold War leading to both sides prioritizing strategic alliances over democratic ideals.
