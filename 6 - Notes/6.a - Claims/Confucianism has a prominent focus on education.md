## Summary
An important facet of Confucianism is its focus on the importance of education, reflected in the Civil Service exams (see The Imperial Chinese Civil Service Exams represent a meritocracy).

## Significance
Confucianism’s focus on education allowed for Chinese state institutions to be operated effectively, and for its civilization to produce a variety of advancements.
