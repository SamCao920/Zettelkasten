## Summary
Financial momentum is when due to a change in prices, further prices change occur. This is considered an anomaly in the efficient market hypothesis. This is also empirical evidence that financial momentum does have some relation with the winner of some bet. 
## Significance
The fact that financial moment actually as has prediction power when it comes to prediction markets mean that prediction market are not perfect examples of the efficient-market hypothesis. And also, this has practical significance.
## Empirical Evidence
From the bar graphs above, it is evident that financial moment does have a statistically significant impact on the outcome of a prediction market bet.
