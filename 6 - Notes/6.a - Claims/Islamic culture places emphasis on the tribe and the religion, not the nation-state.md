## Summary
Unlike the West, which places its emphasis on loyalty to the nation-state, Islamic cultures have a much heavier emphasis on loyalty on the clan level, and beyond the nation-state level, regarding the *ummah*.
## Significance
This explains the lack of inter-state conflict in the Islamic world. This also means that the West’s focus on the nation-state level may be misguided.
## Explanation
Western civilizations, when viewing their loyalty, can be seen to place the largest emphasis to loyalty on a specific nation-state, not one’s immediate community or the large religious community that one is part of. Islamic civilizations on the other hand, emphasize exactly the opposite.
Of note is that while the *ummah* explicitly presupposes the illegitimacy of the nation-state, it would only be able to be united through the actions of such a state. Otherwise, there is no political unity.
