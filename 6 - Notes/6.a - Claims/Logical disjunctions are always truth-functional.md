## Summary
In standard logic, disjunctions are always truth-functional, meaning they are always extensional.
## Significance
This means that extensionality/intensionality is not something that needs to be considered when using/reading a disjunction.
## Note
There are select circumstances under which logical disjunctions are **not** truth functional. For example, in the statement “hand over the money, or I will shoot” is an example of when logical disjunctions are indeed truth functional.
