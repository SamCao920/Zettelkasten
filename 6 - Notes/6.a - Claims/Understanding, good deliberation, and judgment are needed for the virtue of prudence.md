## Summary
- Understanding (*synesis*) is the ability to judge the statements or decisions of others critically.
- Good Deliberation (*euboulia*) is the ability to reason well and to a good goal.
- Judgement (*gnome*) is the ability to see with “fairness,” when particular things in the world are unjust. 
## Significance
These minor intellectual virtues are required in order for someone to be prudent.
## Note
Cleverness, or resourcefulness (*deinotes*) is a neutral faculty that describes the ability of an individual to reason well. It then depends on if they reason to some good (or bad) end that this becomes good deliberation.
- If cleverness is used to do bad, it becomes unscrupulousness.
- If cleverness is used to do good, it becomes good deliberation (and thus prudence).
