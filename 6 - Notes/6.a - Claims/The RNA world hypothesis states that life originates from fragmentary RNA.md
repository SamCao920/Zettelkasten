## Summary
A theory for the origin of life, namely, that life originated from fragmentary pieces of RNA.
## Significance
There are currently no accepted theories for the origins of life, and this is one of the proposed theories.
## Evidence
- RNA is able to store genetic information, though it is more fragile than DNA. 
- RNA can form ribozymes that can catalyze reactions before the existence of protein enzymes.
- Many coenzymes that exist today are similar to RNA, and may be descended from them. 
- Ribosomes, one of the most critical parts required for life function, is primarily constructed from RNA.
