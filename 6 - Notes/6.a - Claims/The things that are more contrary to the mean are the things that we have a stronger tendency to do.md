## Summary
Typically, the things that are more contrary to the mean are the things that we have a stronger tendency to do, since they are usually more pleasurable.
## Significance
Aristotle makes an important point that the things that we most want to do are often things that bring us farther away from the mean.
## Aristotle’s Guide
Aristotle says that to find the mean:
1. Keep away from the side (in the spectrum between the two extremes of some action) that is more contrary to the mean.
2. Notice our own errors and do the opposite.
3. Guard against pleasure.
But he says that getting to the mean is ultimately up to the individual, and that there is no general philosophical principle that can guide someone to the mean in their life. It just our own intuition that has to do the work.
