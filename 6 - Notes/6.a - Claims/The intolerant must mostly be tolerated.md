## Summary
The idea that the intolerant must mostly be tolerated, except in cases where the intolerance threatens free institutions and basic liberties. 
## Significance
This presents one of the main positions on the topic of whether to allow intolerant people in society.
## Explanation
Unless the intolerant people are overtly and actively threatening the free institutions of a society or the basic liberties of the citizens in that society, their rights ought to be protected just like any other person.
Additionally, the intolerant have nothing to complain about, since they do not acknowledge the very principles of liberty that would protect them.
