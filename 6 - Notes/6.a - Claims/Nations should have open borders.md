## Summary
The idea, typically associated with libertarian schools of thought, that borders should be open for any one to across and move to a different country.
## Significance
There are a series of economic and political arguments to be made as to why this should be the case, and this is a cogent issue given contemporary events.
## Bryan Caplan’s Arguments
- Although immigrants (to the US) are typically less pro-market and pro-liberty, there is still a case to be made for open borders.
- Open borders are an extremely important component of the free market and human liberty, since the labor market is 70% of the economy. 
	- The gain of having more laborers outweighs the negatives of having more immigrants who are not pro-market or pro-liberty.
- The median immigrant is not a political radical, and, combined with the fact that immigrants typically have lower-turnout and status quo bias can dilute the political effect of immigrants.
- The very fact that there will be more immigrants will reduce native support for welfare state, and immigrants will be favorable to liberty in one crucial respect in that they will favor more open borders.
