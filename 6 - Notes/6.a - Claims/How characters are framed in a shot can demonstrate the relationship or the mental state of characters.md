## Summary
How characters are positioned within shots, especially as it relates to how they are framed together with other characters, gives an insight into the relationship between the characters. Additionally, the way they are shot can also give us an indication of the mental state of the character.
## Significance
This is cinematic technique used by films such as *The Third Man* or *Fargo*.
## *Fargo*
![[Pasted image 20260216181719.png]]
In the film, Margie is always filmed in the same frame as norm, indicating their close relationship. Additionally, when Mike moves closer to her during their conversation, she explicitly asks him to move to the other side of the table, moving them out of the same frame.
![[Pasted image 20260216181923.png]]
![[Pasted image 20260216181936.png]]
Marge is always portrayed in the interview scene with Jerry as seen over his shoulder, showing her connection with others and her connection with the world at large. On the other hand, Jerry is shown in a claustrophobic manner where the camera gets in his face and there is nothing to locate him in a space (it is merely a blank wall behind him).
