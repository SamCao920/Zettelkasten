## Summary
Scientific knowledge (*episteme*) deals with necessary/eternal truths and uses logic (deductive reasoning, syllogisms, etc.) in order to get from the first principles to the conclusion. 
This virtue is part of the scientific soul.
## Significance
This is an important virtue that makes up one of the parts of wisdom.
