## Summary
Just like how The Romans learned from the people they conquered , the Mongols did the same thing, adopting the advanced siege machinery invented by other civilizations and tolerating their ideas regarding civic society and religion.

## Significance
Their tolerance and adaptation is part of the reason that they were so successful in their conquests.
