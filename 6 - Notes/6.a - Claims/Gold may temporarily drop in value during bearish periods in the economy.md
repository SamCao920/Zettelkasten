## Summary
At the beginning of or quickly after period of bearish activity in the market, there is typically a sell-off of gold, followed by a rise in its prices soon after.
## Significance
This explains a pattern that has been observed regarding the price of gold.
## Explanation
- Investors like gold because it tends to gain value during period of inflation and it is relatively stable even when there are massive disruptions to the market in general.
- Many investors also keep their collateral when they buy on the margin in the form of gold (for the aforementioned reasons). This means that when the market is generally falling, gold may temporary see a drop a value as investors sell off this gold to use as collateral.
