## Summary
Non-truth-functional connectives form compound statements whose truth is dependent on the particular relationship between the atomic sentences, rather than just their surface-level logical connection. 
## Significance
This means that when a sentence contains a non-truth-functional connective, they cannot simply be symbolized as-is.
