## Summary
Xi seeks to enact a “self-revolution” (自我革命), essentially an attempt to redefine the party with a revolutionary zeal and to ensure that it does not become stagnant and corrupt. This is the reason for his anti-corruption campaigns.
## Significance
This explains the fundamental ideological justification for Xi’s anti-corruption campaigns and lays out his grand vision for the party.
