## Summary
Inefficiency in taxes are a means by which the people resist against the encroachment of state power. The people want to be taxed less, so they put up less resistance to taxes that are more inefficient, and since they put up resistance to taxes that are more efficient, the government chooses not to tax those, and instead tax the easier-to-tax but more inefficient options.
## Significance
This explains the persistence of inefficient taxes in the economy.
