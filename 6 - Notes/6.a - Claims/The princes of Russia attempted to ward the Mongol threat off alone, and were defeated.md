## Summary
Because the princes of Russia refused to cooperate each other, they were individually easily defeated by the invading Mongols.

## Significance
This illustrates one of the major advantages that the Mongols had over their enemies. The Mongols were an united, overwhelming force, while their enemies were often weak and divided.
