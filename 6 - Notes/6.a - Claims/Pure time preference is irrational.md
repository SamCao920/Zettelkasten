## Summary
The idea of pure time preference, or that we value something less merely because it is in the future (not taking account things such as the increased risk of the future or other factors like that) is irrational.
## Significance
This is important for specific argument in population ethics.
## Explanation
It seems that we could value all period of our life the same. There is no reason to do otherwise.
This also implies that we should not discount future civilizations, and thus we should not over consume now and doom future generations.
- But it seems that many of the disregarded factors do play a role here. For instance, it seems likely that the longer in the future, the likelier that humanity will go extinct. Therefore, it seems makes sense to discount.
