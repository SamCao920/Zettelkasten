## Summary
Rentier states, which are states that rely primarily on income from natural resources, tend to have high inequality and low political participation.
## Significance
This explains crucial properties that rentier states typically have, which allow us to analyze such states with the appropriate context.
## Explanation
- Since the resource wealth of natural resources tend to be concentrated (there are high fixed costs to extracting natural resources), the wealth tends to flow towards only the upper class.
- Since there is a lot of wealth coming through with the natural resources, the state does not have a reason to raise taxes. This decreases the incentives for people to participate politically, since taxation is typically a very politically salient issue.
