## Summary
The collapse of the USSR left a lasting mark on the institutions of Russia, since elements of statism and corruption continued in the country. This is continuing to have an adverse effect on the country, politically and economically, today.
## Significance
This continues to effect the functioning of the Russian state, and explains, perhaps partly, why the USSR fell back to into autocracy soon after leaving it.
