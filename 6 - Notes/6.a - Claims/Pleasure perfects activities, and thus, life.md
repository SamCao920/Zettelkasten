## Summary
Pleasure accompanies and completes and activity when a well-functioning faculty, perceptual or intellectual, is active towards its appropriate object.
## Significance
This describes the value/meaning of pleasure.
## Explanation
Pleasure perfects an activity, making the activity better and more fully realized than it would otherwise be.  Thus, pleasure perfects life for the individual, since it perfects activities, and life is an activity.
*When we feel pleasure:*
- We don’t constantly feel pleasure due to fatigue. 
- Or, something, we only felt pleasure at something for its novelty, which, after it wears off, means that we no longer feel the same pleasure.
*How pleasure can change:*
- Different activities produce different pleasures, since they involve different actions.
	- Pleasure intensifies their respective activities, increasing enjoyment and allowing us to improve at it.
