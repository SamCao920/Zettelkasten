## Summary
The Luck Argument argues that indeterminism (i.e., that actions are not pre-determined) means that actions are the result of randomness. If that is the case, control over one’s actions is still nonexistent. Therefore, indeterminism does not actually support the case for Libertarianism.
## Significance
This is an important critique of indeterminism.
