## Summary
The cause of the 1997 Asian Financial Crisis was that the Thai government was not able to continue their peg of the *Thai baht* to the US dollar due to a lack of foreign reserves, meaning that their currency collapsed, leading to capital flight and panic.

## Significance
The 1997 Asian Financial Crisis, though short-lived, lead to major regime changes across South and East Asia.
