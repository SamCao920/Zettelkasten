## Summary
The Absurd most directly manifests through cleavages between two diametrically opposed concepts, which both must, to the human mind, exist in some capacity.
## Significance
This is the nature of the Absurd.
## Explanation
Some examples of such cleavages include:
- The mind & nature vs. Unity.
- The eternal vs. The finite.
- “Concern” vs. The ultimate vanity of one’s actions.
