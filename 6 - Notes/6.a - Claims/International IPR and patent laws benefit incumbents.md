## Summary
Due to the high cost of running a patent office and ensuring that all inventions are account for, this may result in a drag on the economies of developing nations (the ones that don’t yet have technology). As a result, this system only serves to benefit countries that are already hubs of innovation.
## Significance
This fits into Chang’s general argument regarding the weakening of existing patent and IPR laws.

## Quote
> [!quote] Martin Wolf, *Why Globalization Works*, pg. 217
> [Patents and similar forms of protection] is a rent-extraction device for [developing nations], with potentially devastating consequences for their ability to educate their people (because of copyright), adapt deigns for their own use (ditto) and deal with severe challenges of public health.
