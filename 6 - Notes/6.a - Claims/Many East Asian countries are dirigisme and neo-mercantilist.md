## Summary
Countries such as China, Japan, and India are dirigisme and neo-mercantilist in that they adopt capitalist methods of production, but many aspects of the economy are controlled by the state or are directly influenced by the state.
## Significance
This adds nuance to the usual characterization of East Asian countries as either capitalist or socialist, or some other vague term.
## Examples
### Japan
- Business cooperated deeply with the government:
	- Many of the biggest international Japanese companies were subsidized by the government.
		- Mainly automobile, technology/manufacturing, and steel.
	- Many were government monopolies, though encouraged (through low tariffs) by the government to trade internationally.
- High savings rates in the country:
	- 40% of savings rate; promoted low interest rates and growth of infrastructure.
	- Foreign savings depreciated the yen as well.
		- Promoted the success of Japanese businesses.
- Deep investments in education (human capital):
	- Made the country technologically sophisticated.
- Great political stability.
	- Predictable future means that investment is encouraged (perceived lower risk).
