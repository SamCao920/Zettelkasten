## Summary
Baumol’s Cost Disease, which is when high productivity sectors force a rise in wages of lower productivity sectors, can slow economic growth.
## Significance
This is one of the main factors that when new innovations appear which can galvanize the economy, they impact is often muted, as costs are rising across the board.
## Effects
For example, labor will shift from highly progressive (high increases in labor productivity) sectors to stagnant sectors (low increases in labor productivity).
![[Pasted image 20251107110430.png]]
The Baumol Effect has a large effect on sectors such as the performance arts, healthcare, or education, where there isn’t a huge amount is innovation/little efficiency gains.
It seems that the effect of this wage equalization would over power the depressed wages that would otherwise result from the influx of labor into low-productivity sectors, since the overall effect is one of wage increase (despite the increased labor leaving high-productivity sectors for low-productivity sectors.)
- I suppose this is an empirical question, since the reason that low productivity sectors would raise wages is due to the fact that workers would otherwise leave for sectors with higher wages, but since there is an influx of new workers, it seems that this effect would be weakened substantially, if not reversed.
