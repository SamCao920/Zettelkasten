## Summary
Justice is considered by Aristotle to be the “sovereign virtue,” since it encompasses all of the virtues that he has described, and it regards the way that one interacts with others under the law.
## Significance
This is Aristotle’s definition of universal/general justice.
## Explanation
Justice the process of aiming at the mean state, while injustice is aiming at the extremes.
