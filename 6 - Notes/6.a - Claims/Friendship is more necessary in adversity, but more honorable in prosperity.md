## Summary
Regarding when we should make friends, Aristotle says that it is more important that we have friends when encountering adversity, since grief is lighted by friends. At the same time, it is more honorable to have friends when you are prospering.
## Significance
Aristotle outlines the duty of friends here.
## Explanation
- We should try not to be open about our grief with our friends, since that would cause them pain. However, we should share our moments of triumph with friends, so that we can experience that together.
- That being said, we can call our friends to aid when in times of need, and when it least disrupts them. We should aid our friends when we notice them suffering.
