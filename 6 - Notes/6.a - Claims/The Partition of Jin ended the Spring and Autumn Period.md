## Summary
The Spring and Autumn period was ended with the partition of the Jin, as the three dukes that ruled under the Jin gained enough power as to establish kingdoms for themselves.
## Significance
This triggered the beginning of the Warring States period, during which there was large scale warfare, as states began to more openly fight against each other.
