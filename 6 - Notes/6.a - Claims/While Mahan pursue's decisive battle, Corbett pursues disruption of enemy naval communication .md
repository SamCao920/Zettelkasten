## Summary
While Mahan pursues the elimination of the enemy fleet through decisive battle, thus establishing absolute naval control, Corbett argues for the disruption of enemy supply and communication lines without decisive battle in an attempt to establish relative naval control.
## Significance
This contrasts the difference between Mahanian and Corbettian naval strategies, as while one focuses on complete and absolute control, the other focuses on disrupting the ability of the enemy to wage war more indirectly.
