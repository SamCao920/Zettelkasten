## Summary
The close ties between the military and the defense industry manufacturers means that the defense industry manufacturers are able to shape policy, perhaps in such as way as to turn against the interest of the public.
## Significance
The military-industrial complex may have a substantial impact on public policy due to the extensive influence of the defense industry on the government and the close ties between the two.

## Explaination
- There is a fragmented monopsony when it comes to the military-industrial complex, as internal divisions allow for defense firms to lobby and influence procurement, which may create demand beyond pure strategic need.
- It also persists due to rent-seeking and regional job incentives, which makes then economically sticky.
- There are long-term contracts, which create a lock-in effect that make it difficult for the government to switch suppliers.
- There is a revolving door, as former military officials and policy makers often join defense firms as consultants or executives.
