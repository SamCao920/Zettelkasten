### Summary
Democracy and constitutional liberalism are not the same thing, and can be unlinked from each other. 
### Significance
Understanding this helps us better analyze the situation present in many nations, where there may be the lack of one or another, but often, we may just say that the country lacks “democracy.”
## The Difference
Democracy refers to the process by which leaders are election, namely, through a free and open election. While this would entail some necessary freedoms, such as freedom of the press and association, it does not require a lot.
Constitutional Liberalism refers to the existence of legal-political institutions that are “constitutional” and “liberal.” This would include things like the protection of property rights, the enshrinment of certain political rights such as speech, gun ownership, or religion, or *habeus corpus* and the rule of law.
## The Conclusion
This supports another argument: Western government is defined by constitutional liberalism, not democracy.
