## Summary
Standard Medicare has a fee-for-service system, meaning that physicians are compensated for the quantity of service that they provide, not necessarily the quality of the service that they provide. 
## Significance
This means that the incentives of doctors can be distorted, causing them to provide services that are not necessarily needed, and thus, drive up the cost of Medicare. This may in part explain my American healthcare is so expensive.
