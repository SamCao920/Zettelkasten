## Summary
The Gold Standard ended because the US spent too much, and other countries began noticing how the US would not be able to be actually exchange all of the cash they have printed for actual gold. This caused a “bank run” on the US for gold, and to stop this, Nixon ended the gold standard.
## Significance
This is the major moment that ended the post-World War II economy and the Bretton Woods system.
