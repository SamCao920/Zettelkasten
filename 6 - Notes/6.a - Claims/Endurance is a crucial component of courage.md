## Summary
Endurance is the ability to withstand pain (whereas continence is the ability to resist the temptation of pleasure). It is closely related to courage, since to the courage, one must first be enduring.
## Significance
This explains what endurance is, and what it is important for.
## Softness & Insensitivity
The deficient state of endurance is softness, or an oversensitivity to pain. Such a person flees all hardship, and does not stand their ground. 
The excessive state of endurance is insensitivity. Such a person experiences pain for no reason.
