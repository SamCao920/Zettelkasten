## Summary
Natural virtue is one’s potential to become virtuous and it is innate, unreasoned, and incomplete. In order for natural virtue to become true virtue, prudence is needed.
Virtue cannot be achieved without at least some innate inclination towards virtue, i.e., natural virtue.
## Significance
Highlights the importance of prudence to acting morally, since it is prudence that elevates natural virtues to true virtues.
