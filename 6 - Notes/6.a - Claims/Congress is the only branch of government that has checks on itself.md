## Summary
Congress is the only branch of government that has “intrabranch checks,” meaning that its power is checked by its internal parts. This comes in the form of the upper and lower houses, which may disagree about something and thus put a check on the power of Congress as a whole.
## Significance
The intrabranch checks on power are important, as they ensure that Congress does not get too power, especially if the other branches of government are ever severely weakened.
