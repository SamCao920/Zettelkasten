## Summary
Aristotle describes two types of justice: particular and general. Particular justice is concerned with fairness, and general justice with lawfulness.
## Significance
This is the broadest division in Aristotle’s taxonomy of justice.
## Note
Particular justice can be further divided into:
- Distributive justice: achieving a fair/equitable distribution of things according to one’s relative merit.
- Rectifictory justice: returning two parties to their state before a transaction occurred.
