## Summary
A large reason for the increase in Chinese firms’ global market shares is due to the large amounts of subsidies that they get from the government, which accounts for around 10% of a firm’s annual revenue. This is compared to the 2% OCED average.
## Significance
This explains the large amount of market share which Chinese companies occupy in many industrial sectors.
## Explanation
Much of the subsidies were given in the form of “soft loans,” or below-market rate loans from the government, and which are hard to trace.
Additionally, this strategy does not increase the productivity of the Chinese firms, but simply makes them unfair competitors compared to other OECD firms.
