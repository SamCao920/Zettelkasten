## Summary
A drop in productivity growth is often hugely detrimental to overall economic growth, due to the immense impact that productivity plays in economic growth.
## Significance
Productivity growth is a huge component in overall economic growth, so a weakening of productivity growth may indicates weak economic growth, even in the long term.
## Explanation
Suppose rGDP (index = 100) and trend productivity growth is 3%/yr. If productivity slows to 1.5%/yr for 4 years, then recovers to 3%:
- Old rGDP path after 4 years: $100×1.034≈112.55100\times1.03^4 \approx 112.55100×1.034≈112.55$.
- With slowdown: $100×1.0154≈106.14100\times1.015^4 \approx 106.14100×1.0154≈106.14$.
RGDP is ~6% below the pre-slowdown path and stays there unless productivity growth runs above 3% later to catch up.
