## Summary
Justice as fairness fosters self-respect since the public institutions affirms the sense that one’s rational, long-term plan in life is worth carrying out. Also, this leads us to respect others, which in turn means others respect us. This eventually leads to more societal harmony and efficient social cooperation.
## Significance
This is a part of Rawls’ argument for justice as fairness.
