## Summary
Instant-runoff voting (alternative voting) is a plurality voting system where the candidates are ranked. It solves the spoiler effect since people rank the party that they favor most as number one, and do not strategically vote by choosing the party that they dislike the least.
## Significance
This explains a significant advantage that instant-runoff voting has over standard SMP voting.
## Note
This being said, instant-runoff voting still shares many problems in common with SMP:
- Cannot prevent gerrymandering.
- Is not proportional.
- Does not elect the Condorcet Winner.
- Both also trend towards two parties
