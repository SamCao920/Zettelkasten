## Summary
The third wave of democracy began around 1974 and ended around 1991.

## Significance
This defines the end of the Cold War and saw many of the formerly Communist nations democratize.
## Third Wave
The third wave began with the Carnation Revolution in Portugal, and spread to many areas around the world, including the Four Asian Tigers and Eastern Europe following the Collapse of the Soviet Union.
