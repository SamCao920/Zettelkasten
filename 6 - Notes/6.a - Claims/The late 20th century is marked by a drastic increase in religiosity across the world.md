## Summary
The late 20th century saw an uptick in the amount of religious belief and the strength of such belief around the world.
## Significance
This is significant to Huntington’s thesis that conflict will more and more be driven by differences in religion/culture.
## Explanation
The increase in religiosity around the world is the result of urbanization/modernization making previously established belief systems inadequate and at the same time, people are not willing to accept moral relativism.
Additionally, the end of competing ideologies at the end of the Cold War likewise left people directionless.
