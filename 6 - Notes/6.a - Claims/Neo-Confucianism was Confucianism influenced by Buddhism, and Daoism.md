## Summary
Neo-Confucianism (宋明理学) was an attempt to create a more rationalist version of Confucianism, free from the influence of Daoism and Buddhism. At the same time, it incorporated many elements of Daoist and Buddhist vocabulary.
## Significance
Neo-Confucianism’s development in the Tang and Song dynasty was an attempt to rid Confucianism of its Daoist and Buddhist roots, and at the same time, revive and reinvigorate an old tradition.
