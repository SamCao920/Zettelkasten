## Summary
When the voltage-gated sodium ion channels open, the sodium outside the cell (which is positive and existing in the higher concentration gradient), floods into the cell, causing depolarization. Through this process, they passively diffuse around the point of entry, causing nearby areas to reach the threshold potential, thereby causing depolarization.
## Significance
This is the crucial process by which a signal is transported down a neuron.
