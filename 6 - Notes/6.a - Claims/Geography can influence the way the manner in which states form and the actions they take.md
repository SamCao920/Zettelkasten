## Summary
Geography can play an important role in determining the way that states can come to form, as different geographic factors can lead to different challenges for civilizations, whether directly as a result of the geography, or due to related causes.
## Significance
This is significant as geography has influenced the way that many civilizations have formed, and the acknowledgement of geography’s influence can better help us analyze the reasons for specific reasons that a civilizations developed in a specific way.
