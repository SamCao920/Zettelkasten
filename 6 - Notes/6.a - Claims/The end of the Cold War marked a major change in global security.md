## Summary
The end of the cold war produced many different effect, a major one of which is the decline of the military power of the West and associated problems.
## Significance
This leaves room for other powers, specifically in East Asia and the Middle East, to rise to regional prominence.
## Explanation
Specifically, Huntington outlines five observations:
1. The collapse of Russian military capabilities.
2. Decline of Western military power in general (decreased spending, etc.)
3. Increase in East Asian spending on military.
4. Military capabilities are becoming more eventually distributed across borders.
5. Security is increasingly being regionalized, such that future conflicts will be largely regional, not directly between superpowers.
