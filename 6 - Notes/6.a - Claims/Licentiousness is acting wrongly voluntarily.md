## Summary
Licentiousness (the embodiment of vice) is acting wrongly, but fully conscious and choosing to act in this way. This is due to the licentious person’s understanding of universal morality being corrupted.
## Significance
This helps us understand why some people act wrongly and contrasts this moral state with other moral states.
