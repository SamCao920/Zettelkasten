## Summary
Since the industrial revolution, inventions have appeared at a rate that is closer to the theoretical maximum.
## Significance
It is unclear whether this is causation or merely correlation, but it is nonetheless important to note the increase in the rate of innovation with the Industrial Revolution.
## Explanation
![[Pasted image 20260521103003.png | center | 500]]
It seems likelier that the Industrial Revolution was the *cause* of the shortening time between the theoretical earliest possible time of invention and the actual time of invention than that there was merely a correlation. This is due to the fact that the industrialization created many new tools and materials that were able to make tools, leading both the ability to mass produce an invention and in case also the ability to create the invention in the first place.
