## Summary
Dirigisme describes a state that manages internal economic affairs, whereas Neomercantilism describes a state that maintains always a current account surplus through government intervention.
## Significance
The terms are similar in that both describe a state’s control of the economy, but one is specifically referring the management of the economy as an internal measure, and the other describes the management of an economy as the management of some external feature.
