## Summary
Every action has an end goal, that is, it’s final cause. There are things which are means to an end, and there are also things that are an end in and of themselves.
## Significance
This is the fundamental premise that underlies Aristotle’s arguments in Book I of the *Ethics*, since if all things have an end goal, it must be the case that humans have one too.
## Explanation
It seems that that things which is an end in and of itself is happiness, or manifestations of happiness is some fashion.
It seems that the fact of “all things having an end goal” can only be established through an inductive argument, whereby all things that we observe have an end goal, so, everything must have an end goal.
