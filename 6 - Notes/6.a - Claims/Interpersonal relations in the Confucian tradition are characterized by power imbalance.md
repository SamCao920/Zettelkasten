## Summary
The “Five Relationships” that Confucianism followed are examples of religiously entrenched power imbalances designed to “preserve harmony.”

## Significance
This could explain the vast disparities in power that exist in modern China, by tracing the normalization of such power disparities to a keystone in Chinese culture.

## Explaination
- Society consists primarily of unequal relationship; i.e., father and son, husband and wife, ruler and subject. At the same time, positions of power could be achieved meritocracy.
	- This provided justification for inequality in society, but also set the expectation that those in power would yield the power justly and care for those below them.
- By maintaining these relationships, harmony would prevail.
