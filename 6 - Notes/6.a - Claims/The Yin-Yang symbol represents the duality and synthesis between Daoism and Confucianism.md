## Summary
The Ying-Yang symbol represents duality and synthesis between Daoism and Confucianism.

## Significance
This explains the presence of both ideas in Chinese culture, despite their conflicting nature.

## Explaination
- While these beliefs are in contrast to Confucianism, they often acted together, in a synthesis, represented by the Yin Yang symbol.
