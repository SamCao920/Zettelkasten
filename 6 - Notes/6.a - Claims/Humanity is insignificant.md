## Summary
The idea that humanity is insignificant in the vastness of existence. This is a core belief in philosophical schools such as the existentialists, or in writers such as [].
## Significance
This is a common idea in many existentialist schools of philosophy and works of visual art and literature. 
## Examples
- In the plays of Sophocles, such as Oedipus the King, Oedipus at Colonus, or Antigone, humanity is always portrayed as entirely subservient to the gods, as they are essentially bent to the will of the gods in their every action. 
	- In fact, the entire plot of Oedipus, first of him as king, and later, when he was at Colonus, was essentially dictated by the gods.
		- If Oedipus does not find out about the truth of his family, the city will continue to suffer from the plague. He is forced, then, to find out the truth.
