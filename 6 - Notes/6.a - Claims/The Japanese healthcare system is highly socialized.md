## Summary
In Japan, the healthcare system is highly socialized, as people are forced to purchase healthcare by the government. Additionally, the government set all prices for medical procedures. At the same time, around 50% of Japanese hospitals were in debt.
## Significance
Japan’s healthcare system was radically different from many different countries, as they managed to merge a public and private system. At the same time, many of their hospitals were hugely in debt.
