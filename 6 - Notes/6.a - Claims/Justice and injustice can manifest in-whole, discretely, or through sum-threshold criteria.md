## Summary
(In)justice can manifest through various forms, including 
- in its total form at once, 
- in a discrete form in a particular rule in an institution or in an institution among many institutions, or 
- apparently non-existently, though in sum, produces a visible effect.
## Significance
This explains the various forms that justice could take.
