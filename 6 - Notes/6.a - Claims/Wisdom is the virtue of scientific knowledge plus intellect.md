## Summary
Wisdom (*sophia*) is the combination of the virtues of scientific knowledge and intellect. A person with wisdom both understands the first principles, and the necessary truths that follow from those first principles. 
It is directed at the most divine and superior objects in the universe (e.g., the gods, the cosmos, etc.), rather than human affairs. It allows the individual to search for truth, and it not aimed at increasing utility for the individual.
## Significance
Wisdom is the highest of all of the intellectual virtues (even above prudence), and thus, it is the most desirable.
