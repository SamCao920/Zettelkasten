## Summary
The Kelly Criterion, which gives the percent of one’s wealth that you ought to bet with the probabilities of the outcomes, helps the better maximize their bet.
## Significance
This is something that has applications across investing and gambling.
## Explanation
For use in gambling, the equation is$$
f* = p-\frac{1-p}{b}
$$ where $f*$ is the fraction of wealth to bet, $p$ is the probability of a win, and $b$ is the proportion of the bet gained with a win (i.e., winnings-to-bet ratio).

In investment, it adopts the form$$
f*=\frac{p}{l}-\frac{1-p}{g}
$$ where $p$ is the probability that the security rises in value, $g$ is the the fraction that is gained in a positive outcome (i.e., the profit-to-principle ratio), and $l$ is the opposite.
