## Summary
Conflicts between nations are often made worse or more intense due to civilizational/cultural differences between the belligerents.
## Significance
This may explain why cross-cultural conflicts often result in greater violence and destruction.
## Examples
During the wars after the dissolution of Yugoslavia, there was brutal fault line conflicts between the various new nations that formed, including mass genocides of people of different cultures.
- The war in Yugoslavia is an example of a complex fault line war, where there is an intricate network of supporting parties on both sides of the conflict that do not actually fight.
