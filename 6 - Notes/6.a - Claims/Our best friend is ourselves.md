## Summary
One’s best friend is themselves, meaning that one loves oneself.
## Significance
Aristotle’s idea of self-love is an important aspect of his philosophy that is rather rare.
## Explanation
- A virtuous man is able to be virtuous to others, so he must be able to be virtuous to himself.
- Importantly, the “self-love” described here is not referring to a type of immoral self-love whereby we attempt to secure for ourselves the maximum amount of money, or honor, or bodily pleasure. 
	- This is referring acting virtuously towards oneself.
	- This also means that a bad man cannot have self-love in the true sense, and the bad man hates himself.
- Since we are our own best friend, it means that we also love ourselves (virtuously) most.
