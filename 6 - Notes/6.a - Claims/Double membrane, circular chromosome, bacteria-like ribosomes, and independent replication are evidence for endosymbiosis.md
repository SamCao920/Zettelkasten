## Summary
If an organelle has a double membrane, circular chromosome (like a bacteria), bacteria-like ribosomes, and independent replication through binary fission, they are most likely an organelle that that was endocytosed.
## Significance
These four pieces of evidence can indicate whether an organelle was originally independent and became an organelle after being endocytosed.
