## Summary
The idea that fair equality of opportunity has to be fully satisfied before any consideration of the difference principle.
## Significance
This is important in understanding the way that the second principle of justice is to be implemented.
## Explanation
This rules out actions such as undermining individuals’ rights to have the ability to hold any position that they would be qualified through the justification that such a case would not be able to benefit the worst-off.
