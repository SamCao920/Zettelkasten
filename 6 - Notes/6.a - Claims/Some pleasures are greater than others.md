## Summary
Some pleasures are greater/better than others, which can be proven from the fact that, when faced with a choice between things that both bring pleasure, can actually make a choice.
## Significance
This is the core of Mill’s tiered utilitarian system where some pleasures are inherently greater than others.
## Explanation
Mill claims that the way in which you decide which pleasures are through collective consensus. If its the case that most or all of those who have experienced the pleasures being compared prefer one to the other (irrespective of any moral obligation to prefer one or the other), then the preferred one is the “greater” pleasure.
