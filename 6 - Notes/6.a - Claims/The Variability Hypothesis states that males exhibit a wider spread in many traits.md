## Summary
The Variability Hypothesis essentially states that males have a greater range in terms of their trait exhibition (ex. IQ, physical traits, personality, spatial reasoning) compared to females. This may be due to how makes are the heterogametic sex ($XY$ chromosomes).
## Significance
This has been used to explain many differences between the two sexes, though the theory is disputed.
