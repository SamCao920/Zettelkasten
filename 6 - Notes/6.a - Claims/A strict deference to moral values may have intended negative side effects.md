## Summary
The idea that strictly following some moral code has downsides.
## Significance
This is a common theme in many works of literature.
## Examples
- Examples include Oedipus, Hamlet, and Frankenstein.
- A negative example of this would be Odysseus: during his journey back to his homeland after the Trojan war, he repeatedly lies, and as a result, he is able to make it back home safe and sound.
