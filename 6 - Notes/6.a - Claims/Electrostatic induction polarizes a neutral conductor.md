## Summary
When a charged item is brought next to a neutral conductor, the conductor is polarized (i.e., the side that is closer to the item accumulates opposite charges.) Such a polarized conductor is said to have an *induced charge*.

## Significance
Electrostatic induction will be on the AP test.

## Connections with Chemistry
- The polarization caused by electrostatic induction is akin to a polar molecule
