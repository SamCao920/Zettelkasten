## Summary
Intellect or intuition (*nous*) deals with the ability to grasp first principles, or the “extremes” that deductive reasoning cannot reach. It operates in two distinct directions: 
- Theoretical *nous* grasps unchangeable first principles (axioms) through induction. 
- Practical *nous* grasps the immediate, variable facts of a specific situation.
## Significance
Intellect also constitute a part of the virtue of wisdom. It is also the foundation upon which scientific knowledge is able to build.
