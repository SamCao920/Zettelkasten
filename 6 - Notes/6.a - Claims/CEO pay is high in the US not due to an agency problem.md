## Summary
Many argue that CEO pay is high in the US due to agency problems whereby the CEO pays themselves a lot at the expense of the company. However, this cannot be true since there is no significant difference between the two, and there is no agency problem in the case of the PE-operated firm.
## Significance
This diffuses the common argument that American CEOs are paying themselves too much.
