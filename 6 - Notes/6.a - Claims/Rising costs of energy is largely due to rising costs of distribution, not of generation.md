## Summary
Rising energy costs is largely due to rising cost in energy distribution, which is caused by the increased costs of replacing old electrical infrastructure and having to repair existing infrastructure. Power generation has actually decreased in price.
## Significance
This clears up a common misconception that rising energy costs is due to increased costs of power generation, rather than the real issue of power distribution.
