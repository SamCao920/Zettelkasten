## Summary
For states that surround a rising hegemon, they can choose between two options:
- Bandwagon, or
- Balance.
## Significance
This provides a framework for thinking about how states surrounding rising powers can respond to the rising power.
## Explanation
If states bandwagon, it means that they join with the rising power. This demands that surrounding states have trust in the rising power that it does not simply consume the surrounding states.
- This is more likely within civilizations, though the likelihood of this happening still depends on the specific civilization.
If states balance, they will do so through the formation of alliances that oppose the rising power (primary balancers).
Huntington argues that with the rise of China, states are likely to be the former and not the latter, while the norm has been the latter in European history.
