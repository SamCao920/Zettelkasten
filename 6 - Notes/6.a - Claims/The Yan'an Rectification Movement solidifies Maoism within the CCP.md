## Summary
The Yan'an Rectification Movement, carried out during the Second Sino-Japanese war, solidified Mao’s position in the CCP, and maintained ideological unity in the party. It marked a split with Soviet Marxist thought at the time, and established a cult of personality for Mao.
## Significance
This is when Mao’s cult of personality was established, and when the CCP officially had an ideological split with the USSR.
