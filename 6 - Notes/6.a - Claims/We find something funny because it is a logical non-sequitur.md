## Summary
The mainstream accepted theory of humor, which states that we find something funny because what actually occurs represents an incongruence with our perceptions of what ought to occur, or simply what we thought would occur.
## Significance
This represents the most widely accepted theory of humor today.
## Example
A comedian tells a joke that includes a pun that changes the entire meaning of the sentence to something that is dark or otherwise unexpected.
