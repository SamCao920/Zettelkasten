## Summary
The principle of careers open to talents does not do enough to rectify the preexistent inequalities in the world.
## Significance
Rawls uses this suggest the necessity of a difference principle, namely the principle of fair equality of opportunity.
## Explanation
Rawls argues that careers open to talents does not mitigate either social contingencies or natural contingencies.
Therefore, we should not adopt it.
