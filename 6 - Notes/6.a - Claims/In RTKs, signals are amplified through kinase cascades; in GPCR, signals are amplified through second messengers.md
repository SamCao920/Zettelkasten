## Summary
In Receptor Tyrosine Kinases, signals are amplified through kinase cascades, which the receptor itself triggers (by phosphorylating a kinase itself). In G-Protein-Coupled Receptors, an enzyme like adenylyl cyclase creates many cAMP (second messenger), which amplifies the signal.
## Significance
The two different signal transduction pathways have different means of amplifying the signal, which is something that I often forget.
