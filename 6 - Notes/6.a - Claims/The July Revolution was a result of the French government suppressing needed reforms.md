## Summary
The July Revolution of 1830 was a revolution against Charles X, due to his poor management. This was due in large to two changes carried out by Charles, namely the [Anti-Sacrilege Act](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Anti-Sacrilege_Act) and the provision of indemnities for those whose properties were seized during the First French Revolution, in addition to overall poor administration by Charles. 
## Significance
This example showcases the important of ensure that adequate and correct reforms are passed, because otherwise, the people may just rebel.
