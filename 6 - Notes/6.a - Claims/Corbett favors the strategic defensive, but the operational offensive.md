## Summary
As a part of his belief in limited warfare, Julian Corbett argued for militaries to adopt a strategically defensive, but operationally offensive approach towards engaging the enemy as to shift the cost-benefit calculus of the enemy until it is more cost efficient for them to surrender.
## Significance
This is one of the core concepts of Corbettian strategy.

## Explaination
This does not mean that a military should adopt such a stance, **always**.
During the opening stages of the war, it may be beneficial to go in the strategic offensive and engage the enemy while they are still unprepared. After establishing control in enemy, the military may then switch to a strategically defensive posture to slowly weaken the enemy.
