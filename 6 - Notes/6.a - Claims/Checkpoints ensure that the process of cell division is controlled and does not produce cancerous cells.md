## Summary
There are checkpoints that are located throughout the cell division process, whereby the process cannot continue unless the requirements for the checkpoint (undamaged DNA being a common checkpoint requirement) is ensured to be met.
## Significance
This ensures that cells do not develop cancerously, or the duplicated cell does not immediately apoptosis.
