## Summary
An electric field is an area around a charged object such that a force would be exerted were there to be other charged objects in the region.
## Significance
This is relevant for AP Physics.

## Mathematical Representation
$$
E = \frac{k_{e}Q}{r^2} \ \frac{Newtons}{Coulumbs}
$$
In other words, it is:
$$
E = \frac{F_{e}}{q_{2}}
$$
