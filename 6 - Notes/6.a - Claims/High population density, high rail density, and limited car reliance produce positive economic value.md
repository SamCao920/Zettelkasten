## Summary
Three factors allow for train networks to have positive economic value:
1. High urban density,
2. High rail density (contributing to network effects),
3. Limited car reliance.
## Significance
This demonstrates the principles that can make a rail network successful, and can to applied in analyzing different rail networks.
