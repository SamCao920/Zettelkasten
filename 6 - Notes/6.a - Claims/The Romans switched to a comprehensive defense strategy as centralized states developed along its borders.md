## Summary
With the development of powerful tribal confederations along the Northern border, the arrival of central Asian nomadic groups, and powerful Eastern states, Rome had to utilize a comprehensive defensive strategy rather than the client state system as they had done before.
## Significance
This shows the evolution of Rome’s defense system with the passing of time, as the prior system of client systems stopped working with the development of centralized states along Rome’s borders.
