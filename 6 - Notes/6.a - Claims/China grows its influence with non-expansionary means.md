## Summary
China has historically been a non-expansionary power, since in its view, it was the only true power around, and there was no civilization that could truly mount a threat against it. This trend is expected to continue being present in Chinese foreign policy.
## Significance
This theory could be used to predict the actions of the PRC.
## Explanation
Though it will not expand militarily, it will instead want to ensure that the nations around it
- Support its territorial integrity (with regards to Tibet, Taiwan, etc.).
- To generate support for Chinese foreign policy.
- To remain aligned with Chinese economic goals.
- To support Chinese culture and Chinese people.
