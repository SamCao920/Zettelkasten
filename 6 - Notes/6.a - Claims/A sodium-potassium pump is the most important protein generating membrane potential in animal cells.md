## Summary
The Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase or sodium-potassium pump is the most important protein that generates membrane potential in animal cells, as it moves 3Na⁺ out, while moving 2K⁺ in, generating an overall negative charge in the cell.
## Significance
This is relevant for the movement of molecules across the cell membrane.
