## Summary
As incomes rise, although the absolute amount spent on food rises, proportionally, the amount spent on food decreases.
## Significance
This means that spending on food is a much larger share of low-income people’s total spending, even though it may be, absolutely, a smaller amount than wealthier people’s food spendings. This also means that the share of a population’s spending on food can be used as a measure of the affluence of the population.
