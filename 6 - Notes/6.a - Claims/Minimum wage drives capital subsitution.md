## Summary
A minimum wage drives the substitution of labor for capital, since it increases the relative marginal product of capital increases with higher marginal product of labor.
## Significance
This explains a downside (for the worker) of minimum wage.
## Explanation
- A 10% increase in the minimum wage is equivalent to an 8% increase in robot adoption.
- Large manufacturing firms are the drivers of the substitution.
	- This is because manufacturing is relatively easier to automate.
	- And this is because large firms have more capital to invest in automation.
- This is a trend seen in the US, China, Turkey, and Germany; it is likely true everywhere.
- A conclusion that can be drawn from this is that labor policy shapes technological diffusion.
