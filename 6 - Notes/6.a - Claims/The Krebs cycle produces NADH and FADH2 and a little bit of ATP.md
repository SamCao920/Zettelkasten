## Summary
The Krebs cycle produces NADH and FADH2, and a little bit of ATP using two Acetal-CoA, which is produced per glucose molecule. As a byproduct, CO2 is produced.
## Significance
This is the second step in cellular respiration, as the pyruvate is used to generate NADH and FADH2, which is then sent to the ETC to power the proton pumping process.
