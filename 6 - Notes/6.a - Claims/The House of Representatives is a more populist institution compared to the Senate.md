## Summary
The House of Representative is a more populist institution since its members are more numerous and are elected from smaller districts. This means that they more directly represent the interests of their constituents. 
The Senate, on the other hand, represents larger populations, and lacks many of the rules that constrain the House, most notably the ability to end filibustering. This is since the Senate is considered a more deliberative body. 
## Significance
This explains the difference between the two chambers of the US Congress.
