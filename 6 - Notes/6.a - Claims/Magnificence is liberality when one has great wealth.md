## Summary
Magnificence is liberality when one has great wealth. 
## Significance
This is interesting because it is in contrast with our putative morality today, which makes morality egalitarian. On the other hand, Aristotle claims that this virtue is not achievable by all.
## Note
The excessive vice of magnificence is vulgarity, meaning spending too much in a tasteless fashion.
The deficient vice of magnificence is pettiness, meaning spending too little, and when spending, spending in a tasteless fashion.
Since it requires having great wealth to be magnificent, this is a virtue restricted to those who have the means to acquire great wealth.
