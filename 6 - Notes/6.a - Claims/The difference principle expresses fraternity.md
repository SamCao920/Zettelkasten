## Summary
The difference principle expresses a sense of fraternity among the people who participate in it, which strengthens the social glue that holds everybody together.
## Significance
This is part of the advantage that the difference principle offers.
## Explanation
Since the principle expresses a certain sense of concern for the less well off, similar to the way the concern is expressed in the family (where fraternity is most present), it can be said to generalize fraternity to the societal level.
