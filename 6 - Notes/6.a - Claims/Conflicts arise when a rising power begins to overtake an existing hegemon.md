## Summary
Also known as the Thucydides Trap, when rising powers begin to overtake an established power, the two powers often come into conflict.
## Significance
This can be used to explain/predict when conflicts occur.
## Explanation
This effect was first observed by Thucydides, and was noted in his *History of the Peloponnesian War*, as when Athens began to grow powerful and threaten the dominance of Sparta, the two nations engaged in the Peloponnesian War.
Such conflicts naturally tend to be core state conflicts.
