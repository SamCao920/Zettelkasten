## Summary
Given my God to Adam in the Garden of Eden, God says how people ought to live in a community, and not just isolate themselves.
## Significance
This is a commandment that God gives.
## Bible
- This is a key insight into what it means to be human, since it is NOT GOOD that man is alone. 
	- This is a contrast with the “and it was good” form the previous verses.
- Man is set up as a mediator between the earthly and the heavenly, answerable to both God and other humans.
- A person’s identity is defined in terms of his relationship with others. 
	- Adam is only able to name himself “ish” after he names Even “isha”
## *The Brothers Karamazov*
This commandment relates to the idea that Dostoevsky explicates in *TBK*, where he essentially advocates for Christian Socialism and existentializes the process of confession into something earthly, specifically, public/group confession.
He also states explicitly that isolation from others (especially as a result of sin/guilt) is the existentialized version of hell.
