## Summary
Daoism argues for self-sufficiency and withdrawal from worldly affairs.

## Significance
This is one of the core principles of Daoism, and is crucial for understanding its impact on Chinese culture.

## Explaination
- One should strive for simplicity in their living, abandon education and self-improvement, and be self-sustaining. Additionally, one should withdraw from worldly affairs.
- While Daoists do want to make the world better, it does not seek to do so through direct action, as that would lead to artificiality and the prevalence of self-interest as a motivating factor.
- Argues for "non-action," or 无为.
