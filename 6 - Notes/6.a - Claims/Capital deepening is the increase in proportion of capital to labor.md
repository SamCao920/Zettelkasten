## Summary
An increase in the ratio of capital to labor (more capital for the same amount of labor); increase in labor productivity. This can also be referred to as an increase in capital intensity. A necessary but not sufficient factor in economic growth. 
## Significance
Capital deepening is important in studying economic growth, as capital deepening is usually seen as important to economic growth.

## Explaination
Depending on the growth model, capital deepening can indefinitely lead to economic growth, or lose it potency due to diminishing returns.
- In the Solow model, the effect of capital deepening will gradually become more muted due to diminishing returns and depreciation.
- In the AK model or some endogenous models, capital deepening can lead to indefinite growth, even without technological progress.
