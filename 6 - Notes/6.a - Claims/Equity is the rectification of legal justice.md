## Summary
Equity is defined as the rectification of legal justice, meaning the application of the generality of laws to specific circumstances.
## Significance
Equity is needed to ensure that laws functions smoothly and are able to be used even when circumstances may differ slightly from the one specified in a code of law.
## Note
Equity is a kind of justice, and it is better than legal justice (since it rectifies the flaws existent in legal justice), though not as good as absolute justice.
