## Summary
Aristotle claims that the best case is that the state, since its laws have compulsory force, should shape the virtues of individual citizens. But if this is not possible, then family or friends should attempt to shape virtue.
## Significance
This explains the nature of how virtue ought to be instilled in the individual.
## Note
Creation of virtue by family or friends could actually be good in a certain respect, in that they are more circumstantial/individual to the person that it is directed to, which could have a better effect than the blanket law of a state.
