## Summary
Many of the legal and economics frameworks setup in the European Union allow for it to be used in a Libertarian manner.
## Significance
Currently, the framework is being used to push a left-wing agenda, even though it has the potential for a Libertarian system to be propagated.
## Explanation
- There are laws that ensure an open market and free trade between countries in the EU.
- There are many laws in the EU that ensure individual protections.
- There are many laws in the EU that allow for deregulation to occur.
