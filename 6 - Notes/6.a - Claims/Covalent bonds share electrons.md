## Summary
Covalent bonds are bonds where two molecules share electrons. An example of a covalent bond is a peptide bond in a polypeptide chain.

## Significance
Biology relies on covalent and ionic bonds, not metallic bonds, and thus covalent bonds are hugely relevant for AP Bio.
## Covalent Bonds
Where the bonded atoms share an electron.
	- *Disulfide bridge*: the only type of covalent bond formed between R-groups. Formed between two molecules ending in -SH. Disulfide bridges are the most stable r-group interactions.
A covalent bond is formed in order for atoms to reach their desired number of electrons. For most atoms, this number is 8.
