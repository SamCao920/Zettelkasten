## Summary
As the values that define our lives begin to fade during the Enlightenment, people sought new ways to define meaning in life. One form of this was the emergence of romantic satisfaction as a means of finding meaning in life.
## Significance
This explains the important that our society places upon romantic affection, since it is a means by which people find meaning and satisfaction in their existence.

> [!QUOTE] Anthony Giddens, *The Transformation of Intimacy*
> The other, by being who he or she is, answers a lack… And this lack is directly to do with self-identity: in some sense, the flawed individual is made whole.
