## Summary
Known as the “Brussels Effect,” due to the geographic influence of the EU, its regulations and laws often spread outside its border. A prominent example of this is the GDPR, as many nations outside the EU have adopted its regulations in order to operate within the EU.
## Significance
This shows the influence that European regulation can have, but also, that the negative effect that come from European regulation could spread around the world.
