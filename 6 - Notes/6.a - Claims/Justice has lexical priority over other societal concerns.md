## Summary
Justice has lexical priority over other societal concerns, such as coordination, efficiency, stability, etc. 
## Significance
This shows the importance of the idea of justice in Rawls' philosophy.
## Explanation
> [!QUOTE] Rawls
> "laws and institutions no matter how efficient and well-arranged must be reformed or abolished if they are unjust."
