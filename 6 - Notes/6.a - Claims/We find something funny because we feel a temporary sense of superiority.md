## Summary
One of the foremost theories of humor states that we find something to be funny because we feel a temporary sense of superiority over the humorous thing.
## Significance
This can be used to explain why we find something funny.
## Example
When a comedian makes a joke about how disable people are not able to ambulate, the audience feels a temporary sense of superiority which makes them laugh.
