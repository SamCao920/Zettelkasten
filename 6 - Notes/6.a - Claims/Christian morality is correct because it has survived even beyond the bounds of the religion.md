## Summary
The idea that Christian morality is at some fundamental level, the closest to the a universal moral system, since it has so widely been adapted everywhere.
## Significance
This is used by some to argue for Christian morality.
## Explanation
In fact, many social movements that are avowedly Atheistic nonetheless adopt the morality associated with Christianity.
## Note
Couldn’t this just because of the large geopolitical influence that Europe has exerted across the world in the past, and not necessarily a result of the inherent appealingness of the moral system?
