## Summary
Proponents of Georgism argue that thee land value tax is the best form of tax since it only taxes what is not the result of human investment and labor. 
## Significance
This is a core component of schools of thought like Georgism or Geolibertarianism, who argue that all other forms of tax are misdirected and unequal.
## Explanation
Proponents argue that taxing land is effective since it is not the product of human labor, and merely a part of nature, which they consider to be owned by no one/all of us. 
Additionally, since a tax would ensure that land does not go to waste, as people simply sit on their land doing nothing as the value of land slowly increases. It incentivizes people to make the best use of their land and make sure that it is productive enough to offset the taxes on the land itself. Otherwise, it will be sold to someone who can better make use of the land.
However, this is premised on the idea that land is somehow communally owned, or that it cannot be the result of private property. Such a view emphasizes the fact that people should be not subject to any taxation, including land, since it violates the property rights of the landowner (this is mostly an Austrian view).
