## Summary
It is ineffective to place a minimum wage on gig workers, since they supply is so elastic that it can adjust easily to any such practice.
## Significance
This offers a critique of why minimum wages should not be implemented for gig workers.
## Example
For instance, is a minimum wage is implemented for Uber drivers, because there are so many people with a car, the supply of driver would simply increase and there would be less work for each Uber driver, essentially keeping their overall net hourly wage the same.
When the experiment was done in Seattle, the following was noted:
- Consumers tipped less.
- Each full time diver experienced a -26% change in the number of monthly tasks, due to the decreased demand (from high prices) and more drivers.
- All in all, the net change in the amount earned by the drivers was 0.
