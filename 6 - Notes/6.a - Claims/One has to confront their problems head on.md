## Summary
It is important to confront problems head on.
## Significance
This is an idea that is put forth often in books regarding ethics, or what one ought to do.
## In [[Essays (book)]]
 *To consider and estimate the danger is in a way the reverse of being haunted by it.*
 Montaigne urges us to, instead of shying away from a danger, to confront it directly. And so, to deeply consider and estimate the danger is the best way to ensure that you are not affected by it.
