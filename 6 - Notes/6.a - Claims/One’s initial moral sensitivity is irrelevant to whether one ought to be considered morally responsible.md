## Summary
One’s initial moral outlook is irrelevant to how one should be assigned moral responsibility.
## Significance
This is a point of clarification that Aristotle makes when he defends against the objection that some people are just naturally less able to be moral than others.
## Explanation
Aristotle argues that if we somehow absolve some people of moral responsibility since they are less able to perform moral actions due to some innate factor, it would be like saying that people who do act virtuously are somehow just innately predisposed to act in the same way and thus should not be praised. This is clearly false.
