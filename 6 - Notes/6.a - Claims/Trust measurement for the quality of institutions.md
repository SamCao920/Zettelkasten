## Summary
The amount of trust in a society is a proxy measure which one can use to determine the effectiveness of the society’s institutions. The lower the trust, the lower the quality of institutions.
## Significance
This explains the important of institutions in a society, since they are the foundations for the establishment of trust, and important factor in the way that societies function. 
## Note
It seems to me a little of hard to have a causality measure in this case. Is it low trust that creates bad institutions, or is it bad institutions that lead to low trust? Or is it both at the same time?
