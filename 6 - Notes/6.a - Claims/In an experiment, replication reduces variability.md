## Summary
By repeating an experiment (or increasing the sample size), you reduce variability introduced by random chance.
## Significance
Replicating an experiment repeatedly allows for data irregularities to be reduced, and thus produces more accurate results.
## Explanation
In order for an experiment to actually prove something, it must be account for random chance. Therefore, it must be conducted multiple times in order to prove that there was no confounding factor at play which distorted the result of the experiment.
