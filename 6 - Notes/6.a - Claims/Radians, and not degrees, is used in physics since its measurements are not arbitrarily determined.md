## Summary
A radian represents an angle when the arc of the circle is exactly equal to the radius of the circle. Because of this relation, they directly relate arc length to radius, rather than being based off some arbitrary degree system that is meaningless.
## Significance
The radian system is used in calculus and physics heavily.
