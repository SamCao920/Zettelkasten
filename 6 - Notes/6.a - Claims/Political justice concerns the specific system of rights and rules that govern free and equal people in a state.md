## Summary
Political justice is the justice that can exist in the presence of 
- people who share a common life aimed at self-sufficiency,
- people who are free,
- people who are equal, proportionally or arithmetically.
It is distinguished by law, i.e., what obeys the law is just, and what does not is unjust.
## Significance
Political justice addresses the question of what justice means in a political community.
## Explanation
There are two types of political justice:
- Legal justice: arises out of the particular circumstances of the time, which produced unique laws which one must obey.
- Natural justice: arises naturally; the intuitive morality for justice in each individual.
Domestic justice is similar to political justice, since it also specifically regards a community of people. However, the household is hierarchical, with a basis of authority and necessity, not law and reason. Therefore, it is not relevant in a discussion of justice.
