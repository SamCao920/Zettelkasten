## Summary
When new nucleotides are added to a DNA or RNA, it is added to the 3’ end of the chain of nucleotides. Thus, DNA or RNA is always read from the 5’ to the 3’ end.
## Significance
This information might appear on an AP Bio test. This is also analogous to how New amino acids are added to the C-terminus on a polypeptide chain.
