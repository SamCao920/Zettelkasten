## Summary
When a friendship occurs between two people of different status (father/son, elder/younger, ruler/subject, etc.), the affection, respect, and service exchanged between the two ought to to be proportional to the status differential between the two.
## Significance
This is important to understand friendships between two people with different statuses.
## Note
In such a friendship, the one who benefitted financially or morally should give the other person honor in return, since that is the only way for the lesser to repay the greater.
