## Summary
For regulators, taxes are generally better tools than bans, since they make markets more efficient and increase welfare.
## Significance
This argues against the use of bans, which are common in policy.
## Explanation
If regulators want to ensure that the price of a product does not rise too high, they don’t have to simply ban firms from raising their taxes above a certain threshold. Rather a more sensible strategy would be to instead place a progressive tax on the revenue of firms that rise about the threshold. This has several advantages:
- This eliminates inefficient firms that have high marginal costs.
- It preserves access for a good, instead of destroying it as in the case of bans.
