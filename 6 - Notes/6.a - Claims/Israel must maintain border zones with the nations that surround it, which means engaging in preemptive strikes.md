## Summary
Due to the attacks of October 7, Israel has adopted a strategy of engaging in preemptive strikes against enemies to ensure that no other state is able to develop weapons of mass destruction.
## Significance
Regarding the current approach of Israel to foreign policy.
## Explanation
- Along with this, they have adopted a strategy, when entering a conflict with a foreign power, of eliminating their power structure first and additionally to significantly degrade their economic, politic, and military capacities to establish deterrence.
