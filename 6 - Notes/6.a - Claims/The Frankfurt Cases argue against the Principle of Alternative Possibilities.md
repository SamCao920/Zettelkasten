## Summary
The Frankfurt cases argue against the Principle of Alternative Possibilities (PAP).
## Significance
The PAP is a core tenet of libertarian schools of thought. If it is invalidated, then the libertarian school of thought will be damaged.
## Argument
- **The Counterexample:** Frankfurt describes a scenario where a neurosurgeon implants a chip in your brain. If you try to vote for Party A, the chip forces you to vote for Party B. If you choose to vote for Party B on your own, the chip does nothing.
- **The Result:** You voted for Party B on your own. You are responsible. Yet, because of the chip, you _could not have done otherwise_. This suggests freedom depends on the _source_ of the action, not the options available.
