## Summary
The idea of time preference in economics. This note explores various theories of time preference.
## Significance
Time preference is one of the most important concepts in economics, since it explains things like interest rates and people consumption behavior.
## Theories
Some theories to explain the existence of time preference includes:
1. (Böhm-Bawerk) Current goods are able to provide both for current consumption and future consumption, while future goods are only able to provide for future consumption.
2. (Böhm-Bawerk) People have a lack of will, an imaginative mind, or are uncertain of the future, and thus would prefer to consume today than in the future.
3. (Böhm-Bawerk) Receiving a consumption good today means freeing up resources for investment in capital goods, which provides long term benefit. Receiving a consumption good in the future does not allow this.
