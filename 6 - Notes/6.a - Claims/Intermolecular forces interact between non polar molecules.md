### Summary
Some of the key intermolecular forces include hydrogen bonds and Van der Waals interactions.

### Significance
Intermolecular forces are important since they play an inportmant role in biology, for example, in the formation of amino acids.

## Forces
- **Hydrogen bonds**: a result of partial chrages of polar molecules.
- **Van der Waals**: a weak attractive force that occurs between all molecules.

Hydrogen bonds allow for the formation of secondary structures, as the various R-structures of polypeptides interact with each other to create $\alpha$ -helices and $\beta$ -sheets.
They are most prominent in non-polar molecules, since such molecules have no other interactions, but are present in all molecular interactions.
