## Summary
The hypothesis that business cycles are the result of the central bank control of interest rate. Essentially, that the central bank control of the interest rate makes it so that interest rates are kept too low during booms, and too high during depressions, meaning that the effects of these interest rate changes are exasperated.
## Significance
This is the Austrian explanation for the existence of the business cycle, and this explanation differs from the explanations for the business cycle offered by other schools of thought.
