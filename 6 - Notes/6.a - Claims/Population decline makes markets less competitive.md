## Summary
Population decline has made markets less competitive and more prone to concentration of power by a few.
## Significance
Another reason why population decline would be bad for economies beyond the macro effect (such as the effect on the labor market).
## Explanation
- The correlation between labor force growth and startup entry rate is 0.74.
- There are less entry into markets, the average firm size has been increasing, and the share of young firms is decreasing.
- Without new firms/ideas, there is less creative destructions, and so firms hold onto products longer and allow markups to drift up.
- The economy also in the process loses from the new variety creation that comes with new entrants into markets.
- This calls for greater anti trust laws in order to prevent the formation of monopolies.
