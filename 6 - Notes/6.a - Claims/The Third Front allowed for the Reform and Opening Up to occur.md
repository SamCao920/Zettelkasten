## Summary
The Third Front is a period of massive infrastructure development which spread human and physical capital around the nation, ultimately making the Reform and Opening Up possible.
## Significance
The Reform and Opening Up, an important moment in Chinese history, was only possible due to the preexisting infrastructure that is constructed during this period of time.
