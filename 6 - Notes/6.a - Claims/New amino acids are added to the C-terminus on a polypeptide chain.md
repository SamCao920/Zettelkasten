## Summary
A polypeptide chain’s N-terminus is created first, so new amino acids are attached via peptide bonds on the C-terminus.

## Significance
This is something that might appear on an AP Bio test. This is also analogous to how New nucleotides are added to the 3' end of a DNA or RNA.
