## Summary
The primary purpose of fair equality of opportunity is simply to ensure that no one is unjustly treated based on factors outside of their control (i.e., social contingencies).
## Significance
This reveals the important of justice, since it is placed even above concerns of efficiency.
## Note
Rawls elaborates that even if restricting access to positions ultimately would produce better results, such actions would still not be justified.
`What about for cases such as men wanting to model for female modeling positions? Or patients requesting female doctors but there are equally qualified male doctors? This principle seems awfully vague.`
