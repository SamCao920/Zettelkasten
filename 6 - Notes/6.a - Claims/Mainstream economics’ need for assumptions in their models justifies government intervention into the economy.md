## Summary
Mainstream economics makes many claims about the assumptions which must be true in order for its economic models to be applied and thus the economy to be managed. Consequently, using the fact that their models typically fail to be satisfied, they justify state intervention into the economy.
## Significance
This is an implicit argument against mainstream economics.
## Explanation
The critique is essentially that mainstream economic is focusing too much on models and math, and failing to see that they are too focused on their models and not enough on the actual behavior of people and the applicability of their models. In fact, Vernon Smith demonstrated that many of the typically assumptions in mainstream economics are in fact unnecessary.
