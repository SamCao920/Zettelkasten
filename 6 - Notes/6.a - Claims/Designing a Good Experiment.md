## Summary
To design a good experiment, there must be Random Assignment, Experimental Control, and Experimental Replication.
## Significance
In the Scientific Method, perhaps the most important component is designing a good experiment to be able to test a hypothesis. There are some criteria for a good experimental design though.
## Principles of Experimental Design
1. The sample must be random (see Random Sampling) for the results of the experiment to be widely applicable to a population.
2. There must be a process of random assignment of treatment, as only by doing so can causation be determined (see  Randomization allows for causation to be determined).
3. Extraneous factors must be controlled as to prevent how  Confounding Factors can Distort the Outcome of an Experiment.
4. The experiment must be able to be replicated (both its steps and its outcome).
Only when all four of the above steps are taken, can the following conclusions be drawn: treatment $x$ has the effect $y$ on the population $z$ because of $u$.
