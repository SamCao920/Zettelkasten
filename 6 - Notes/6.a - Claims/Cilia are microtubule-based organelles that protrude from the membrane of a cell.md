## Summary
Cilia have two primary functions: One, the ambulatory ones help move stuff across the surface of the cell (or move the cell itself). Two, the ones that is not ambulatory act as sensory instruments, helping detect certain stimuli. 
## Significance
The presence of cilia can be used to determine the function of the cell.
