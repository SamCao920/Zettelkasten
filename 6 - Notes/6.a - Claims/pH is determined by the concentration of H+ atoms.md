## Summary
How basic (alkaline) or acidic a solution is is determined by the concentration of $H^+$ atoms in the solution. Higher concentration indicates alkalinity, and lower concentration indicates acidity.
## Significance
This is relevant for AP Bio, as Change in pH or salinity can break ionic bonds and hydrogen bonds.

## Formula
$$pH = -\log[H^+]$$
