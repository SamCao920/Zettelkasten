## Summary
Reasoning LLMs use reinforcement learning to optimize the reasoning process, in addition to being first trained using supervised pretraining. On the other hand, normal models also first train with the supervised pretraining, then they are  subject to reinforcement learning, but simply to optimize their outputs.
## Significance
This elucidates the difference between reasoning and normal models.
## Explanation
Reasoning LLMs, in their post-training phase, focus on optimizing their reasoning process through RLHF (reinforcement learning from human feedback) or RLAIF (reinforcement learning from AI feedback). On the other hand, normal models will simply have their outputs optimized with the same tools.
