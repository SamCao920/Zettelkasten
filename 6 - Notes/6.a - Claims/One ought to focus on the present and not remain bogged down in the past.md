## Summary
The idea that it is important to focus on the present and not be contained by what happened in the past.
## Significance
This is both a common trope in literary works and also something that is empirically demonstrated.
## In Economics
In economics, this manifests in the form of the concept of *sunk cost*, or costs that have already been incurred and which are irreparable. 
Indeed, the sunk cost fallacy is the inability to see this by operating in a manner which suggests that the costs are not actually sunk. 
The correct decision is to ignore sunk cost and operate on marginal costs.
## In Literature
This is also a theme in many works of literature, for instance,
- *The Sound and the Fury* is in large part about the failure of the Compson family to move past the Antebellum South.
