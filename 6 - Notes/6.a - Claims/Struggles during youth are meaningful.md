## Summary
The idea that our struggles during our youth have some special meaning, something which disappears during our struggle in our mid and late life.
## Significance
This is an oft-referenced idea in literature.
## Examples
- [[Youth (short story)]]: Marlow’s struggles during his youth are said by himself to have been meaningful, perhaps both for their aesthetic value and also the practical experience gained.
