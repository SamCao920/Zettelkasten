## Summary
The irrational part of the soul that Aristotle defines is composed of the vegetative (which humans share with animals) and the appetitive.
## Significance
This part of the soul is the domain of the moral virtues.
## Vegetative vs. Appetitive
- **Vegetative:** controls the basic/unconscious elements of an individual, i.e., biological growth, nutrition, sleep, etc. It functions even when the human is unconscious. Does not play a part in human excellence.
- **Appetitive:** controls the emotions, desires, and appetites of the individual. It is irrational, but is able to be tamed with reason. Moral training aligns this part of the soul with the rational part, producing the moral virtues.
