## Summary
The left-wing biases of researchers manifest through their research, as was proven through a study whereby teams of scientists were given the same data on immigration, yet around 2/3 of the researchers found positive outcomes for immigration. Additionally, the personal biases of the researchers correlate positively with the outcome of their research.
## Significance
This means that much of the science that comes out of researchers, especially in social science and humanities fields could potentially be biased.
## Note
- This does not regard a particular orientation, since while left-leaning researchers tend to find conclusions that support their political belief, right-leaning researches tend to find conclusions that support their political belief.
