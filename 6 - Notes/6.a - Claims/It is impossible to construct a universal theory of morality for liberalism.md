## Summary
With Rawls’ A Theory of Justice and Mill’s works as examples, while it seems that both attempt to construct a moral theory which is able to exist in a liberal political framework, though they both fail. Therefore, it seems that it is just impossible to construct a universal moral theory for liberalism.
## Significance
If this is true, then people should stop attempting to do so.
## Explanation
The moral theories proposed in *A Theory of Justice* and Mill’s works, such as *Utilitarianism* or *On Liberty* seems to have unsolvable flaws which undermine the very nature of the frameworks. 
The fact that they have both failed seems to suggest that it is simply impossible to construct a moral framework for liberalism.
