## Summary
One should choose to do the things that good for them (in their particular circumstances), not what is “good in themselves,” i.e., honor, wealth, power, pleasure, etc. 
## Significance
This explicates Aristotle’s theory of virtue, as he says that the most important thing is that one cultivates their overall virtue, not the amount of something that one has.
## Explanation
The unjust person is one who pursues things that are “good in themselves,” and not necessarily what is “good for them” in the specific circumstances that they are bound to. In this way, since it is the pursuit of what is good for oneself at the specific moment and under specific circumstances that brings the individual virtue, by not engaging in such pursuits, but are no longer virtuous and thus become unjust.
