## Summary
A conductor, due to the presence of free electrons, allow for electric charge/current to flow through the material with ease. An insulator does not allow electric charge/current to easily flow through the material, as the electrons in such a material are tightly bound to the atoms.

## Induction
Since electrons are able to easily move in a conductive material, it can generate strong inductive effects. On the other hand, insulators can only generate local polar effects.
