## Summary
While people have good economic intuitions about the nature of market forces in many areas of life, one areas where this intuition seems to be missing is when it comes to housing. This leads to people blaming the wrong demographic for high housing prices.
## Significance
This shows how misconceptions about the way that the economy works can have tangible and notable effects on the actual function of the economy.
## Explanation
While many people think that developers and landlords are the problem behind high housing prices/high rent, the root of the problem is ultimately that lack of housing restricts supply, and ultimately leads to higher prices.
