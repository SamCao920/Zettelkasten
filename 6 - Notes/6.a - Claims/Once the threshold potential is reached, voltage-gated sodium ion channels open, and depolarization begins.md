## Summary
When the dendrite receives a collection of different charges which average to around $-50mV$, voltage-gated $NA^+$ channels open, and cause a spike in the charge through a process known as depolarization.
## Significance
This is a crucial part of how muscles and neurons operate, as this is the beginning of a signal that is sent down the cells (in a neuron).
