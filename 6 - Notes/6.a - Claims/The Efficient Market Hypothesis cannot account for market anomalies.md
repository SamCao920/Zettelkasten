## Summary
The Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH) is not able to account for market anomalies such as the Momentum Effect or the January Effect.
## Significance
This illustrates that there are moments when the market becomes inaccurate predictors of all information relating to an asset.
## Explanation
Market anomalies exist for three main reaons:
1. Limits in risk models: if there are inaccuracies with the basic models of the EMH, then what may actually be risk hedging could appear as if they were anomalies.
2. Arbitrage is not always risk-free and frictionless.
3. Humans are not perfectly rational and they may make mistakes or suffer data issues.
