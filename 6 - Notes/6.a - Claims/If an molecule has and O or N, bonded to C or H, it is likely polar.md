### Summary
To determine polarity, use this simple method: if there is a “strong” molecule ($O$ or $N$), and they are bonded to a “weaker” molecule (that has less electronegativity), the molecule is polar.

### Significance
This is an important concept in biology, since it is important to know if a molecule is polar. For instance, The more polar a molecule, the more soluble it is in water is an example of how this Zettel can be applied.

## Note
If a “strong” molecule bonds with another strong molecule, it is non-polar.
If a "strong" molecule bonds with a "weak" molecule, then it is polar.
The bonds that are formed here are covalent bonds (see Biology relies on covalent and ionic bonds, not metallic bonds).
Strength here is determine by electronegativity, as The more electronegative an atom, the more it wants electrons.
