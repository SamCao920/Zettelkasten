## Summary
In general, deregulation and laissez-faire is good for the economy.
## Significance
This is a general approach for how the economy should be run.
## Explanation
This is especially true when the following three criteria are met:
- **Demand is relatively inelastic:** under such circumstances, taxes distort supply without lowering price.
- **High costs are common:** high probability of inefficient firms means that many necessary producers could be excluded.
- **Low redistributive motive:** there are some other reason for caring about firm profits.
## Examples
- The deregulation of the airline industry in the 1970s:
	- This drastically reduced the price of taking a flight with a decrease in safety. Prices fell by about 1/3.
	- Many more routes were offered.
	- Quality fell, and airlines moved to competition based on prices rather than quality.
