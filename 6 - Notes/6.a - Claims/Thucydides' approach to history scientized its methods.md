## Summary
Thucydides’ approach to history in his *History of the Peloponnesian War* scientized the study of history compared to the approaches taken by his predecessors, most famously Herodotus and Homer. Rather than focusing on divine intervention and inspiration, Thucydides seeks concrete evidence.
## Significance
Thucydides’ work laid the groundwork upon which future histories were created, as he set the standard for good history for the future.
