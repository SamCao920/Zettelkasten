## Summary
France’s system of strict regulation on business (high working standards), and extremely generous pensions are crippling the French economy.
## Significance
This poses an existential threat to the French state.
## Explanation
- France has the 3rd highest tax wedge in the OECD on an average single worker, at 47.2%.
- It is very difficult to fire people.
	- This means that firms are incentivized to hire less people, leading to a high youth unemployment rate (since established, older workers cannot be fire, and new workers won’t be hired since they can’t be fired).
	- They have consistently high unemployment rates as a result, being around 7% (across the general population as well, not just youth).
- The 35 hour work week led to higher labor turnover rates, since labor costs were essentially higher.
- France has an exceedingly low age of retirement (62), and as the ratio of retiree to worker increases, it is going to get more and more difficult to fund the pension program.
