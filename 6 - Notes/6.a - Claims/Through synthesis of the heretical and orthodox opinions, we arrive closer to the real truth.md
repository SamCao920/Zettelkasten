## Summary
While it is often assumed that either the heretics or the orthodoxy is right, a far more common occurrence is that both are right to some degree, and that a synthesis of the two viewpoints produces the best approximation of the underlying truth.
## Significance
This explains another situation in which it is beneficial to allow for freedom of expression.
