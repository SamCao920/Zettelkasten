## Summary
There are some cell organelles which originated as bacteria cells in a luminal space. For example, the mitochondria.
## Significance
This explains many odd features of these organelles, including how they have two cell membranes, have some chromosomes, and have internal ribosomes different from those extant in the cytoplasm.
