## Summary
Pleasure is not a process, or something that brings us from one state to another. 
## Significance
This is important for understanding the refutations against the claim that pleasure is bad.
## Explanation
- It is complete at every moment that it is experienced. 
- A process has steps that it is divided into but you cannot divide pleasure. 
- Pleasure does not take time, while processes take time.
This contributes to the argument that pleasure is not something that is merely for returning to an equilibrium state, or many other arguments that frame pleasure as a process, and therefore, not something that is good or worthy.
