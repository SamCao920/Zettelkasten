## Summary
This is known as the Equimarginal Principle. Due to the law of diminishing marginal utility, when choosing between two goods, we optimize utility by choosing to consume the amount where the marginal utility per dollar of the two goods is the same.
## Significance
This law allows us to understand how to maximize utility.
## Explanation
This intuitively makes sense because if one of the goods has a higher marginal utility per dollar, than we would shift our consumption from the other good to that good in order to maximize utility.
The mathematical formulation of this principle is as follows:
$$
\frac{MU_{x}}{P_{x}}=\frac{MU_{y}}{P_{y}}
$$
This model can actually account for many different things since we can consider one of the goods to be a composite good, i.e., money. Then the mathematical formulation would be as such:
$$
\frac{MU_{x}}{P_{x}}=\lambda
$$
Where $\lambda$ represents the marginal utility of income.
