## Summary
Cellular respiration consists of three parts: glycolysis, the Krebs cycles, and the E.T.C. Of these, the E.T.C. produces the most ATP, around 34. Total, this process produces about 36 ATP.
## Significance
This is one of the most important parts to understanding how biology works, since cellular respiration is extant in all living things. Things that are not performing cellular respiration are dead.
