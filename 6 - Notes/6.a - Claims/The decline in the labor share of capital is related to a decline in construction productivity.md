## Summary
The decline in the labor share of income can be partly attributed to the decline inn construction productivity and the rise in real housing prices (the two are complements).
## Significance
This worsens inequality and stunts economics growth.
## Explanation
- Due to the stagnant productivity in the real estate market, the real price of buildings have risen. Since workers have to be housed in buildings, this means that the labor share of income would fall.
- The Baumol effect is also at play, since while manufacturing productivity has skyrocketed, construction productivity has not.
