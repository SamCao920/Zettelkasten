## Summary
Traditionalism, or a lack of desire to accumulate capital, is capitalist spirit’s greatest enemy. It is in essence a rejection of the entrepreneurial spirit and in general a rejection of the desire to improve. It is also a rejection of the divine calling that Weber attributes for the genesis of the capitalist spirit.
## Significance
This concept is used to contrast the capitalist spirt with the “status quo" at the time, which Weber analyzed as being traditionalism.
