## Summary
Whether is currency can continue to have exorbitant privilege is determined by whether there is a real alternative to that currency extant in the world. 
## Significance
This can be used to understand if a currency dominant currency with exorbitant privilege is able to continue having that privilege.
## Explanation
This is something that has been observed in past examples of currencies which have enjoyed exorbitant privilege.
- When the Dutch gilder enjoyed the exorbitant privilege, it was the only nation in Europe that had a modern banking system that allowed it to operate in the way that it did and keep the Netherlands wealthy. 
	- This ended when the Bank of London rose in power and the pound was able to overtake the gilder, partially due to the involvement of the Dutch in continental conflicts.
- When Britain's pound enjoyed the exorbitant privilege, there was no real alternative to it. 
	- It was not until after WWII when the US financial system became globalized and US industrial power become something that was felt all around the world that the dollar became the premier currency.
