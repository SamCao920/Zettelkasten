## Summary
Turgot pressure, which is caused by osmosis (a difference in water potential), is when vacuoles in plants bulge outward, thus applying outward pressure, keeping the plant upright and aiding in its growth.
## Significance
Turgor pressure is one of the most important things that helps plants survive, as they keep the plant upright, allowing it to reach sunlight and produce sugar through photosynthesis.
