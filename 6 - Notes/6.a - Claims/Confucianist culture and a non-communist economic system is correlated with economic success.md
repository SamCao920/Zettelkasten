## Summary
There seems to be a correlation between Confucian culture in non-communist states and rapid economic growth, though there are some confounders which make it difficult to draw a clear connection between the two concepts.
## Significance
This could serve as a launching point for a deeper understanding of Confucianist culture and/or the rapid development of the Asian Tigers.
## Explanation
![[gdp_chart.svg|450]] ![[gdp_chart_mean.svg|450]]
The above charts seems to demonstrate that there is in fact some correlation between Confucianism and higher levels of economic growth, such that it is statistically significant that Confucianist, non-communist countries have higher levels of growth than non-Confucianist, non-communist countries.
