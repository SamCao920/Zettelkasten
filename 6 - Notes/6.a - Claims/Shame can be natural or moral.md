## Summary
Rawls identifies two types of shame: moral and natural.
## Significance
This explains the two types of shame that is possible for someone to have, and their different implications.
## Explanation
Moral shame arises from a failure to manifest or betrayal of some virtues that is explicated in one’s long-term rational plan of life. 
Natural shame comes from feeling deficient relative to one’s own ideals of excellence.
