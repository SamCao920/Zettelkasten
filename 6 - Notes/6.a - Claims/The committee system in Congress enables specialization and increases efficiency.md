## Summary
The committee system in Congress enables specialization, since individual congress people are able to work in the area that they are most familiar with. At the same time, it increases efficiency since the people most familiar with a topic are able to work on that topic with a small group of other people proficient in the topic, and many of these groups can work at the same time.
## Significance
This explains the benefits of a key institution in Congress.
