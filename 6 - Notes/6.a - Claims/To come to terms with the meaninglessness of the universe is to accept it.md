## Summary
The idea that the meaninglessness of the universe is best dealt with through acceptance, through the realization of the fact that one is not alone since the universe itself is meaningless and absurd.
## Significance
This is the central concept in Absurdism.
## Note
Often times, people can have a passive acceptance of the absurd, but it is an active embrace of the absurd which Camus is arguing for here, something that is more profound.
