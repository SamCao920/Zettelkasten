## Summary
An encompassing interest is one such that an actor’s private stake is intertwined with the stake of the broader society. This means that the actor will undertake actions that not only benefits itself, but also the entire society in which it exists. A narrow interest is one such that a actor’s private stake is disconnected and opposite the interests of the broader society. This means that it will engage in rent-seeking behavior.
## Significance
This allows for the categorization of incentives along a spectrum of encompassing to narrow, allowing for the analysis of policy implications along those lines.
## Example
A government has an encompassing interest in ensuring stable economic growth, since with economic growth its own tax base expands. Therefore, it will work to ensure that the economy grows.
On the other hand, oil companies could be considered a narrow interest. It engages in business activities that harm the environment, which is detrimental to the society around it, although the company itself profits from this damage.
