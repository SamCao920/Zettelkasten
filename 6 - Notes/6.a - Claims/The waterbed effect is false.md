## Summary
The waterbed effect is commonly cited as a reason for instituting antitrust regulation, since it supposedly hurts consumers. However, there are some issues with the commonly espoused theory.
## Significance
This is important since it undermines a commonly cited theory.
## Explanation
There are two things which together undermines this theory:
1. The firms must be able to take the markup, or otherwise they will break-off the agreement and switch to using a different supplier.
2. It must be the case that the weight average price for the consumer is higher after such a negotiation (since the lowering of input costs for the larger firm could leader to lower prices for consumers).
However, it might not be the case that both assumptions are true for all circumstances, and also, in some circumstances, accepting one of the assumptions prevents one from being able to accept the other assumption.
