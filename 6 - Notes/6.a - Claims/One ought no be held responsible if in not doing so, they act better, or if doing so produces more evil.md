## Summary
While usually one should be held responsible for their actions, they should not be held responsible when: 
1. by holding the person responsible, we end up producing more harm than good, or 
2. the person is likely to act better while not compulsed by others.
## Significance
This explains exceptions to the commonly held precept that people ought to be held responsible for their actions.
## Note
The exceptions noted here are clearly utilitarian in basis. A deontology is hardly accept either of these exceptions.
