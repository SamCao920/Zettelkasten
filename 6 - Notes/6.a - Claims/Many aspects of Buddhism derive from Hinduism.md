## Summary
There are many similarities between Buddhism and Hinduism, and that is largely because many elements of Buddhism are derived from Hinduism.

## Significance
This explains many of the origins of the practices from Buddhism that are reminiscent of Hinduism. It also explains why Buddhism made such a large impact when it was introduced in India.

## Buddhism & Hinduism
- Many aspects of Buddhism were derived from Hinduism, and Buddhism can be seen as a simplified and standardized version of Hinduism.
- At the same time, Buddhism challenged the inequalities that were introduced by a Hindu caste system, arguing that there are no barriers to enlightenment.
