## Summary
Unlike lipids, which have much more carbons than oxygens, carbs have roughly equivalent amounts of carbons and oxygens due to their structure ($CH_2O$)

## Significance
This is important for AP Bio, as it helps one to indentity whether a molecule is a carb.
