## Summary
While many point to the problem of incentives as the biggest problem facing systems like communism, these problems can usually be solved with coercion and violence. The bigger problem that is unsolvable in a non-market system is the problem of Economic Calculation, as since state-run economies lack market signals, their capital allocation is extremely inefficient.
## Significance
This explains the greatest problem that faces a state-run economy, important for when analyzing such economies.
