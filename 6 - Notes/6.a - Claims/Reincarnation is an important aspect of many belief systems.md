## Summary
In many different religions/belief systems, the idea of reincarnation is an important facet.

## Significance
This shows a connection between many different belief system in the world, helping us find similarities between otherwise disparate ideas.

## Examples
- In Hinduism, As the human soul migrated from body to body across lifetimes, *karma*, depending on the "pureness" of one's actions, may allow oneself to be reborn to a higher caste, eventually, to achieve Brahman (see The goal of Hinduism is to achieve Brahman).
