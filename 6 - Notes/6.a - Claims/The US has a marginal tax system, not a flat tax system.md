## Summary
The US has a marginal tax system, meaning that for each additional dollar of income earned, that dollar is taxed at a higher rate. In essence, this means that the US has a progressive taxation policy. The US does not have a flat tax system.
## Significance
This is important to understand, as many people misunderstand how the US tax system works, believing it to be a flat tax rate. This is important to understand when analyzing the US tax system.
