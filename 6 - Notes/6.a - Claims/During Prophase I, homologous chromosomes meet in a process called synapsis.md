## Summary
After S phase, there are two copies of every chromatid. Since you have two copies for each chromosome (each one having come from your parents) even before that, you are left with 4 chromatids. During Prophase I, the sister chromatids come together to form a tetrad (four chromatids) in a process called synapsis.
## Significance
This is important, since if the homologous chromosomes do not meet together during Prophase I, they would not be separated correctly in order for Meiosis II to occur.
