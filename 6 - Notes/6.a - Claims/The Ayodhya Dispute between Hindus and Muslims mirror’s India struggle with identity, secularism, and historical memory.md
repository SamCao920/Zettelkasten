## Summary
The Ayodhya dispute centers on a plot of land in Ayodhya where both Hindus and Muslims claims is a religious site. Although the issue has largely been settled by 2020, it serves as an indicator of religious tensions in India before that.
## Significance
The event highlights conflicts over religion in India, and disputes the claim that due to Hinduism’s tolerance for other belief systems, that there would not be any religious conflict.
