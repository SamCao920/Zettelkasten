## Summary
A system that elects a Condorcet Winner prioritizes ensuring the will of the majority, and not ensuring the that outcome is optimal in the utilitarian sense.
## Significance
Because electing a Condorcet Winner is only concerned with electing the leader who beats all other candidates individually, it does not take into consideration the level of support that the candidate has relative to other candidates.
## Explanation
Where as Condorcet Winner are merely the candidates who are supported by the most people, a winner elected via Score Voting ensures that the winner selected maximizes social welfare.
