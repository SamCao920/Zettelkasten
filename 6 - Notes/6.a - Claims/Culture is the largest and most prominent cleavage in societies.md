## Summary
Culture has become the most important factor in determining the identity of people in a nation. Nations that are significantly divided between cultures is known as a “torn country.”
## Significance
This means that a nation’s stability can partly be determined by the cultural homogeneity present in its society.
## Explanation
Culture becoming the most important factor in the way that a society views itself means that
- There is the creation of out-groups, leading to feeling of superiority, fear and lack of trust in people of different cultures, differences in communication, and lack of familiarity.
The cultural question is one that cannot be negotiated, since it is a zero-sum game.
Additionally, generally, it seems that nations with more diversity of culture tends to be more unstable than nations with more homogeneity of culture.
