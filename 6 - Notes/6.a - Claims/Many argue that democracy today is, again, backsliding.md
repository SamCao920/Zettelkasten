## Summary
Many are arguing that 2006 was the apex of the current wave of democratization, and that democratization has be in a downward decline following that point (around the same time as the Great Recession).
## Significance
This represents a backsliding of democracy again, as has happened in the previous waves, although this time, Democratic Backsliding has become more insidious, as authoritarians are allowing Competitive Authoritarianism and the slow corruption of democratic values, rather than more direct means, such as a violent coup.
