## Summary
Rawls argues that the most important primary good to have is that of self-respect.
## Significance
Self-respect is an important aspect of Justice as Fairness.
## Explanation
Self-respect mean:
1. One’s own conviction that his conception of the good is wroth carrying out.
2. A confidence in one’s ability to fulfill one’s intentions.
