## Summary
Inequality has not skyrocketed in the world, despite what is often claimed in the media.
## Significance
This disproves a common myth that is spread that inequality has been continually rising around the globe, but supports the idea that inequality has increased intra-nation.
## Explanation
![[Pasted image 20260123172359.png]]
Since the early 2000s, and especially with the 2008 Financial Crisis, inequality has been falling across the world. Global income inequality has also been falling.
At the same time, inequality has risen in individual countries.
