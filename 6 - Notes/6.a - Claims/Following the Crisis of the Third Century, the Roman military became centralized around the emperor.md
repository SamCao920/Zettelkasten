## Summary
Due to the increased risks of powerful generals rising up in an attempt to take the emperorship, emperors began centralizing control of the army so that the military moved where the emperor did.
## Significance
This is the final evolution of Roman border defense as troops were removed from the border and centralized around the emperor in order to ensure that power was centralized. This made border defense difficult, since it required long distance travel in order to reach a particular spot and only one border defense issue can be resolve at one time.
