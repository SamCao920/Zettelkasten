## Summary
The Labor Theory of Value is false.
## Significance
This is a theory that is argued for my Marx, and forms the basis of Marxist economics.
## Counterarguments
1. “**Intrinsic Value” is non-Popperian**: The idea of Socially Necessary Labor Time as something that is only able to be considered through price is unfalsifiable. It is entirely constructed based on the prices observed in the market ex-post.
	- A good that sells at a low price has a low SNLT, and a good that sells at a high price has a high SNLT. Both these are only determined after the fact, and thus can be defined as anything someone wants.
2. **The Transformation Problem**: 
	- Marx states the Law of Value: since profit only comes from “surplus value,” or laborers who are paid below their marginal product, a labor-intensive venture should have higher profit margins than a capital-intensive venture.
	- He also states the Law of Equal Profit: capital moves around the market until profit equalizes across all industries.
	- The problem is that how can every capitalist have the same amount of profit even if they have different amounts of labor use?
	- To solve this, Marx introduces the transformation, which says that firms that are more labor intensive are contributing to firms that are more capital intensive. 
		- He does this by defining the Price of Production: $Constant \ Capital + Variable \ Capital + Average \ Profit$.
		  And Value: $Constant \ Capital + Variable \ Capital + Surplus \ Value$
		- When you subtract the two, labor heavy firms lose value, while capital heavy industries gain the value lost by the capital heavy industries (assuming that capital does not create value).
	- The problem here is that the price of constant capital changes due to the that surplus value adds to the price of goods needed as raw materials for production. This means that any moment, the total price in the economy is greater than the total value, meaning that capital must have created value, disproving the labor theory of value.
3. **Joint Production**: When multiple goods are produced at the same time, any attempt to quantify the labor value between the goods produced at the same time is arbitrary.
4. **Homogenous Labor is false**: Labor is not homogenous. The labor of a doctor and the labor of janitor cannot be quantified the same way.
5. **Natural Resources and Scarcity Rents:** Factors such as the local supply of something also determines the price. A bottle of really rare wine, although it might have taken the same amount of labor as many other wines, is nonetheless valued more because it is rare.
