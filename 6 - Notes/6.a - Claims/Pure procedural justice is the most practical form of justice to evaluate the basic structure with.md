## Summary
Pure procedural justice, since it does not involve judging the result against an independent criteria. Doing this would be impractical since the basic structure would produce too many individual distributions and transactions.
## Significance
This explains the reason that Rawls adopts pure procedural justice in analyzing the basic structure.
