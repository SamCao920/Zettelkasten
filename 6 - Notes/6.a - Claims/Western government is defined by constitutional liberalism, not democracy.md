## Summary
The idea that Western government is better envisioned as being built upon Constitutional Liberalism, and not Democracy (mass plebiscite).

## Significance
This offers an unique view on the nature of Western governments, deriving from the distinction between Democracy and Constitutional Liberalism.

## Source
> [!quote] Fareed Zakaria
>  The “Western model of government” is best symbolized not by the mass plebiscite but the impartial judge.

## Evidence
- Ever since Roman times, there has been a strong traditional in the Western world of preserving certain individual rights and enforcing the rule of law.
- "Democracy" in the sense of a mass plebiscite did not really originate in any meaningful capacity until the 19th century, in the US and the UK.
