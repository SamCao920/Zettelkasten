## Summary
The idea that someone who is “good” goes above and beyond the standard moral conventions that agents would rationally agree to in the original position.
## Significance
This is the definition that Rawls gives, and the definition which he uses in his book. 
## Explanation
A good person engages in good acts. A good act is an non-compulsed act which is intended to advance the rational long-term plan of another person.
