## Summary
All energy generated through light-dependent reactions in photosynthesis are used up during the process, specifically, the ATP and NADPH generated during light-dependent reactions are all used up during the Calvin cycle to produce G3P. None of this energy goes outside of this system.
## Significance
This is a common misconception that can distort one’s understanding of how photosynthesis works. Photosynthesis does not “produce” energy in any concrete way, such as in the form of ATP. Rather, it only produces G3P.
