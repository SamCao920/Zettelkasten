## Summary
In the future, due to the rise in the ability of capital to entirely replace human labor (rather than be complements), capital’s share of income will drastically increase.
## Significance
This is the thesis of Thomas Picketty, and although he had been wrong about the past, this note argues that he will be correct in the future.
## Explanation
While in the past, it has not been the case that capital has been able to largely replace labor (and thus occupy a larger share of national income), as Picketty predicted, mainly due to the fact that capital remained complementary to labor, in the future, especially due to AI, this is unlikely to be the case.
AI is able to perform many agentic tasks that require complex cognitive functions, which has been the factor that has ensured that labor is complementary to capital (labor is needed to ensure that the repetitive work that capital does is directed in the right way). This means that they will likely begin to overtake labor’s share of income in the economy.
- This also means that wealth inequality could rise, since the people who originally had more wealth are better able to utilize AI (since they can hire “AI Advisors”), and thus generate wealth, and so on.
- Additionally, while developing nations have traditionally relied on lower labor costs to attract work, now that capital is able to replace labor, these countries lose their comparative advantage there. Naturally resources are the only means for them to grow rich at that point.
Some ways to potentially mitigate this is a strong wealth tax or inheritance tax.
