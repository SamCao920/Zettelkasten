## Summary
The first principle in Rawls’ two fundamental principles of justice. 
## Significance
This is fundamental to Rawls’ theory of justice.
## Explanation
The more precise statement: Equal person is to have an equal right to the most extensive basic liberty compatible with a similar liberty for others.
Additionally, this condition has lexical priority over the second condition (i.e., fair equality of opportunity and the difference principle).
This principle is derived from the general conception of justice by a rational agent.
