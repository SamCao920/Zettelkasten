## Summary
The idea that religion/faith is able to be reconciled with science, despite the typically dogmatic approach that faith holds towards belief.
## Significance
This is a project that has been attempted by many.
## *The Brothers Karamazov*
This is something that Dostoevsky attempts to accomplish in *TBK*. He attempts to existentialize religion to make it both not coerce belief (the existence of miracles, in his view, would coerce belief), but ensure that the individual can still believe. He does this through the extraction of the existential core of Christian rituals.
Some examples of the rituals that he is able to existentialize include:
- **Confession**: public/communal confession of sin to all of the world, since one is responsible for everything.
	- The moral “butterfly effect.”
- **The incarnation**: someone receives a divine spirit when they embrace agape and the spirit of loving all that is around them.
- **Ordination of priests**: God enters first Markel, then Zossima, then Alyosha.
- **The crucifixion**: when people experience suffering, they can
	- “lacerate” themselves, they “crucify” themselves to feel a sense of moral superiority to others. But is equivalent to being crucified, and staying that way.
- **Immortality**: ?
- **Angels**: someone who is able to, with agape, bring out the good in others.
	- Ex. Alyosha to Grushenka.
- **Devils**: someone who desires to bring out the worst in others.
	- Ex. Rakitin when he tried to make Alyosha sin.
- **Miracles**: when something unexpected situation is created through love.
	- Ex. When Alyosha saves Grushenka.
- After Zossima dies, the book gives specific, scientific reason for why the body stinks. This ruined the miracle of the death of Zossima.
	- Tangentially, this also leads to a lot of disbelief, as people prove right the thesis of the GI, that they are weak and need guidance and miracles for faith.
- But eventually Alyosha is able to overcome this through a realization of his own, something that comes internally.
Religion is not observed through miracles that break the laws of physics; rather, it is observed through human interconnection.
