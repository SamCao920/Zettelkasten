## Summary
While there were many technological innovations before the Industrial Revolution, there was also a disconnect between practical (prescriptive) knowledge and theoretical (propositional) knowledge.
## Significance
 This disconnect between the two forms of knowledge is the reason that the innovations were not able to produce economic growth.
