## Summary
A common tactic used by leaders to maintain control when there is internal division is to turn your attention outwards.
## Significance
This is one possible motivation for leaders to seek external conflict, and may explain the underlying cause of some conflicts.

## Examples
- [Invasion of Algiers (1830)](https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!&&p=e9adc0cd945de2565d39f8b76f7f46bea866252ed164dfc404f27d1a9a20d612JmltdHM9MTc1NzYzNTIwMA&ptn=3&ver=2&hsh=4&fclid=02b3cf74-2a65-6907-1060-d9392b216882&psq=charles+x+invasion+of+algeria&u=a1aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvSW52YXNpb25fb2ZfQWxnaWVyc18oMTgzMCk): Charles X invaded Algiers in order to distract form domestic problems and restore his prestige.
- [Falklands War (1982)](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Falklands_War): Argentina’s government ordered an invasion of the Falklands in order to distract form their popularity and mismanagement of the nation.
- [Russo-Japanese War (1904-1905)](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Russo-Japanese_War): Russia attempted to attack Japan in order to distract from its internal problems. However, they were defeated.
