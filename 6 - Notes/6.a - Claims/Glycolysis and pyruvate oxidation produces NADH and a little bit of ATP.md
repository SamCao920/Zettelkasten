## Summary
Glycolysis, or the process by which a glucose is turned into 2 pyruvate, produces two net ATP, but more importantly, produces two NADH. The pyruvate is then again oxidized (now inside the mitochondria), where more NADH is produced.
## Significance
The two NADH produced travels inside the mitochondria, specifically the inner mitochondrial membrane, at the ETC, where the NADH is needed. If the cell in engaging in anaerobic respiration, then fermentation occurs instead.
