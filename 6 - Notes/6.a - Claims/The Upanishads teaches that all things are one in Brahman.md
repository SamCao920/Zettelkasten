## Summary
A collection of sacred texts composed by anonymous thinkers from around 800 to 400 B.C.

## Significance
This is one of the most significant texts for Hinduism.

## Contents
Discusses the idea of the Brahman, the World Soul, and the final and ultimate reality, which is the primal unitary energy that infuses all things. Essentially, the idea that "All Things Are One."
