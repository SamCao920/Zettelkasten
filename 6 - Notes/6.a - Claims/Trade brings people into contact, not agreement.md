## Summary
Trade brings people into contract, but it does not necessarily mean that people will be brought into agreement. 
## Significance
This challenges the narrative presented by the Doux Commerce thesis.
## Examples
For instance, there is a persistent cultural divide between Australia and the rest of Asian (which is its biggest trading partner). Although they have close trading ties, their diplomatic relations remain tense, and there is no acceptance of Australia as an "Asian country" among the actual "Asian" countries.
