## Summary
The minorities in a nation, due to limited access to political influence, are “driven with peculiar force into economic activity.”
## Significance
This helps establish the reason for the Protestants to focus on economic activity, thus finally leading to the development of capitalism.

## Connection with the Protestant Work Ethic
Since the Protestants were the minorities, they focused their efforts mainly on economic advancement. This lead to their development of capitalism.
## Merchants in China
An an interesting parallel of this idea is that merchants were the lowest social class of people in Imperial China. Compared to the peasants, which made up around 90% of the population, they were definitely the minority, who just so happened to also work in a very lucrative profession.
