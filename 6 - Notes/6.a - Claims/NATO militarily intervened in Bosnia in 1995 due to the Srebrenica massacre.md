## Summary
The July 1995 massacre of Bosnians by the Serb army was the worst massacre post-WWII, and led to the involvement of NATO forces in Bosnia in order to prevent further war crimes from being committed.
## Significance
This explains the immediate spark for NATO intervention in the Bosnian War.
