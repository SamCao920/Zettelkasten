## Summary
The British political system is suffering from a number of problems that has fundamentally changed the way that it works compared to the government from three decades ago.
## Significance
This illustrates a major change in the British political system that could potentially have lasting effects on the way that politics is conducted in the future in Britain.
## Explanation
- People are becoming less loyal to their party, meaning that new political parties are rising into the forefront. The traditional Labor and Conservative parties are both now trailing behind Reform UK.
- The electorate is becoming more fractured and the majoritarian system means that leaders supported by only a small number of people come to power.
- There are a rising number of independent political figures that become elected, who essentially run their own campaigns and also are often single-issued.
	- This could leads to a Dutch-style pillarization, where parties become increasingly single-issued, or it may lead to a US style government where political outsiders are able to enter the political sphere by taking advantage of frustration.
