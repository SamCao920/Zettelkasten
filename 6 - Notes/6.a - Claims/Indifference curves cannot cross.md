## Summary
Indifference curves, which are the curves that highlight an individual's preference between two goods (one of which may be a composite good). This means that two indifference curves cannot cross, since that would mean that there could be two difference levels of preference for an item.
## Significance
This is significant because the fact that indifference curve cannot cross is one of their fundamental properties.
