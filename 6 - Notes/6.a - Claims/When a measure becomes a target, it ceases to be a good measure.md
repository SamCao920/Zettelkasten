## Summary
When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure, since the original phenomenon that the measure was intending to record may be distorted by attempts to increase the observed measurement.
## Significance
This is a general adage that applies to many areas of study, but especially when it comes to economics, since the measurements are the only way to determine the success of a policy.
