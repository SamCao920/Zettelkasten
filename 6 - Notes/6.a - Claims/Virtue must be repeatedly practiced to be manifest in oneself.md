## Summary
The idea that virtue must be repeatedly practiced in order for it to be manifest in a person, that an action is not merely virtuous if it is the result of some fleeting impulse and not fundamentally a part of someone’s character.
## Significance
This is Aristotle’s most important point regarding the nature of virtue, that it must be constantly practiced.
