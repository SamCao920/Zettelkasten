## Summary
The structure of the government of the Roman Republic was designed in such a way as to balance the power of its various elements, so that the government is more structurally sound.
## Significance
This explains the efficiency and power of the Roman Republican government.
## Explanation
The three parts of the government: the Senate, the Consuls, and the people all balance each other, since they each hold powers which check the others’ power.
