## Summary
The basic unit of many nomadic groups was the tribe. This meant that power almost always fell to the strongest, since the tribe’s survival was dependent on the leadership of the leader, and weak leaders would be abandoned.

## Significance
This was the context in which mongol society developed, which speaks to the way that they approach life and warfare.
