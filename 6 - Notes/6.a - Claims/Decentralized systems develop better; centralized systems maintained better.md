## Summary
During the development of a system, it is better to have decentralization, as it allows for independence and autonomy which can aid with growth. However, when it has been developed to a certain extent, more control should be exerted over the system to ensure no unwanted/misguided growth, decay, or destabilization.
## Significance
This is important when examining the growth of states and their maintainance.
