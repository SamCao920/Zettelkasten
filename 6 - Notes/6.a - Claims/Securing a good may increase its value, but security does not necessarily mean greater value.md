## Summary
Securing a good may increase its value (i.e., gold in a vault is more valuable than gold that is just lying in the open), but security does not necessarily mean value will be enhanced. Generally, the greater the inherent value of the good, the greater security may enhance its value.
## Significance
This explains the effect that security may have on a good, namely that given the good is inherently valuable, security would increase its value.
## Explanation
Given a good that is inherently value-less, like dirt, increased security does not mean that the value of the dirt is greater. One would not prefer dirt that is locked in an ultra secure vault compared to dirt that is scattered randomly on the ground.
