## Summary
All three types are carbohydrates.
- A monosaccharide (also known as "simple sugar") are the building blocks of carbs.
- A disaccharide is two monosaccharides joined together.
- A polysaccharide is a long chain of monosaccharides; they can be a starch or a fiber.
