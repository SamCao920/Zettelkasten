## Summary
Of all the different things that bring about happiness, contemplation is the one that brings about the most profound/highest form of happiness.
## Significance
This is a core aspect of the happy and virtuous life.
## Explanation
Happiness is achieved in its highest form with contemplation since:
- Contemplation is the highest, and most continuous of all activities. We are most capable of practicing it, and we can do it anywhere, at anything.
- Contemplation is the most pleasurable thing. Those with knowledge are more pleasured than those still searching for knowledge.
- Contemplation is self-sufficient, and it is something we can do by ourselves.
- Contemplation is appreciated for its own sake. It is not a means to an end; it is the end.
- Contemplation must be the activity of the gods (if only that is because they have nothing else to do).
Moral activity is secondary happiness, since it engages with worldly and practical things, whereas contemplation is the greatest form of happiness, since it concerns the heavenly/divine.
