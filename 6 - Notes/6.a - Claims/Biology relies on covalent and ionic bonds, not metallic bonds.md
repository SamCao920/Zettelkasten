## Summary
There are three different types of chemical bonds: covalent, ionic, and metallic. However, only covalent and ionic are relevant for biology.

## Significance
Chemical bonds, along with Intermolecular forces interact between non polar molecules intermolecular forces, are important in biology.

## Bonds
There are three types of chemical bonds:
-  Covalent bonds share electrons 
-  Ionic bonds transfer electrons
