## Summary
There are both devices in the eyes and beaks of birds that rely on magnetic fields in order to maintain their latitude and their direction of travel.
## Significance
This explain the ability that birds have to be able to travel in the correct direction intuitively.
## Explanation
**Eyes**: There are special proteins in the eyes of birds (cryptochromes) which, when hit by blue light, knocks electrons in the protein into “radical pairs” which are quantumly entangled pairs of electrons that are extremely sensitive to gravitational fields. The birds are thus able to “see” the magnetic field, allowing them to travel in the right direction.
**Beaks**: There are small magnetite inside the beaks of birds that are pulled upon by the Earth’s magnetic field, the strength of which depends on the latitude of the bird’s location. This means that they give the bird a physical sense of their location on Earth.
