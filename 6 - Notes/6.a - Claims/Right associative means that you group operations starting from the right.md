## Summary
Right associative means that logical sentences are grouped (paratheses are used) starting with placing a parathesis on the right of the expression.
## Significance
This is important convention for writing logical notation.
## Examples
The following expression would be incorrect:
$$
(P\to Q)\to Z
$$
Rather, this would be the correct placement of paratheses:
$$
P\to (Q\to Z)
$$
That is, the paratheses should be placed first started with the expression most on the right.
