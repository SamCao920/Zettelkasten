## Summary
Obstinacy is not the same thing as continence, though they may share some similarities.
## Significance
Continence is an important moral state, and it is important to not get it mixed up with obstinacy.
## Explanation
- Obstinacy can mean that someone is “hard to persuade” or “not easily persuaded.”
	- In the former case, it means that they are difficult to change their mind. 
	- In the latter case, it means that it is difficult to change their minds once they are already set on something.
- There are three types of obstinacy:
	- **Opinionated**: relates to pleasure and pain; such a person like being right too much. It causes them pain when they are wrong.
	- **Stupid**: such people are not able to figure something out because they can’t think rationally about a problem, or they don’t have the relevant knowledge.
	- **Boorish**: such people are stubborn due to lack of sensitivity or social awarness.
