## Summary
Prudence (*phronesis*) is the ability to deliberate rightly about what is good and advantageous for oneself (not what is good for something in particular, but what is good to the good life in general). Focuses on the action, not the production. It deals with the contingent/variable.
Experience with particulars (through the virtue of intellect, *nous*) is required to become more prudent.
## Significance
This is one of the most important intellectual virtues, since it is something that is practical and used everyday in order to rationally deliberate on what is needed to achieve the good life.
