## Summary
To be happy, one must not only be virtuous, one must also have proper material conditions. Aristotle argues that to be truly happy, we must only only be virtuous (embody “goodness”), we must also have proper material conditions.
## Significance
This portrays Aristotle’s philosophy as one that is eminently practical and realistic, rather than idealistic and aesthetic.
