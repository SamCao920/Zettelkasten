## Summary
Unlike what might seem logical at first, the capitalist spirit was developed to a far greater extent in the heavily protestant North of the US, rather than the South, many of which were founded with the sole purpose of generating income.
## Significance
This explain the importance of religion (specifically Protestantism) when it comes to the development of the capitalist spirit.
