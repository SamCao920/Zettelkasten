## Summary
The Three Represents (三个代表) marked a shift of party ideology from class struggle to economic development, helping the party align with the rapid economic development that has occurred in China in the past decade.
## Significance
This is one of the most important chances to party ideology, since it marked a major shift from the ideology of previous Chinese leaders.
## Explanation
By allowing the wealthy elite into the party, the CCP successfully integrated the economic elite into the party system by arguing that entrepreneurs were essential to “advanced productive forces.” This was done partly to ensure the survival of the party, by essentially co-opting the emerging economic elites and intellectuals, ensuring that these powerful people were on their side.
