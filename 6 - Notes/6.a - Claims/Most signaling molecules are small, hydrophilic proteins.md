## Summary
Most signaling molecules that exist are small, hydrophilic, and proteins. This includes cytokines, hormones, and growth factors. However, steroids, for example, are not proteins.
## Significance
This is significant because the type of molecule that a signaling molecule is can determine the way that it is able to interact with a cell. For example, protein signaling molecules are likely not able to cross membranes.
