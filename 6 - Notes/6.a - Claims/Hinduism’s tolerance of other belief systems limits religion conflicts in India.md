## Summary
Due to the tolerant nature of Hinduism, which offers many “different paths to the same top of the hill,” religion conflict is reduced in India.

## Significance
This illustrate how particular viewpoints towards the religion, more specifically, the acceptance towards religious values different than yours, may shape a civilization in ways beyond the immediate influence of the religion itself. 

## Explaination
Hinduism is very tolerant of other religions, leading to very little religious conflict in India throughout history, except when outside religions attempted to move in (usually headed by a hostile army).
