## Summary
Only realized institutions, i.e., institutions which are effectively and impartially administered in reality, can be considered to be unjust or just. To judge an abstract institution means to judge the realized version of that institution.
## Significance
This means that in order to consider some institution, we have to consider the way that it would function in reality (though still idealized), and not just consider it as an abstract concept.
