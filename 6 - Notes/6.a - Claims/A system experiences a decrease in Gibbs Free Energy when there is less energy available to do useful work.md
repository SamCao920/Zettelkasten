## Summary
A negative change in Gibbs Free Energy ($-\Delta G$) indicates that the total amount of energy available to do work is decreased following some reaction or occurrence.
## Significance
This is crucial for understanding the second law of thermodynamics, which states that energy tends to move towards lower levels.
