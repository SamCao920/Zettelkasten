## Summary
As the Mongols originated in the plains of Northern China, where the temperature fluctuates widely (-30 C to 40 C), they were adapted to fight in many different areas around the world.
## Significance
This explains why the Mongols were able to take such a large amount of land, and also shows that conditioning oneself to a variety of situations allows oneself to perform better when those situations inevitably arrive.
