## Summary
Interest groups often suffer from collective action problems, which means that the groups which are best able to solve this problem are able to operate the most efficiently and promote their interest as best as possible.
## Significance
This explains why some groups are more effective than others.
## Explanation
- Solving this problem depends on some key factors, such as the group size and ability to offer selective incentives.
- There can be material selective incentives, social selective incentives or expressive selective incentives, though material incentives are typically considered the most effective.
