## Summary
The tale follows Harry, a dying writer on safari in Africa, as he lies suffering from gangrene and reflects bitterly on his wasted talent, bad choices, and failed relationships, especially with his wealthy companion Helen, who tries to care for him. As he drifts in and out of consciousness, the story moves between his present physical decline and his memories and imagined scenes from his life, revealing his regret that he traded serious writing for comfort and superficial living.
## Significance
This is one of Hemmingway’s most acclaimed short stories.
