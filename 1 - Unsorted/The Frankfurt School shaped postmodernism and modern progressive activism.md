## Summary
The idea that modern progressive activism was shaped by the Frankfurt School, though many of the core leftism tenants are perversions of the original thought of the Frankfurt School.
## Significance
This explains the ideological origins of the modern progressive activism movement.
## Explanation
- The Frankfurt School practiced *negative dialectics*, which involved describing a concept by what it is not.
