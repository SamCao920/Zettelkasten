## Summary
in most cases, the government should not interfere in the affairs of private citizens, since such intervention is either inefficient or actively harmful.
## Significance
This is one of the core ideas in Mill’s *On Liberty*.
## Explanation
Government intervention is not justified for the following reasons:
1. Individuals can do tasks better without government intervention.
2. It is good for individuals to do something, even though the government may do it ore efficiently, because it helps to develop the citizenry.
3. It unnecessary to add to the powers of government, which can be abused.
