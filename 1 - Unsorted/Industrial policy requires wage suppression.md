## Examples
We can look to India and Japan for an illustration of this. At the beginning of the 20th century, the two countries had approximately equal levels of wages and productivity. However, due to the more luddite views of Indian laborers, and the fact that there is an inelastic supply of labor in India, Japan was able to surpass in Indian in terms of (lower) levels of wages and higher productivity. This led to successful industrialization in the country.
