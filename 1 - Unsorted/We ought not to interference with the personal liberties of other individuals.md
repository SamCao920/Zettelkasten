## Summary
We should not interfere with the personal liberties of others, for a variety of reasons.
## Significance
This is the core axiom of liberalism.
## Explanation
There are many explanations which can be given as to why this is ought to be followed. 
- We don’t necessarily know what is best for others.
- It could be that people who act unorthodoxly are actually right, and by suppressing their behavior, we are in fact moving further away from the truth.
## Note
There are some personal injuries which must be tolerated, since they arise out of people considering the protection of their own rights and liberties. 
- For instance, someone who is short tempered may have very few friends, though they cannot claim that this is an injustice done to them, since people are merely protecting their own interests.
- Likewise, societal institutions such as competition cannot cause harm, since there has not been an unwarranted invasion of one’s rights which is contrary to the general interest.
