## Summary
The laws of some state does not constitute justice. In other words, it is not the case that whatever the laws say is exactly what justice is.
## Significance
This fundamentally weakens the justificatory power of law and the nature of the legal system.
## Explanation
This is proven by the fact that there are and have been obviously unjust laws, and therefore, laws could not possibly be the only valid interpretation of justice.
