## Summary
The idea that human social relations are non-trivial and go beyond mere prudential concerns.
## Significance
This builds upon the contractarian moral framework by appending the consideration that people’s relation with each other are non-trivial.
## Explanation
In order to ultimately reach one’s full potential, a community is required. In other words, the whole community together outweighs each individual by themselves added together. 
There are two sub-claims related to this idea:
1. That successful, just institutions are the shared and final end.
2. That such institutions are prized as goods in themselves, rather than merely instrumentally.
