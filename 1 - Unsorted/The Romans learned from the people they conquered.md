## Summary
One of the key features of the Romans were their readiness to adapt. They would adopt the most effective means of accomplishing something, even if it meant copying from their enemies.
## Significance
This explains their success in creating such a large empire and ruling it successfully.
## Examples
- The Romans adopted the Greek style of cavalry which provided better equipment for them and allowed the units to be more useful in combat.
- They also copied the Gladius from Spanish tribes which they fought against.
