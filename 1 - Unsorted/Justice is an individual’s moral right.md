## Summary
Mill’s ultimate definition of justice is that it is someone’s moral right, and it represents the rules of conduct applicable to all and intended for the good of all.
## Significance
This is Mill’s definition of justice.
## Explanation
Injustice occurs when:
1. Someone is deprived of their liberty, property, or any other such lawful thing.
2. Taking something away from someone where that thing is a moral right.
3. One does not obtain what they deserve.
4. One breaks faith (a promise).
5. One is partial when one ought not to be.
