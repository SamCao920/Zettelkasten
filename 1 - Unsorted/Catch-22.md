## Summary
A darkly comic World War II novel about Captain Yossarian, a U.S. Air Force bombardier desperately trying to survive the absurd bureaucracy and endless dangers of war. The book’s title refers to a contradictory rule that traps people in impossible situations: if a soldier is insane, he can be grounded, but asking to be grounded proves he is sane. Through a chaotic, satirical style, the novel attacks war, power, and institutional logic, showing how systems meant to protect people can instead become cruel and irrational.
## Significance
One of the most famous and important novels in the 20th century.
