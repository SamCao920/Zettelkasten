## Summary
All ethical theories, despite their justification, are ultimately justified by their consequence (their teleology).
## Significance
This is what Mill contends, and one of his supporting arguments for utilitarianism.
## Explanation
Even though theories might claim that their ethical principles are derived out of some first principles unrelated to the outcome of those principles, ultimately, it is the outcome of those principles that lead the first principles to be established in the first place.
