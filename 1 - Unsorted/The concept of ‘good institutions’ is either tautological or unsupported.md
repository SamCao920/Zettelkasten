## Summary
The idea that the thesis that “good institutions” lead to economic growth is false because the concept of “good institutions” is fundamentally mistaken.
## Significance
This rejects the thesis that there is such a thing as “good institutions,” or that it does not prove a causation that “good institutions lead to economic growth.”
