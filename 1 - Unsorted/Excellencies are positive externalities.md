## Summary
Excellencies are essentially an individual’s traits which have broader benefits for the society at large, rather than just benefiting the individual themselves.
## Significance
That excellencies are public goods means that we should try to promote them in society. It also has implications for concepts such as shame.
## Explanation
Excellencies are essentially virtues in Rawls’ view, something that is done that is beyond the requirements of conventional morality that is self-centered.
