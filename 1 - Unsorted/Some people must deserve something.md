## Summary
A Moorean refutation of the idea that nobody deserves anything (as per luck egalitarianism) by claiming that, *surely*, *some* people deserve *some* things.
## Significance
This is a significant argument against the claims made by luck egalitarianism and Rawls.
## Explanation
This is specifically outlined in Zaitchik’s “On Deserving to Deserve.” The target is the regress principle "If A deserves X in virtue of Y, then A must also deserve Y." He rejects it as a suppressed premise: one can deserve the outcome of one's effort without deserving the capacity to exert effort. If Zaitchik is right, that desert does not require ELC as a precondition, it requires only that the agent exercises agency in the relevant way.
