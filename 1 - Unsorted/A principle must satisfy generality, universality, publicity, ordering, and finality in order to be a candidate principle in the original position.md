## Summary
In order for a principle to be considered in the original position, there are several conditions that it must meet.
## Significance
This outlines some of the requirements that principles must abide by in the original position.
## Conditions
There are five conditions that Rawls identifies in order for a principle to be seriously considered in the original position:
1. Generality condition: principles must be general and does not refer to specific people or require knowledge of contingent particulars.
