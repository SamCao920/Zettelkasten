## Summary
Under most circumstances, the act of falsifying never reduces to clean deduction.
## Significance
This disproves a common misconception about the nature of science.
## Explanation
There are two main reasons for this:
1. There is not a bijection between hypotheses and models which explains the hypotheses. In fact, many hypotheses could be related to a model, and many models and be related to a single hypothesis.
2. Inaccuracies in the data could lead to incorrect falsifications.
