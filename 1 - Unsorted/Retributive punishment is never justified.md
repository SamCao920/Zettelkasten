## Summary
The idea that retribution punishments are never justified, which is often justified by invoking several theses (together): 
1. determinism (or some form of compatibilism) is true such that we are not at least fully responsible for our actions, 
2. suffering is never intrinsically good, 
3. we can punish the crime-doer not directly through retributive punishment, and 
4. punishment’s main goal should be deterrence and prevention.
