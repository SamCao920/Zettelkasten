## Notes
The way that the frame is slightly tilted creates a sense of incoherence and confusion for the viewer, and also, as one observes closely, the lighthouse is way to big for the background of the view, which means that the perspective would only make sense if the lighthouse were placed at the bottom of a cliff such that only the top of the lighthouse shows.
These inconsistencies create a sense of the disquiet in the viewer.
