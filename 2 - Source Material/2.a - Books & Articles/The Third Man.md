## Summary
A film about a Holly Martin who travels to Vienna for a job from his friend Harry Lime. However, he finds out that Harry had recently been killed when he gets to Vienna, and goes on a quest to find his killlers.
## Significance
This film explores themes of existentialism and tragedy in an entertaining and profound manner.
## Themes & Ideas
- [[The 'tragedy’ in tragic works of art often spring from the main character’s own actions]]
	- While *The Third Man* is not exactly a tragedy, it could be argued that in his pursuit of Harry’s killers, Holly alienated himself from his love interest, Anna.
- [[It is not always good to know the truth]]
	- Finding out about the truth regarding the condition of his friend Harry meant that Holly now had to consider a dilemma between his loyalty to his friend and his moral duty.
	- For Anna, the truth does not matter. She is unceasingly loyal, and thus, the dilemma does not occur. Holly’s pursuit of the truth, in fact, is what drives her  away from him, as Anna views Holly as disloyal and a traitor.
- [[A strict deference to moral values may have intended negative side effects]]
	- This is perhaps both true for Holly and Anna?
		- Holly’s determination to find out about Harry and turn him in is what prevents him from getting together with Anna.
		- Anna’s obstinate desire for Harry obscures the morally ignoble things that Harry has actually done.
## Characters
- **Harry** 
	- Loves others, but he is willing to sacrifice others in order to save himself.
- **Holly** 
	- Loves others, and he is willing to sacrifice a lower ethics (betraying his friend) in order to achieve a higher ethics (catching a murderer). 
	- He is dangerous because he is so innocent, he cannot understand the motives of all of the evil people out there.
- **Anna** 
	- Loves others, and is unchangeable in her love. 
	- She does not change her love for Harry even after she learns that he was a murderer. 
	- She is not ethical. She is viewing things through the individual perspective, not the universal perspective. 
	- Her principle of non-betrayal is beyond the ethical. It is a categorical rule outside of the ethics.
- **Calloway**
	- Cannot understand anything outside the ethical either. He trumps unconditional commitments with his professional work. 
	- He is not callous; he is professional. 
	- He is sober and professional, where as Holly is cowboy and professional.
