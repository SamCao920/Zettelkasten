## Summary
A story about a man who travels through the wild with his dog, and eventually succumbs to the pressures imposed by nature.
## Significance
This is famous story by Jack London that explores many interesting themes.
## Themes
This short story explores themes such as death, individualism, and the idea of intellect vs. Instinct.
- Death: this story portrays the nature of death as peaceful and dreamy. This, alone with the heavy themes of the power nature in the story suggests nature as a dual force that is deadly, and at the same time, beautiful.
- Individualism: the main character refuses to travel with a companion, and ignores the advice given to him days before by the old man he met.
- Intellect vs. Instinct: the contrast with the way that the dog is able to survive in the environment, and the troubles endured by the man is apparent. While the man is able to reason and perform complex actions, he lacks the natural instincts the dog posses to be able to survive in the harsh cold.
