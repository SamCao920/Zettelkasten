## Summary
An article that outlines some critiques of meritocracy and some proposed solutions for it.
## Significance
This describes some recent work into the study of meritocracy.
## Explanation

Many have offered alternatives, mostly in the form of how we can improve meritocracy (make systems less meritocratic) or make it matter less that systems are meritocratic:^alternatives-to-meritocracy
1. Improve access to higher education.
	1. This means things like removing barriers to higher education, removing their tax-exempt statuses.
2. We could generally just distribute non-market goods more non-meritocracally.
3. We could make more goods non-market.
