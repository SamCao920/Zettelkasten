## Summary
Trump benefitted the economy by deregulation and tax cuts, which supports small businesses and thus the economy in general.
## Significance
An argument for why Trump’s economic policies may benefit the US economy, though I think that that there are some flaws with the arguments presented here.

## Notes
- Improved franchising in the US.
	- Trump made the joint employer standard into law, which ensured autonomy between the franchisors and the franchisees.
		- Talk about how businesses want economic certainty, which is what the Trump administration is going to deliver.
- Trump’s Tax cuts (Tax Cuts and Jobs Act).
	- Helped small business owners.
	- 20% deduction of qualified income for pass-through businesses.
		- Pass through businesses are only taxed once, through the owners own income, and not through the business it self.
- Weaken the FTC, which is strangling American business.
	- Replaced its head to someone who would ostensibly be more in favor of reduced government intervention.

## Opinions
I think it is interest how the article doesn’t really even mention Trump’s tariffs, which turned out to be a major part of his economic policy. Also, I think the points about how the Trump administration is going to bring economic certainty is questionable, as their tariff policy has definitely not be very consistent. Otherwise, I think the article brings up some good points.
