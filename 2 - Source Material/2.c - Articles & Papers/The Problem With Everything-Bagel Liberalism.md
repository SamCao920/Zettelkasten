## Summary
Argues for supply-side progressivism, a combination of Liberal politics and supply-side economics.
## Significance
This is a departure from traditional Liberal ideas.
## The Idea
Currently, Liberalism is dominated by left-wing economic progressives, but this theory of supply-side progressivism provides an interesting alternative approach to the Liberal worldview.
The idea is that currently, Liberals are attempting to do too much, when it clearly isn’t possible. By focusing on the core issue, rather than the plethora of issues that surround the core issue (though they might exist), thing are actually able to be done, rather than just endlessly being bogged down my regulation.
