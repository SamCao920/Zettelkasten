## Summary
Here, Marlow recalls his first voyage to the East aboard the _Judea_, a shabby ship that seems doomed from the start. The journey is full of hardship, from delays and fires to storms and a leaking, aging vessel, but Marlow remembers it as the most thrilling time of his life because he was young, hopeful, and full of energy. When the ship finally reaches its destination after great struggle, Marlow reflects that youth gives suffering a sense of adventure and meaning, even when experience later reveals how dangerous and absurd it really was.
## Significance
One of Conrad’s most famous short stories.
