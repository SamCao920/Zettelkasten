## Summary
A collecting of essays that explore topics that range from lying to cannibalism. This book invented the modern concept of an “essay,” or, from the original French, “an attempt.”
## Significance
There are many impactful messages scattered throughout this book, making it one of my most interesting reads.
## Themes & Ideas
- [[Morality is relative]]
- [[We have power over nothing except our will]]
- [[One has to confront their problems head on]]
