## Summary
Argued against Fukuyama’s thesis of the “end of history” while presenting a culture-centric view of geopolitics.
## Significance
This is an important work on foreign relations that continues to impact foreign policy of nations such as the US today.

## Three Rules for Foreign Policy in the Future
1. **Abstention rule:** Core states must abstain from intervening in conflicts of other civilizations.
2. **Joint Mediation rule:** Core states ought to negotiate to end fault line conflicts as much as possible.
3. **Commonalities rule:** All civilizations ought to search for common values, institutions, and practices and emphasize them in international dealings with each other.
