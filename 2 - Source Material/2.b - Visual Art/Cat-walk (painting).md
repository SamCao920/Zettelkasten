## Note
Its quite interesting how the industrial forms are able to be simplified down to nothing, just a collection of lines and shapes and colors.
- It shows something about the artificiality of human architecture.
Additionally, as you look closer at the painting, you notice that aspects of it don't really line up: some areas are missing shadows, or the perspective is slightly off, or lines may lead nowhere.
