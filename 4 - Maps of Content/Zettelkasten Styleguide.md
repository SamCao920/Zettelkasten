## Summary
A guide to organizing information in Obsidian.
## Significance
This note is needed in order to ensure that all notes are consistently stylized and notes and created correctly and under correct circumstances. This is to ensure that the system is operating with maximize efficiency, specifically, that the marginal benefit of every additional note is maximized.
## Procedure
### A Typology of Notes
There are a few types of Obsidian notes structured in this system:
- **[[atomic]]**: notes that represent one ideas, claim, or thought. Interlinked with other notes in order to 
	- Support a claim or provide a piece of evidence in a [[writing]] note,
	- Create new ideas at their intersection, which can be transformed into a [[writing]] note,
	- [anything else?]
- **[[writing]]** : notes that represent an original piece of writing my me.
- **[[media]]** : notes that represent an outside piece of information, which can be a book, an article, a art piece, a film, etc.
	- These notes are often the origin of large amounts of interconnected [[atomic]] notes, since a singular piece of media tend to have cohesive themes that are manifested through the atomic notes that it produces.
### When Should I Create a Note?
- A note is created when the idea that it expresses falls under one of the stated categories above.
- Alternatively, if the item is
	- A definition, create a definition via the the create definition command.
	- A relation of ideas/connection of various ideas best expressed visually, create a canvas.
- When it comes to citing a note, and there were multiple layers of sources or multiple sources linked together that support the note, 
	1. cite the most reliable source in the “chain” of sources,
	2. cite the most “upstream” source possible.

**This system is designed such that it works under only a modicum of rules.**
